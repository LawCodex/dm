# Money Laundering (Prevention) (Amendment) Act 2022

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 3 OF 2022
I assent

LS

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President
24th August, 2022
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE AMENDMENT OF
THE MONEY LAUNDERING (PREVENTION) ACT,
CHAP. 73:03.
(Gazetted 25th August, 2022.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows –

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
(PREVENTION) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 73:03

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Money Laundering
(Prevention) Act.

Amendment of
section 2.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
definition in proper alphabetical order:
“Securities Regulatory Commission” means the Eastern
Caribbean Securities Regulatory Commission
established by Article 3 of the Agreement
establishing the Eastern Caribbean Securities
Regulatory Commission made on the 24th
November 2000;”.

Amendment of
section 3.

### 4.

Section 3 of the Act is amended by –
(a) deleting subsection (3) and substituting the following:
“ (3) A person who is guilty of an offence under
subsection (1) or (2) is liable –
(a)

(b)

on summary conviction, to
(i)

a fine not exceeding five
hundred thousand dollars, and
to imprisonment for a term not
exceeding seven years, if he is
a natural person;

(ii)

a fine not exceeding five million
dollars, if it is a body corporate;

on conviction on indictment, to
(i)

a fine not exceeding five million
dollars, and to imprisonment for
a term not exceeding fifteen
years, if he is a natural person;

(ii)

a fine not exceeding fifteen
million dollars, if it is a body
corporate;

(b) inserting immediately after subsection (3), the following
new subsection (4):
“(4) In this section “transaction” includes the
receiving or making of a gift.”.

### 5.

Section 7 of the Act is amended –

Amendment
of section 7.

(a) in subsection (1) by deleting the word “The” and
substituting the words “Subject to subsection (3),
the”;
(b) by inserting after subsection (2), the following new
subsection (3):
“(3) The Securities Regulatory Commission is
established as the Money Laundering Supervisory
Authority of securities exchange.”.

### 6.

Section 15 of the Act is amended in subsection (1) by
deleting the words “a representative of the Banking Supervisory
Authority” appearing immediately after paragraph (g) and
substituting the following paragraphs (h), (i) and (j):
“(h) a representative of the Banking
Supervisory Authority;
(i)

the Director of Public Prosecutions;

(j)

a respresentative of the Securities
Regulatory Commission.”.

Amendment of
section 15.

Amendment of
section 28.

### 7.

Section 28 of the Act is amended in subsection (2) by
deleting the words “liable on conviction” and substituting the
words “liable on summary conviction”.

Amendment of
the Schedule.

### 8.

The Schedule of the Act is amended in Part I by –
(a) deleting the full-stop appearing at the end of the
schedule and substituting a semi-colon;
(b) inserting after item 16 the following new item 17:
“17. Virtual asset business within the meaning of
the Virtual Asset Business Act.”.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 22nd day of August, 2022.
