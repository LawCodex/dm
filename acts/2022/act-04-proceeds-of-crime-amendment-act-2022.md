# Proceeds of Crime (Amendment) Act 2022

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 4 OF 2022.
I assent

LS

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President
24th August, 2022
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE AMENDMENT OF
THE PROCEEDS OF CRIME ACT, CHAP. 12:29.
(Gazetted 25th August, 2022).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows –

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 12:29.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Proceeds of Crime Act.

Amendment of
section 2.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
definitions in appropriate alphabetical order:
““bank” means any person whose regular business is
the carrying on banking business; “banking
business” has the meaning assigned in the
Banking Act, 2015; “Banking Supervisory
Authority” means the Money Laundering
Supervisory Authority of Banks established
under section 7(2);
“Central Bank” means the Eastern Caribbean Central
Bank established by the Agreement establishing
the Eastern Caribbean Central Bank done at
Port-of-Spain, Trinidad on 5th July 1983;
“proliferation” means the development or production,
or the facilitation of the development or
production, of nuclear, radiological, biological
or chemical weapons or systems for their
delivery;
“Securities Regulatory Commission” means the
Eastern Caribbean Securities Regulatory
Commission established by Article 3 of the
Agreement establishing the Eastern Caribbean
Securities Regulatory Commission made on
the 24th November 2000;
“Supervisory Authority” means –
(a)

in relation to banks, the Central Bank;

(b)

in relation to securities exchange, the
Securities Regulatory Commission;

(c)

in relation to financial institutions other
than those mentioned in paragraph (a)
or (b), the Unit;”.

### 4.

Section 41 of the Act is amended by inserting after
subsection (5) the following new subsection (5A):

Amendment of
section 41.

“(5A) Where the material, to which an application
under this section relates, consists of information
contained in or accessible by means of a computer –
(a)

a production order under subsection
(5)(a) shall have effect as an order to
produce the material in a form in
which it can be taken away and in
which it is visible and legible; and

(b)

a production order under subsection
(5)(b) shall have effect as an order to
make the material available in a form
in which it is visible and legible.”.

### 5.

Section 47 of the Act is amended in subsection (7) by
deleting the words “section 60B and 61” and substituting the
words “section 61”.

### 6.

Section 52 of the Act is amended by deleting the words
“section 60B and 61” and substituting the words “section 61”.

### 7.

Section 59BB of the Act is amended as follows:
(a) in subsection (1) by deleting the full-stop appearing at
the end of that subsection and substituting a comma
and inserting the words “ and he may apply to the
Registrar of Titles under section 114 of the Title by
Registration Act for a caveat.”;

Amendment of
section 47.

Amendment of
section 52.

Amendment of
section 59BB.

(b) by deleting subsection (2) and substituting the following:
“(2) The Registrar of Titles may, on an application
made under subsection (1), order the entry of a caveat.”.
Amendment of
section 60.

### 8.

Section 60 of the Act is amended –
(a) in subsection (2) by inserting after the words “consult
with”, the words “other Supervisory Authorities and”;
(b) in subsection (3) in paragraph (a) by deleting the
words “the Unit” and substituting the words “a
Supervisory Authority”;
(c) in subsection (8) by deleting the word “Unit” and
substituting the words “Supervisory Authority”;
(d) by deleting subsection (9) and substituting the following:
“(9) An administrative penalty collected by a
Supervisory Authority by virtue of subsection (8) shall
–
(a)

in the case of the Unit, be paid into the
Asset Forfeiture Fund;

(b)

in the case of the Central Bank, be
retained for use by the Central Bank
for the purposes of its functions;

(c)

in the case of the Securities Regulatory
Commission, be retained for use by
the Commission for the purposes of its
functions.”.

### 9.

Section 60A of the Proceeds of Crime Act is amended in
subsection (2) –
(a) in the chapeau by deleting the words “money laundering
and terrorist financing activities” and substituting the
words “money laundering, terrorist financing activities
and financing of proliferation”;
(b) in paragraph (c) by deleting the words “money
laundering and terrorist financing activities” and
substituting the words “money laundering, terrorist
financing activities and financing of proliferation”;
(c) in paragraph (d) by –
(i) deleting the words “money laundering and terrorist
financing activities” and substituting the words
“money laundering, terrorist financing activities
and financing of proliferation”,
(ii) deleting the full-stop which appears at the end of
that paragraph and substituting a semi-colon;
(d) by inserting the following new paragraphs (e) and (f):
“(e) advise the Minister on the development
of national policies to combat money
laundering, terrorist financing and the
financing of proliferation;
(f)

coordinate actions to assess the
national money laundering, terrorist
financing and financing of proliferation
risks.”.

Amendment of
section 60A.

Repeal of section 60B.

### 10.

The Act is amended by repealing section 60B.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 22nd day of August, 2022.
DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly
