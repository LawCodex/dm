# 2008/2009 Appropriation Act 2008

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 5 OF 2008

I assent

LS
NICHOLAS J. O. LIVERPOOL
President
8th August, 2008.
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE THIRTIETH DAY OF
JUNE 2009.
(Gazetted 14th August, 2008).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the 2008/2009 APPROPRIATION ACT 2008.

Short title.

Appropriation.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of three
hundred and ninety one million, seven hundred and fifty five
thousand, five hundred and seventeen dollars ($391,755,517) is
hereby authorised for meeting the expenditure for services for the
year ending 30th June, 2009 under the Heads of Expenditure
specified in the Schedule hereto, and the sum specified in the said
Schedule opposite to the Head is hereby appropriated out of the
sum of $391,755,517 for the purposes specified in relation to the
Head in the Estimates for the said year.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 5

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and
required from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for
Finance, to pay out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the
several services specified in the said Schedule, the several monies
hereby appropriated in such manner as the said warrant shall
direct without further order or formality.

### 4.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the
1st day of July, 2008.
