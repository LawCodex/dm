LS

N. J. O. LIVERPOOL
President

10th December, 2008.
AN ACT TO AMEND THE INDUSTRIAL DESIGNS
ACT, NO. 2 OF 1998.

(Gazetted 18th December, 2008.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –

INDUSTRIAL DESIGNS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008.

Short title.

INDUSTRIAL DESIGNS (AMENDMENT)

ACT 15

Interpretaion.
Act No. 2 of 1998.

### 2.

In this Act the Industrial Designs Act 1998 is referred to
as “the Act”.

Amendment of section 2
of the Act.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended in the definition of
Registrar by deleting the words which follow the word “Property”.

Amendment of section 14
of the Act.

### 4.

Section 14 of the Act is amended by renumbering
subsections (3), (4) and (5) as subsections (2), (3) and (4).

Amendment of section of
16 of the Act.

### 5.

Section 16 of the Act is amended in subsection (1) by
deleting the word “application” and substituting the word
“applicant”.

Amendment of section 22
of the Act.

### 6.

Section 22 of the Act is amended by deleting the words
“who has been enrolled to practice in the Courts of Dominica” and
substituting the words “who is resident and practicing in Dominica”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 14th day of October, 2008.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
