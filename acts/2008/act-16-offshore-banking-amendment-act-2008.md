### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
OFFSHORE BANKING (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Act No. 8 of 1996.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Offshore Banking Act.

Amendment of section 5.

### 3.

Section 5 of the Act is amended –
(a) in subsection (1) by deleting the words “acting on the
recommendation of the Central Bank”;
(b) in subsection (3) by deleting the words “In considering
an application for a licence the Minister shall consult
the Central Bank in conducting such investigation as
may be necessary to ascertain” and substituting the
words “In granting a licence under this Act the
Minister shall have regard to”;
(c) by deleting the words “Provided that on such
consultation, the Minister shall furnish the Central
Bank with all the information obtained from the
applicant pursuant to section 4 subsection (3)”.
