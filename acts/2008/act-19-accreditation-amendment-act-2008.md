COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA

President

23rd December, 2008.
AN ACT TO AMEND THE ACCREDITATION ACT,
NO. 13 OF 2006.
(Gazetted 8th January, 2009)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
ACCREDITATION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2008.

Short title.

ACCREDITATION
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 19

Interpretation.
Act No. 13 of 2006

### 2.

In this Act the Accreditation Act 2006 is referred to as
“the Act”.

Amendment of section 2
of the Act.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
definition in alphabetical order –
“Medical Board” means the medical board
established by section 3 of the Medical Act;”.

Insertion of new section
2A.

### 4.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
after section 2 –
“Non-application

Amendment of section 6
of the Act.

Amendment of section 19
of the Act.

2A. This Act does not apply to medical
schools.”.

### 5.

Section 6 of the Act is amended by deleting the word
“seven (7)” and substituting the word “five (5)”.

### 6.

Section 19 of the Act is amended in subsection (7) by
deleting the words “and in the case of medical schools, the
Dominica Medical Board”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 11th day of December, 2008.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
