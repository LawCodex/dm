Dominica as follows:1. This Act may be cited as the TOURISM (REGULATION AND
STANDARDS)(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008.

Short title.

TOURISM (REGULATION
AND STANDARDS) (AMENDMENT)

ACT 11

Interpretation. Act No.
19 of 2005.

### 2.

In this Act the Tourism (Regulations and Standards) Act
2005 is referred to as “the Act”.

Amendment to section 2
of the Principal Act. Act
No. 19 of 2005.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by deleting the definitions
of “Corporation” and “General Manager” and inserting the
following new definitions in their appropriate alphabetical order:

Act No. 8 of 2007.

“Authority” means the Discover Dominica Authority established
by Section 3 of the Discover Dominica Authority Act,
2007;
“Chief Executive Officer” means the Chief Executive Officer”
of the Discover Dominica Authority.

Amendment to Principal
Act.

### 4.

The Act is amended by deleting
(a) the words “Corporation” and “National Development
Corporation” wherever they appear and substituting
the word “Authority”; and
(b) the words “General Manager” wherever they occur
and substituting the words “Chief Executive Officer”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this14th day of October, 2008.
