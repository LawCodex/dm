file returns.

77A. A person who, for two or more

both.

Collection

issue
invoices.

77C. A registered person who within 60 days

section 29(5), fails to issue invoices or sales
receipts on the supply as required under section
29 commits an offence and on summary
conviction is liable to a fine of $10,000 or
imprisonment for six months, or to both.”.
