AN ACT TO AMEND THE COURTS OF JUSTICE
FEES ACT, CHAPTER 4:31.
(Gazetted 4th May, 2021.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the COURTS OF JUSTICE FEES
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021.

COURTS OF JUSTICE FEES

ACT 2

(AMENDMENT)
Interpretation.
Chap. 4:31.

### 2.

In this Act the Courts of Justice Fees Act is referred
to as “the Act”.

Repeal and replacement
of section 5.

### 3.

follows -

Section 5 of the Act is repealed and replaced as

“Payment of
court filing fees.

### 5.

(1) The payment of court filing fees and
percentages, including payments for transcripts of
court proceedings payable, may be paid and received
by (a)

credit card;

(b)

debit card;

(c)

cash; and

(d)

cheque.

(2) Where the payment methods under subsection (1) are not possible, the payment may be
made in postage stamps denoting the amount payable.”.
Repeal and replacement
of section 6.

4\.
follows:

Section 6 of the Act is repealed and replaced as

“Stamps to be
impressed or
adhesive.

### 6.

The postage stamps referred to under section 5(2) shall be impressed or adhesive and shall only
be distributed by the Financial Secretary and such
other public officers as the President may, from time
to time, direct.”.

COURTS OF JUSTICE FEES

ACT 2

(AMENDMENT)
5\.

The Schedule to the Act is repealed.

Repeal of Schedule.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 27th day of April, 2021.
