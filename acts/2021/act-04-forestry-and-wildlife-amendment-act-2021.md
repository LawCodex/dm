ACT TO AMEND THE FORESTRY AND WILDLIFE
ACT CHAP. 60:02.
(Gazetted 4th May, 2021.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the FORESTRY AND WILDLIFE
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Ch. 60:02.

### 2.

In this Act the Forestry and Wildlife Act is referred to as
“the Act”.

Amendment of
section 2 of the Act.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
new definition immediately after the definition of “director”:
““export” means (a) to take or cause to be taken outside the territorial
waters of Dominica;
(b) to load or cause to be loaded on a vessel or an
aircraft;”.

Repeal and replacement
of section 32 of the Act.

### 4.

Section 32 of the Act is repealed and replaced as
follows:
“Exporting of
wildlife.

Insertion of new
section 60A.

32\. No person shall export any live or dead
wildlife or parts thereof cooked, frozen or processed,
except with a permit issued by the Director in Form
5, and in accordance with conditions specified in
the permit.”.

### 5.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
60A immediately after section 60:
“Penalties for
contravening
sections 32, 33
and the Ninth
Schedule.

60A. A person who contravenes –
(a) section 32 is liable on summary conviction to
a fine of five thousand dollars;
(b) section 33 is liable on summary conviction to
a fine of two thousand five hundred dollars;
(c) regulation 2(c) of the Ninth Schedule is liable
on summary conviction to a fine of seven
thousand five hundred dollars.”.

### 6.

Section 63 of the Act is repealed and replaced by the
following:

Repeal and replacement
of section 63.

“Penalty for
contravening
section 62.

63\. A person who contravenes –
(a) section 62 (a) is liable on summary conviction
to a fine of ten thousand dollars;
(b) section 62 (b) is liable on summary conviction
to a fine of twenty-five thousand dollars;
(c) section 62 (c) or (d) is liable on summary
conviction to a fine of five thousand dollars,
and to imprisonment for three years.”.
