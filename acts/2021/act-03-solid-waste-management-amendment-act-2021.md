Amendment of section 2.

Amendment of Schedule

### 3.

SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 3

3\. Section 2 of the Act is amended in the definition of the
word “Minister” by deleting the word “Health” and substituting
the words “Solid Waste Management”.

### 4.

Schedule 3 to the Act is amended in (a) section 1 of the ANNEX by deleting subsection (1)
and substituting the following “(1) The Corporation consists of the following
five members:
(a) the General Manager of the Dominica
Port Authority;
(b) a senior representative in the Ministry
responsible for Solid Waste Management;
(c) an Attorney-at-Law; and
(d) two other persons.”;
(b) section 2(1) of the ANNEX by deleting the word
“three” and substituting the word “two”;
(c) section 3 of the ANNEX by -

SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 3

(i) deleting the word “members” and substituting the
word “member”; and
(ii) deleting the words “and (c)”.
