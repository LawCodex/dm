### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
AUTHORITY ACT, 2021.

Short title and
commencement.

(2) This Act shall come into force on such date as the
Minister may by Order published in the Gazette appoint.
Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, unless the context requires otherwise –
“Authority” means the Dominica Hospitals Authority established
under section 4;
Board” means the Board of Directors of the Authority appointed
under section 6;
“Chief Executive Officer” means the person appointed under
section 24(1);
“committee” means a committee established under section 20(1);
“designated health facility” means a hospital or other health
facility designated under section 5(1);
“direction” means a direction in writing;
“Director of Clinical Services” means the person appointed under
section 25(1);
“external auditor” means the external auditor appointed under
section 44(1);
“fees” includes any charges to be paid by or in respect of patients
accessing medical or other services from the Authority,
whether in-patients or out-patients, including charges for
treatment, nursing, accommodation, attendance, food,
dressings, drugs, medicines or other supplies or services of
whatever kind rendered to a patient by the Authority, but
does not include fees payable to an independent contractor;
“internal auditor” means the internal auditor appointed under
section 28(1);

“medical service” means any service provided by a medical
practitioner, a dental practitioner or other health practitioner;
“medical staff” includes a person who provides diagnosis or
treatment as –
(a) a medical practitioner, a dental practitioner or other
health practitioner;
(b) a nurse; or
(c) a pharmacist;
“member” means a member of the Board;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for health;
“officer”, in relation to the Authority, includes a Director and a
Manager appointed under section 26;
“premises” include messages, buildings, lands, easements and
hereditaments of any tenure;
“prescribed” means prescribed by regulations;
“regulations” means regulations made under section 54;
“rules” means rules made by the Board under section 53;
“Secretary” means the Secretary appointed under section 19;
“staff” means an officer or employee of the Authority.

### 3.

This Act binds the State.

Act binds the State.

## PART I

THE DOMINICA HOSPITAL AUTHORITY
Establishment of the
Dominica Hospitals
Authority.

### 4.

There is established a body corporate to be known as “the
Dominica Hospitals Authority”.

Functions and powers of
the Authority.

### 5.

(1) The Authority shall be responsible for the overall
management and administration of such hospitals and other health
facilities as the Minister may, by Order, designate.
(2) The functions of the Authority are –
(a) to administer designated health facilities generally
to promote the welfare of patients of designated
health facilities;
(b) to make available at designated health facilities
best practices in methods of treatment of the sick
and differently-abled;
(c) to control, regulate and administer all matters
related to the management of designated health
facilities;
(d) to ensure the application of efficient and
appropriate techniques, systems, standards and
models for the delivery of health care in designated
health facilities, towards the goal of achieving
international accreditation;
(e) to operate, construct, equip, furnish, maintain,
manage, secure and repair all its property for use
by the general public;
(f) to adopt telehealth solutions as a major
transformation technology for improving access

to diagnostic and other services within designated
health facilities and primary care networks;
(g) to facilitate the use of its designated health facilities
for service, teaching and research and to establish
and develop relationships with national, regional
and international bodies engaged in similar or
ancillary pursuits; and
(h) to collaborate with educational institutions, such
as the University of the West Indies and any other
recognised training institutions, in the education
and training of persons, and in research in medicine,
nursing, dentistry, pharmacy and biomedical and
health science fields, as well as any related
ancillary and supportive fields.
(3) In carrying out its functions, the Authority may –
(a) acquire by purchase, gift, lease or otherwise, hold,
deal with and dispose of movable property or any
interest in personal property;
(b) accept, deposit or act as trustees or managers of
any legacy, endowment, bequest or gift for
purposes of patient care, treatment or research;
(c) contract with any person for the supply to, or by,
the Authority of any equipment, supplies,
appliances or other goods or services;
(d) open, maintain and close accounts with banks
outside Dominica with the approval of the Minister
responsible for finance;
(e) publish, in such manner as it considers appropriate,
such information relating to its functions as it
thinks fit;

(f) write off debts;
(g) acquire, purchase, take, hold and enjoy any property
that may be vested in the Authority;
(h) engage in fund raising activities for the benefit of
the Authority;
(i) fix, charge and collect fees and may, with the
approval of the Minister, reduce, waive or refund
any fees, having regard to the principle that no
person should be prevented, through lack of
means from obtaining adequate medical treatment;
(j) utilise telemedicine to facilitate the remote
diagnosis and treatment of patients by means of
telecommunications technology;
(k) dispose of immovable property of every
description with the approval by the Minister;
(l) enter into contracts, including contracts for the
provision of services to the Authority by
independent contractors;
(m) participate in international recognition programmes
that promote quality care and patient safety; and
(n) do all such things as may be necessary or conducive
to the proper discharge of its functions.
Board of Directors.

### 6.

(1) The Authority shall be managed by a Board of
Directors which shall comprise –

(a) nine voting members appointed by the Minister as
follows:
(i) six persons possessing special qualifications,
substantial experience or proven capacity in
one or more of the fields of finance, hospital
administration, management, law, medicine,
nursing, economics, science, engineering or
industrial relations;
(ii) a representative of the Dominica Medical
Association who is not employed by the
Authority;
(iii) a person with knowledge and experience in
Information Communications Technology;
(iv) a representative of civil society; and
(b) the following ex officio non-voting members:
(i) the Chief Executive Officer;
(ii) the Chief Medical Officer; and
(iii) the Director of Clinical Services.
(2) The Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Medical
Officer and the Director of Clinical Services shall absent
themselves during discussions on matters of compensation, audits
and performance appraisal and on any occasion that a conflict of
interest may arise pursuant to section 22.
(3) The Minister shall designate a member appointed
under subsection (1)(a)(i) or (iii) to serve as –
(a) Chairperson; and

(b) Deputy Chairperson.
(4) The Minister shall cause to be published in the Gazette
the names of all members as first appointed, including the
Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson and every change in the
membership of the Board.
Functions and duties of
the Board.

### 7.

(1) The functions of the Board are –
(a) to co-ordinate the administration and operation of
the Authority and designated health facilities in an
efficient manner, in order to promote the welfare
of patients;
(b) to make available at designated health facilities
best practices in methods of treatment of the sick
and differently abled;
(c) to equip, furnish, maintain, manage, control and
operate designated health facilities;
(d) to have general supervision of the buildings,
premises and grounds of designated facilities;
(e) to fix fees with the approval of the Minister;
(f) to make recommendations to the Minister on the
development of the Authority and the services
provided at designated health facilities;
(g) to establish and determine the functions and
duties of committees, subject to section 20;
(h) to employ, discipline and terminate medical staff,
officers and employees, subject to this Act;

(i) to determine the duties of its officers, subject to

## Part I;

(j) to give effect to any directions given by the
Minister under section 18;
(k) to carry out any other functions specified in this
Act.
(l) to fix qualifications and terms and conditions of
service relating to its officers and other employees;
and
(m) to consult with the Minister on matters of national
health policy and capital development programmes
for hospitals and other health facilities.
(2) The Board shall consider and advise on all matters
which may from time to time be referred to it by the Minister and
shall furnish the Minister such information as he or she may
require relating to the administration of this Act.
(3) In the performance of its functions, the Board shall
operate in accordance with the highest standards of transparency,
financial management and accountability.

### 8.

(1) Subject to this Act, the Board may delegate to any
appropriately qualified member or committee of the Board or any
appropriately qualified member of staff, the power and authority
to carry out on its behalf such functions as the Board may
determine.
(2) A delegation of power or authority under subsection
(1) does not prevent or affect the exercise of such power or
authority by the Board where the Board considers it necessary.

Delegation of functions.

(3) Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Board shall not
delegate its power to (a) advise the Minister under section 7(2);
(b) make Rules under this Act;
(c) adopt the annual budget of the Authority; or
(d) approve spending of funds available to the
Authority by way of bequest, donation or special
grant.
Duration of appointment.

### 9.

(1) Subject to subsection (2) and section 12, a member
shall hold office for a period of not more than three years from the
date of his or her appointment, and is eligible for re-appointment.
(2) A member shall not hold office for more than seven
years in aggregate.

Acting appointment.

### 10.

Where for any reason the Chairperson, Deputy
Chairperson or any other member is unable to carry out his or her
functions under this Act, the Minister may appoint a member to
act in the place of the Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson or other
member –
(a) until the Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson or
other member is again able to carry out such
functions; or
(b) until another person is appointed as a Chairperson,
Deputy Chairperson or member in accordance
with section 6.

### 11.

The Minister may, on the application of a member in
writing grant leave of absence to the member for a period not
exceeding three months.

Leave of absence.

### 12.

The Minister may, at any time, revoke the appointment of
a member if –

Revocation of
appointment.

(a) the member is incapable for any reason of
performing his or her functions as a member;
(b) the member has been absent from three
consecutive regular meetings of the Board, other
than by reason of section 11;
(c) the member fails to disclose his or her interest in
accordance with section 22; or
(d) the Minister, on reasonable grounds, thinks it
expedient to do so.

### 13.

(1) A member, other than the Chairperson, may at any
time resign his or her office by notice in writing addressed to the
Minister and transmitted through the Chairperson and the
resignation takes effect on the date the notice is received by the
Minister or, if a later date is specified in the resignation, on that
later date and the member ceases to be a member of the Board.

Resignation.

(2) The Chairperson may at any time resign his or her
office by notice in writing addressed to the Minister and the
resignation takes effect on the date the notice is received by the
Minister or, if a later date is specified in the resignation, on that
later date and the Chairperson ceases to be the Chairperson of the
Board.

### 14.

The members shall be paid such remuneration as may be
determined by the Minister.

Remuneration.

Seal of the Board.

### 15.

(1) The common seal of the Board shall be kept in the
custody of the Chairperson, the Deputy Chairperson or the
Secretary to the Board.
(2) The seal of the Board shall not be affixed to any
instrument except by the authority of a resolution of the Board and
attested by the signature of –
(a) the Secretary; and
(b) either –
(i) the Chairperson or in his or her absence, the
Deputy Chairperson; or
(ii) one other member authorised to act in that
behalf.

Signification of other
documents.

### 16.

All documents other than those required by law to be
made under seal and all decisions of the Board may be signified
under the hand of –
(a) the Secretary; or
(b) the Chairperson or, in the absence of the
Chairperson, the Deputy Chairperson, or any
other person authorised to act for that purpose.

Fixed address.

### 17.

(1) The Authority shall at all times have a fixed address.
(2) The address for service of a notice, order or other
document on the Board shall be published in the Gazette.

Policy directions,
requests for information
and reporting.

### 18.

(1) The Minister may give general directions as to policy
to be followed by the Board in the performance of its functions and
exercise of its powers, as appear to the Minister to be necessary
in the public interest.

(2) The Minister may at any time request the Board to
provide him or her with information concerning any matter
relating to the functions of the Board, the Authority or a designated
health facility or that the Minister considers to be in the public
interest and the Board shall provide the information requested
within fourteen days of such request or within such other period
as may be specified by the Minister.
(3) The Board shall ensure that a copy of any documentation
relating to the functions and powers of the Board is made available
to the Minister on his or her request in writing.

### 19.

(1) The Board shall appoint an officer of the Authority to
serve as Secretary to the Board, on such terms and conditions as
the Board may determine.

Secretary to the Board.

(2) The Secretary to the Board shall perform such duties
as are normally assigned to and consistent with the post of a
Corporate Secretary, in particular, the Secretary shall –
(a) make preparations for and attend meetings of the
Board;
(b) prepare and keep minutes of meetings of the
Board; and
(c) assist the Board in all respects and in such manner
as the Board may from time to time require in the
discharge of its functions.
(3) The Secretary shall not be a member of the Board.

### 20.

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Board may establish
such standing or special committees as it thinks fit to assist it in the
discharge of its functions under this Act and may refer or assign
to a committee any matter for consideration, inquiry or management
by the Board.

Establishment of
committees.

(2) The Board shall establish a standing committee to be
known as –
(a) “the Clinical Committee” which shall assist the
Board with respect to matters relating to clinical
governance, including clinical standards, quality
management, risk management and patients’
complaints; and
(b) “the Finance and Audit Committee” which shall
assist the Board with respect to matters relating
to finance, budgeting, service planning, audit and
procurement.
(3) A committee may –
(a) include the Chief Executive Officer or his or her
representative; or
(b) consist wholly or partly of members as may be
necessary for the proper discharge of its functions.
(4) The Board may co-opt to a committee such specialised
personnel as it thinks fit.
(5) A person co-opted to a committee pursuant to
subsection (4) shall not have the right to vote.
(6) The Board shall determine the remuneration of a
member of a committee who is not a member of the Board.
(7) The Board may make rules to prescribe the functions
and procedure of a committee.
Meetings of the Board.

### 21.

(1) The Board shall meet at least once in each month and
at any other period as may be necessary or expedient for the
discharge of its functions.

(2) The Chairperson may at any time call a special
meeting of the Board –
(a) where a written request for that purpose is
addressed to him or her by any three members of
the Board;
(b) where a direction to that effect is addressed to
him or her by the Minister; or
(c) at his or her own discretion.
(3) Meetings of the Board shall be held on such day and
at such time and place as the Chairperson may determine.
(4) Due notice of the day, time and place of a meeting
under subsection (3) shall be given to each member in writing at
least seven days before the day on which the meeting is to be held.
(5) The Chairperson may convene a meeting of the
Board by video, teleconference or other electronic means.
(6) A member is deemed to be present at a meeting of the
Board where the member participates by teleconference or other
electronic means and all members participating in the meeting are
able to communicate with each other.
(7) At every meeting of the Board, the Chairperson or in
the absence of the Chairperson, the Deputy Chairperson shall
preside.
(8) The quorum of meetings of the Board is five voting
members, and if a quorum is present, the validity of any act,
decision or proceedings of the Board shall not be affected by
reason of a vacancy among its members or any defect in the
appointment of a member.

(9) A decision of the Board shall be adopted by a majority
of the vote of members present and voting, and in the event of an
equality of votes, the Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson presiding
at the meeting shall have a casting vote.
(10) Whenever, in the judgment of the Chairperson, there
arises a situation where any action contemplated by the Board
requiring the approval of the Board should not be postponed until
the next meeting of the Board and cannot await the calling of a
special meeting of the Board under subsection (2) –
(a) the Chairperson shall –
(i) request the members to vote without meeting;
(ii) present to each member by rapid means of
communication a motion embodying the
proposed action;
(b) each member shall as soon as possible, notify the
Chairperson of his or her decision or
recommendation on the proposed action;
(c) the Chairperson shall cause the results of the vote
to be recorded, and shall notify all members of the
results and of the status of the decision or
recommendation;
(d) the decision or recommendation in writing signed
by all the members entitled to receive notice of a
meeting of the Board, shall be valid and effectual
as if it had been passed by a meeting of the Board
duly convened and held and may consist of
several documents in the like form each signed by
one or more members;

(e) a decision made under paragraph (d) shall be
ratified by, and recorded in, the minutes of the
next meeting of the Board.
(11) Notwithstanding subsection (10)(c) and (d), if any
member so requests, the matter on which a decision is required
shall be referred for consideration by the Board at its next
meeting.
(12) The Board may co-opt any one or more persons to
attend a meeting of the Board for the purpose of assisting or
advising the Board, but no such co-opted person shall have any
right to vote.
(13) Minutes of each meeting and transactions of the
Board shall be recorded by the Secretary and shall be confirmed
by the Board at its next meeting and signed by the Chairperson or
Deputy Chairperson, as the case may be.
(14) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Board may
regulate its own procedure for meetings.

### 22.

(1) A member of the Board or a committee who is
directly or indirectly interested in a transaction, arrangement or
contract made or proposed to be made by the Board or committee
or any other matter whatsoever in which the Board or committee
is concerned –
(a) shall disclose the nature of his or her interest at the
first meeting of the Board or committee at which
he or she is present, after the relevant facts have
come to his or her knowledge; and
(b) shall not take part in any deliberation or decision
of the Board or committee with respect to the
transaction, arrangement, contract or other matter.

Disclosure of interest.

(2) A disclosure under subsection (1) shall be recorded in
the minutes of the meeting and after the disclosure, that member
shall not be present or take part in any discussion or decision on
the transaction, arrangement or contract.
(3) A member of the Board or a committee shall be
treated as having an indirect interest in any transaction, arrangement
or contract with the Board or committee or in which the Board or
committee is interested if the member is a director, shareholder,
trustee, agent or employee of the company or undertaking that is
a party of the transaction, arrangement or contract or proposed
transaction, arrangement or contract with the Board or committee
or where his or her immediate relative or cohabitant holds an
interest in that company or undertaking.
(4) For the purpose of this section, a general notice given
to the Board or a committee by a member to the effect that –
(a) he or she is a member of or is otherwise associated
with a company or undertaking and is to be as
interested in any transaction, arrangement or
contract which may after the date of the notice be
made with that company or undertaking; or
(b) he or she is in any way interested in a matter
which is being dealt with by the Board or
committee,
is deemed to be sufficient disclosure of interest in relation to any
transaction, arrangement, contract or matter referred to in
subsection (1).
(6) A member of the Board or a committee who fails to
comply with this section is liable to revocation of his or her
appointment from the Board or committee.
(7) In this section –

“cohabitant”, in relation to a member of the Board or a committee,
means a person who ordinarily lives with the member
in the same dwelling house;
“immediate relative”, in relation to a member of the Board or a
committee, means the spouse, parent, child, stepchild,
brother or sister of the member, or the parent, child,
stepchild, brother or sister of the spouse of the
member.

### 23.

Any act done or proceeding taken under this Act by the
Board is presumed to be valid despite any omission, defect or
irregularity which does not affect the merits of the act or
proceeding.

Validity of actions of the
Board.

## PART II

SENIOR MANAGEMENT, OTHER STAFF AND
ADMINISTRATION

### 24.

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Minister shall, on the
recommendation of the Board appoint a suitably qualified and
experienced person as Chief Executive Officer of the Authority,
who shall be a full-time officer of the Authority and shall not
engage in any other trade, profession, occupation or business
without the consent of the Board.
(2) The Chief Executive Officer shall be appointed to hold
office for a period not exceeding three years, and shall receive
such emoluments and be subject to such terms and conditions of
service as the Board with the approval of the Minister and shall
be eligible for reappointment.
(3) The Chief Executive Officer shall –
(a) be responsible to the Board for the implementation
of the policy and day-to-day management of the
Authority and of all operations at designated

Chief Executive Officer.

health facilities;
(b) be the administrative head of the Authority;
(c) participate in the policy formulation and strategic
planning activities of the Board;
(d) co-ordinate organisational emergency planning;
(e) serve as chairperson of the management team;
(f) attend all meetings of the Board;
(i) make recommendations to the Board regarding
the improvements of management systems and
human resource development at the Authority;
(k) be responsible for the internal organization and
administration of the Authority and do all things
necessary or convenient to be done for or in
connection with the performance of his or her
duties; and
(l) perform any other duties assigned to the Chief
Executive Officer under his or her contract of
employment.
(4) The Chief Executive Officer shall act, contract and
sign instruments and documents on behalf of the Authority in
accordance with policies determined and directions given by the
Board.
Director of Clinical
Services.

### 25.

(1) The Board shall appoint a Director of Clinical Services
for the Authority, who shall be a medical practitioner and a fulltime officer of the Authority who shall not engage in any other
trade, profession, occupation or business without the consent of
the Board.

(2) The Director of Clinical Services shall be responsible
to the Chief Executive Officer for the clinical organisation of the
Authority and for the supervision of the clinical care given to all
patients of designated health facilities in accordance with the
policies established by the Board.
(3) The Director of Clinical Services shall –
(a) ensure and monitor the quality of medical care at
designated health facilities;
(b) ensure the achievement of continuing quality
improvement in all aspects of medical care at
designated health facilities;
(c) advise the Chief Executive Officer on –
(i) the priorities in the budget of the Authority in
relation to clinical services; and
(ii) the employment and allocation of medical
staff to clinical departments of the Authority;
and
(d) perform any other duties as may be specified by
the Board and under his or her contract of
employment.

### 26.

(1) The Board shall appoint such Directors and Managers
as are necessary for the due performance of the functions of the
Authority.
(2) A Director or Manager appointed under subsection
(1) shall be a full-time officer of the Authority who shall not

Directors and Managers.

engage in any other trade, profession, occupation or business
without the consent of the Board.
Specialists and
consultants.

### 27.

The Board may employ, after consultation with the Chief
Executive Officer, specialists, consultants and other persons to
provide specific services or perform specific tasks that the Board
considers necessary for the due performance of its functions and
exercise of its powers under this Act, on such terms and
conditions as are agreed between the Board and the specialists,
consultants or other persons.

Other staff

### 28.

(1) The Board shall, subject to such terms and conditions,
including salaries, allowances, other remuneration and disciplinary
control, appoint an internal auditor and such other officers and
employees as the Board determines necessary from time to time
for the due performance of the functions of the Authority.
(2) A member of staff of the Authority appointed under
subsection (1) shall not engage in any other trade, profession
occupation or business without the consent of the Board.
(3) The Board may delegate in writing to the Chief
Executive Officer the power –
(a) to appoint, discipline and terminate employees or
classes of employees; and
(b) to consent on behalf of the Board to the employee
engaging in any trade, profession, occupation or
business.

Minister to assist with
staffing arrangements.

### 29.

The Minister shall ensure where necessary that
arrangements are made for the services of the staff presently
employed by the hospital to be made available to the Dominica
Hospital’s Authority from the commencement of this Act.

### 30.

(1) Where secondment of a public officer to the Authority
is approved by the Public Service Commission, subject to such
