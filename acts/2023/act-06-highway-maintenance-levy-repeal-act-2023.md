# Highway Maintenance Levy (Repeal) Act 2023

HIGHWAY MAINTENANCE

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT No. 6 OF 2023.
I assent

LS

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President

.

16th February, 2023
AN ACT TO REPEAL THE HIGHWAY MAINTENANCE LEVY ACT, CHAPTER 67:12.
(Gazetted 17th February, 2023).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the HIGHWAY MAINTENANCE
LEVY (REPEAL) ACT, 2023.

Short title and
commencement.

HIGHWAY MAINTENANCE
LEVY (REPEAL)

ACT 6

(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st
day of August, 2022.
Interpretation.
Chapter. 67:12.

Repeal of the Act.

Saving.

Chap. 46:50.

### 2.

In this Act the Highway Maintenance Levy Act is referred
to as “the Act”.

### 3.

The Act is repealed.

### 4.

Notwithstanding section 3, where immediately before the
commencement of this Act the highway maintenance levy is still
due and payable by any person, all the provisions of the repealed
Act, and the repealed penal provision of section 111A of the
Vehicle and Road Traffic Act shall be deemed to remain in full
force and effect; and all proceedings under for the recovery of any
fees or penalty in respect of any offence committed may be
instituted or contained accordingly as if the enactment had not
been repealed.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 8th day of February, 2023.
DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly
