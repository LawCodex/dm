AN ACT TO AMEND THE STAMP ACT, CHAP 68:01.

(Gazetted 22nd December , 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows1. (1)This Act may be cited as the –
STAMP (AMENDMENT) ACT 2023.
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of
January, 2024.

commencement.

ACT 17

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” refers to the Stamp Act.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
34A immediately after section 34:
“Comptroller
of
Inland
Revenue:
Administration and
Enforcement.
Chap. 67:08.

Amendment of the
Schedule to the Act.

STAMP
(AMENDMENT)

34A.(1) Notwithstanding sections 32 and 34, the
Comptroller of Inland Revenue may, on behalf of the
Financial Secretary, administer and enforce this Act
and collect stamp duties imposed by this Act.
(2) The provisions of the Value Added Tax
Act apply with such modifications as the circumstances require, as if the stamp duty were value added
tax and in particular, sections 7, 8, 32, 34, through 51, 55,
through 72, 74, 75, 78, 79, 84 through 88, 90, 92, through
95, 100, 105 and 106, apply to duty with suitable
modification.”.

### 4.

The Schedule to the Act is amended by inserting immediately before the words “RELEASE OR RENUNCIATION of
any property” the following:
“RECEIPT given for or upon outbound money
transfers through money services regulated under the
Money Services Business Act, Chapter 78:82 A
stamp duty of 2% on the monetary value of the
transfer.

STAMP
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 17

This stamp duty shall be payable to the Comptroller
of Inland Revenue no later than the 20th day of the
following month.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 14th day December, 2023.
