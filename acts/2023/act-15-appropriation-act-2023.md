# Appropriation Act 2023

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO.15 OF 2023.

I assent

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President

4th August, 2023

AN ACT PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE THIRTIETH DAY OF
JUNE, 2024.
(Gazetted 8th August, 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the2023/2024 APPROPRIATION ACT, 2023.

Short title.

Appropriation.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

2023

APPROPRIATION

ACT 15

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of one
billion, two hundred thirty-four million, five hundred twenty-four
thousand, and thirty-six dollars($1,234,524,036)is hereby authorized for meeting the expenditure for services for the year ending
June 30th, 2024 under the Head of Expenditure specified hereto,and
the sum specified in the said Schedule opposite to the Head is
hereby appropriated out of the said sum for the purposes specified
in relation to the Head of the Estimates for the said year.

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance, to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the Schedule, the several monies appropriated in the
manner as the said warrant shall direct witout further order or
formality.

### 4.

This Act, is deemed to have come into force on the 1st day
of July, 2023.
