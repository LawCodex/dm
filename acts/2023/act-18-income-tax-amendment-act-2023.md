# Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2023

INCOME TAX
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 18

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 18 OF 2023.
I assent

SYLVANIE BURTON
President

20th December, 2023
AN ACT TO AMEND THE INCOME TAX ACT,
(CHAP. 67:01)

(Gazetted 22nd December, 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –

Short title.

INCOME TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Income Tax Act.

Interpretation Chap.
67:01.

Amendment of section
66\.

INCOME TAX
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 18

### 3.

Section 66 of the Act is amended in subsection (5)(a) in paragraph (b), by deleting the comma appearing at the end of that paragraph and substituting
a semi-colon;
(b) by inserting the following new paragraph (c) after
paragraph (b):
“(c) a resident individual whose income
accrues entirely from employment
with the State.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 14th day of December, 2023.

DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
