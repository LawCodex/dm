AN ACT TO AMEND THE STAMP ACT, CHAPTER
68:01.
(Gazetted 17th February, 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the STAMP (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023.

Short title and
commencement.

STAMP
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 5

(2) This Act comes into force on the 1st day of
November, 2022.
Interpretation.
Chapter. 68:01.
Amendment of Schedule
to the Act.

### 2.

In this Act the Stamp Act is referred to as “the Act”.

### 3.

The Schedule to the Act is amended by deleting the
paragraph appearing immediately after the heading –
(a)

“CONVEYANCE OR TRANSFER ON SALE
OF ANY PROPERTY (except stock and debentures) up to and including the word “transferee” and
substituting the following –
“A stamp duty of 4.5% on the value of the real
property transferred shall be paid of which 2.5%
shall be paid by the transferor and 2% by the
transferee;
A stamp duty of 6.5% on the value of the
personal property transferred shall be paid of
which 2.5% shall be paid by the transferor and
4% by the transferee.”;

(b) “CONVEYANCE OR TRANSFER operating as a
voluntary disposition inter vivos, up to and including
the words “and the transferee shall pay no stamp
duty” and substituting the following –
“A stamp duty of 4.5% of the value of the
property so conveyed of which 2.5% shall be
paid by the transferor and 2% by the transferee
except that where the transfer is between parent
and child a stamp duty of 2% of the value of the
property so conveyed shall be paid by the

transferor and the transferee shall pay no stamp
duty.”.
