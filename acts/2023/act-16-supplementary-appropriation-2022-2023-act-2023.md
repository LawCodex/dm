# Supplementary Appropriation (2022/2023) Act 2023

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO.16 OF 2023.
I assent

SYLVANIE BURTON
President
20th December, 2023
AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM
THE CONSOLIDATED FUND OF DOMINICA
IN EXCESS OF THE SUM PROVIDED BY LAW
FOR THE SERVICES OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA DURING THE YEAR
ENDING THE THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE 2023.
(Gazette 22nd December , 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(2022/2023) ACT, 2023.

Short title.

Legislation
Unforeseen.

### 2.

(1)The sum of three hundred and forty-nine million, seven
hundred and two thousand, sixty hundred dollars and forty-nine
cents ($349,702,600.49) as described in the Schedule was withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during the
financial year 2022/2023 ending on the 30th day of June, 2023.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen Government expenditure in anticipation of the
granting of an appropriation by the House of Assembly for the
issue of a specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of July, 2022 and appropriated to the
service set out in the Schedule.
