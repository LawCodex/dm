AN ACT TO AMEND THE PENSIONS ACT, CHAP 23:80.
(Gazetted 7th February, 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
PENSIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023.
(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st
day of January, 2023.

### 2.

In this Act the Pensions Act is referred to as “the Act”.

Short title and
commencement.

Interpretation.
Chap. 23:80.
Amendment of
section 2.

PENSIONS
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 3

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
new subsection (3) after subsection (2):
“(3)For the purposes of this Act “salary” includes” acting allowance –
(a)

in the case of an officer who held an
acting appointment in the same office
for a period of three years immediately preceding the date of retirement
or resignation of the officer;

(b)

in the case of an officer who held an
acting appointment and a permanent
appointment in the same office for a
period of three years immediately preceding the date of retirement or resignation of the officer.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 8th day of February , 2023.

DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
