AN ACT TO AMEND THE VALUE ADDED TAX ACT,

## CHAPTER 67:08.

(Gazetted 17th February, 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the VALUE ADDED TAX
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023.
(2) Except for sections 5 and 6, this Act is deemed to
have come into force on the 1st day of August, 2022.

Short title and
commencement.

ACT 2

(3) Sections 5 and 6 are deemed to have come into force
on the 1st day of September, 2022.
Interpretation.
Chapter. 67:08.

### 2.

In this Act the Value Added Tax Act is referred to as “the
Act”.

Amendment of
section 9.

### 3.

Section 9 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
new subsection (4) immediately after subsection (3) –
“(4) Subsection (1)(e) does not apply to a person referred
to in section 54(1)(a) and (b) of this Act, where –
(a) the supply of goods takes place within three years
after the goods are acquired; and
(b) there is evidence of the person’s permanent or
intended departure from Dominica within three
months after the supply.”.

Amendment of
Schedule I.

### 4.

Schedule I to the Act is amended in paragraph 3 under the
title “SUPPLY OF GOODS” by –
(a) deleting the figure “100” in subparagraph (l) and
substituting the figure “150”;
(b) deleting the full stop at the end of subparagraph
(q) and substituting a semicolon;
(c) inserting a new subparagraph (r) immediately
after subparagraph (q) –
“(r) the fuel surcharge on electrical energy
provided by Dominica Electricity Services
Limited.”.

Amendment of
Schedule II.

### 5.

Schedule II to the Act is amended in paragraph 2
subparagraph (aa) by -
