16th February, 2023
AN ACT TO AMEND THE VEHICLES AND ROAD
TRAFFIC ACT, CHAPTER 46:50.
(Gazetted 17th February, 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the VEHICLES AND ROAD TRAFFIC
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023.

Short title and
commencement.

VEHICLES AND ROAD
TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT)

ACT 7

(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st
day of August, 2022.
Interpretation.
Chapter. 46:50.

### 2.

In this Act the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act is referred
to as “the Act”.

Insertion of a new
section 21A.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting a new section 21A
immediately after section 21 –
“Temporary
reduction in
licence
duties.

21A. Notwithstanding section 21(1), from the 1st
day of August, 2022, to the 31st day of July, 2023, the
licence fees set out in the First Schedule shall be
reduced by fifty percent in respect of licences issued for
the following vehicles:
(a) public service vehicles;
(b) trucks and dumpers available for hire.”.

Repeal of section 111A.

### 4.

Section 111A of the Act is repealed.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 8th day of February, 2023.
DANIEL JAMES
