16th February, 2023
AN ACT TO AMEND THE TITLE BY REGISTRATION
ACT, CHAPTER 56:50.
(Gazetted 17th February, 2023.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the TITLE BY REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023.

Short title and
commencement.

TITLE BY REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 4

(2) Except for section 3(b), this Act comes into force on
the 1st day of November, 2022.
(3) Section 3(b) of this Act is deemed to have come into
force on the 1st day of August, 2022.
Interpretation.
Chapter. 56:50.

Amendment of the
Fourth Schedule.

### 2.

In this Act the Title by Registration Act is referred to as
“the Act”.

### 3.

The Fourth Schedule to the Act is amended by –
(a) deleting paragraph 5 and substituting the following –
“On every memorandum of transfer of land, a judicial
fee of 1% of the value of the land.”.
(b) inserting the following as paragraph 8 –
“On replacement of a lost certificate of title, the
lesser of the following two fees:
(a) assurance fund 1%, judicial fee 1%, and
stamp duty $7.50 on the value of the land; or
(b) five thousand dollars.”.

Amendment of the Fifth
Schedule.

### 4.

The Fifth Schedule to the Act is amended in paragraph 1
by deleting the words “of 3 %” and substituting the words “not
exceeding 2.5 %”.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 8th day of February, 2023.
DANIEL JAMES
