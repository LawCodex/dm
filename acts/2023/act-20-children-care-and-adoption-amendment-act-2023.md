Interpretation.
No. 12 of 2023.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” refers to the Children (Care and
Adoption) Act.

Amendment
of section 2.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by deleting the definition
of “Court” and substituting the following:
“ “Court” means the Magistrates Court, except in relation
to provisions dealing with adoption in which instance it
means the High Court;”.

Amendment of
section 121.

### 4.

Section 121 of the Act is amended in paragraph (a) by (a) deleting the word “consent” where it first appears
and substituting the word “consents”;
(b) inserting a comma immediately after the first
appearance of the word “adoption”.

Amendment of
section 148.

### 5.

Section 148 of the Act is amended by deleting subsections
(1) and (2) and substituting the following:
“(1) If an application for an order was made under the
former Act, that application shall be continued under the former
Act.

(2) If an order has been made in respect of a child under the
former Act, this Act applies if that order is brought before the
Court for review.”

Passed in the House of Assembly this 14th day of December, 2023.
