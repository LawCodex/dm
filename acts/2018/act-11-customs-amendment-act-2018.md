AN ACT TO AMEND THE CUSTOMS ACT, NO. 20 OF
2010\.
(Gazetted 5th July, 2018).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
CUSTOMS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2018.

Short title.

Amendment of section 2.

### 2.

The Act is amended in section 2 by inserting the
following definitions in appropriate alphabetical order:
““advance cargo information” means
information or data concerning (a)

cargo and stores including all imports,
exports, in transit and transshipment
goods contained in a manifest, airway
bill or bill of lading; and

(b)

any package in respect of which there
is not a bill of lading or airway bill,

on board a conveyance as set out in Part 1
of Schedule 6;
“cargo reporter” includes a freight forwarder,
consolidator, and non-vessel operating
common carrier;
“IMPACS” means the Implementing Agency
for Crime and Security established
under the 2006 Agreement establishing
the CARICOM Implementing Agency
for Crime and Security;”.
Repeal and Replacement
of section 18.

### 3.

Section 18 of the Act is repealed and replaced by the
following:
“Advance
passenger and
cargo
information.

18\. (1) The master or commander of every
vessel or aircraft whether laden or in ballast, and
every cargo reporter shall, prior to arrival of the
vessel or aircraft in Dominica, provide to IMPACS
through the proper officer the cargo information set
out in Part 1 of Schedule 6 and a list of all passengers

and crew on board the vessel or aircraft within the
periods specified in Part 2 of that schedule.
(2) Where the distance from Dominica to a
port of departure of a conveyance arriving in
Dominica does not permit the master to provide the
information within the time specified in Part 2 of
Schedule 6, the Comptroller (a)

may exempt the master or commander
of the vessel or aircraft from the
requirements set out in that Schedule;
and

(b)

shall establish alternative notification
arrangements for the conveyance to
which this subsection applies.

(3) The master or commander of a
conveyance and every cargo reporter shall, prior to
departure of the vessel or aircraft from Dominica
provide to the proper officer, the cargo information
set out in Part 3 of Schedule 6 and a list of all
passengers and crew on board the vessel or aircraft.
(4) On the arrival of a vessel or an aircraft
within Dominica or the waters of Dominica, the
master or commander of the vessel or aircraft or the
relevant agent shall proceed directly to the customs
place notified under subsection (1), unless directed
elsewhere by a proper officer.
(5) A master or cargo reporter who fails to
comply with subsections (1), (2), (3) or (4) commits
an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a
fine of $10,000 or to imprisonment for a term of one
year.

(6) The Minister may, by Order subject to
negative resolution of the House, amend or replace
Schedule 6.
(7) The Minister may make Regulations
prescribing procedures for the reporting and
processing of advance cargo information.”.
Insertion of new section
18A.

### 4.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section 18A immediately after section 18:
“Use, sharing
and retention
of advance
cargo
information.

18A.(1) Advance cargo information shall be
used solely for the purposes of this Act.
(2) IMPACS shall use advance cargo
information to conduct screening against watch
lists of cargo on conveyances that enter into, depart
from, and travel within, the regional space in order
to provide information to assist Member States.
(3) The Comptroller may share the
information contained in the ACIS with the entities
specified in Part 4 of Schedule 6.
(4) Advance cargo information shall be
retained for a period not exceeding seven years
from the date collected.
(5) For the purposes of this section“ACIS” means the electronic manifest
single window for advance cargo
information data interchange;
“Member States” has the meaning assigned
in section 109;

“watch list” means a list that is used to
watch or track the current activity or
movements of criminals and
suspicious or prohibited cargo,
including terrorist, other persons and
cargo of interest to the community.”.

## PART 2

(Section 18(2))
The information set out in Part 1 of this Schedule shall be
provided:
(a) in the case of commercial or private aircraft, not later
than 40 minutes prior to departure from their last port
of call;
(b) in the case of a vessel or conveyance arriving from a
member state of the Caribbean Community, not later
than one hour prior to its arrival from that member
state; and
(c) in the case of a vessel or conveyance arriving from a
country which is not a member state of the Caribbean
Community, at least 24 hours prior to its arrival;
(d) in the case of commercial or private aircraft, not later
than 40 minutes prior to departure from Dominica;
(e) in the case of a vessel or other conveyance, not later
than one hour prior to its departure from Dominica.

## PART 3

The information set out in Part 1 of this Schedule shall be
provided:
(a) in the case of commercial or private aircraft, not later
than 40 minutes prior to departure from Dominica;
and
(b) in the case of a vessel or other conveyance, not later
than one hour prior to its departure from Dominica,
(2) The passenger and cargo information referred to in
subsection (1) shall be (a) the country of destination;
(b) the estimated time of departure;
(c) the customs port, customs airport or place of departure;
(d) a list of cargo for export and the country to which the
cargo is to be exported;
(e) a list of the names of all passengers and crew on
board; and
(f) such other information as may be prescribed by
Regulations made under this Act.
