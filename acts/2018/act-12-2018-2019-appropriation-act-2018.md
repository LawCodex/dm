# 2018/2019 Appropriation Act, 2018

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 12 OF 2018
I assent

C.A. SAVARIN
President
3rd August, 2018
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE THIRTIETH DAY OF
JUNE, 2019.
(Gazetted 9th August, 2018.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the-

2018/2019 APPROPRIATION ACT , 2018.

Short title.

Appropriation.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of nine
hundred and seventy-eight million, one hundred and one thousand,
six hundred and twenty-five dollars ($978,101,625) is hereby
authorized for meeting the expenditure for services for the year
ending June 30th, 2019 under the Head of Expenditure specified in
the Schedule, and the sum specified in the Schedule opposite to the
Head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum for the purposes
specified in relation to the Head of the Estimates for the said year.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 12

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance, to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the Schedule, the several monies appropriated in the
manner as the said warrant shall direct without further order or
formality.

### 4.

This Act, is deemed to have come into force on the 1st day
of July, 2018.
