### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –

(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 56:50.

### 2.

In this Act the Title by Registration Act is referred
to as “the Act”.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by deleting the definition
of “Deputy Registrar”.

Amendment of section 2
of the Act.

Amendment of Part IX of
the Act.

### 4.

Part IX of the Act –
(a) is amended by (i) deleting the following:
“PART IX
POWERS AND DUTIES OF REGISTRARS
OF TITLE AND MODE OF
REVIEWING THEIR DECISIONS

Registrar of High
Court to be
Registrar of Titles.

Notings by
Registrars.

138\. (1) The Registrar of the High Court shall
be, ex-officio, the Registrar of Titles.
(2) All notings by the Registrar of Titles may
be done either by his own hand, or by the hand of one
of his clerks if such notings are duly signed and
authenticated by the Registrar.”; and
(ii) substituting the following:
“PART IX
LAND TITLE REGISTRY DIVISION,
POWERS AND DUTIES OF REGISTRAR
AND REVIEW OF DECISIONS
DIVISION I – LAND TITLE REGISTRY
DIVISION

Land Title
Registry
Division.

137A. There is established in the Ministry
responsible for legal affairs a Land Titles Registry
Division.

137B. (1) There shall be a Registrar of Titles
who shall be a public officer and an attorney-at-law
who shall be the head of the Land Title Registry
Division.
(2) The Registrar of Titles is responsible for
carrying out the functions and duties conferred on
him by this Act.

Staff of the
Division.

137C. There shall be staff of the Land Titles
Registry Division as are considered necessary for
carrying out the provisions of this Act.

DIVISION II - POWERS AND DUTIES OF

Notings by

of his clerks if such notings are duly signed and
authenticated by the Registrar.”;

(b) is amended by repealing sections 153A and 153B.
