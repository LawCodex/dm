# Police (Amendment) Act 2018

POLICE (AMENDMENT)

ACT 9

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT No. 9 OF 2018.

I assent

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President

14th May, 2018
AN ACT TO AMEND THE POLICE ACT (CHAP 14:01).
(Gazetted 17th May, 2018.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –

POLICE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Ch. 14:01.
Amendment of
section 2 of the Act.

POLICE (AMENDMENT)

ACT 9

### 2.

In this Act the Police Act is referred to as “the Act”.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by deleting the definition
of “gazetted officer” and substituting the following –
““gazetted officer” means the Commissioner, Deputy
Commissioner, Assistant Commissioner, Superintendent
or Assistant Superintendent;”.

Repeal and replacement
of section 15 of the Act.

### 4.

Section 15 of the Act is repealed and replaced by the
following:
“Justice of
the peace.

15\. The Commissioner, Deputy Commissioner
and Assistant Commisssioner shall be ex officio
justices of the peace.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 3rd day of May, 2018.

DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly
