# Aliens Land Holding Regulation (Amendment) Act, 2018

2018 ALIENS LAND HOLDING REGULATION ACT 7
(AMENDMENT)

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 7 OF 2018
I assent

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President
14th May, 2018
AN ACT TO AMEND THE ALIENS LAND HOLDING
REGULATION ACT, NO. 17 OF 1995.
(Gazetted 17th May, 2018.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the ALIENS LAND HOLDING REGULATION
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018.
(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 27th
day of November, 2015.

Short title and
commencement.

ALIENS LAND HOLDING REGULATION ACT 7
(AMENDMENT)

Interpretation.
Act No. 17 of 1995.

### 2.

In this Act the Aliens Land Holding Regultion Act 1995 is
referred to as “the Act”.

Amendment of section 4
of the Act.

### 3.

Section 4 (2) of the Act is amended by inserting the words
“or section 5” immediately after the figure “(1)”.

Amendment of
section 5A of the Act.

### 4.

Section 5A of the Act is amended by deleting subsection
(1) and substituting the following:
“(1) On acquiring land under section 5 for an approved
development, section 4(2) does not apply and the alien shall pay a
fee of six thousand dollars to the Accountant General on the
acquisition.”.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 3rd day of May, 2018.

DANIEL JAMES (MR.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly
