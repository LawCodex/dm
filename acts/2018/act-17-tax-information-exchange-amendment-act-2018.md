TAX INFORMATION EXCHANGE

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA

I assent

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President
19th December, 2018
AN ACT TO AMEND THE TAX INFORMATION EXCHANGE ACT, CHAP. 67:02.
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:
(Gazetted 20th December, 2018.)

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
TAX INFORMATION EXCHANGE
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 67:02.

### 2.

In this Act, the Tax Information Exchange Act is
referred to as “the Act”.

Insertion of new
section 2A.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section 2A after section 2:
“Inconsistent
laws.

TAX INFORMATION EXCHANGE

ACT 17

2A. (1) If any inconsistency arises between this
Act or an Agreement and any other law, the provisions of this Act or the Agreement shall prevail over
the other law to the extent of the inconsistency.
(2) If any inconsistency arises between this
Act and an Agreement, the provisions of the Agreement shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
(3) In this section “Agreement” means a
treaty, convention or any other international agreement that makes provision for the exchange of
information with respect to tax matters between a
foreign state and Dominica which has legal effect in
Dominica.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 12th day of December, 2018.

JO-ANN THOMAS (MRS.)
