### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
ENGINEERING PROFESSION ACT 2002.

Short title.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act –
“Appeals Tribunal” means the Tribunal referred to in section 22;

Act 21 of 1994.

“Association” means the body known as the Dominica Association of Professional Engineers incorporated in the Commonwealth of Dominica under the Companies Act;
“Board” means the Board of Engineering established under
section 4;
“Disciplinary Committee” means the Committee referred to in
section 11(1);
“Engineer” means a person who through specialized education,
training and experience is skilled in the principles and
practice of engineering;

First Schedule.

Second Schedule.

“Engineering” means the performance of or the assumption of
responsibilities for any professional service or creative
work requiring engineering education, training and experience, and the application of special knowledge of the
mathematical, physical and engineering sciences to such
professional services or creative work as consultation,
investigation, evaluation, planning, design, inspection and
supervision of construction for the purpose of assuring
compliance with specifications and design, execution of
responsible maintenance and operational functions, in
connection with the utilisation of the forces, energies and
materials of nature in the development, production and
functioning of any of the works or operations or other
matters set out in the First Schedule;
“Form”, identified by a number, means the Form of that number
set out in the Second Schedule;
“Minister” means the Minister with responsibility for engineering;
“Organisation” means a body of persons whether corporate or
unincorporate;
“Register” means the Register of Engineers required to be kept
by the Registrar in accordance with section 19;

“registered engineer” means an engineer registered under this
Act to engage in the practice of engineering as an engineer
in the category attested to by his certificate of registration;
“Registrar” means the Registrar appointed by the Board under
section 18.

### 3.

(1) Three months after the coming into operation of this
Act, no person, unless he is a registered engineer under this
Act, shall (a) carry on the practice of engineering;
(b) take or use, in relation to himself, the
description“registered engineer”, “Professional
engineer”, “engineer”, or any abbreviation of
those titles either alone or in conjunction with any
other words or initials in such a manner as to imply
that he is qualified to carry out the practice of
engineering;
(c) take or use, in relation to himself, any designation,
title, name, initials or description indicating or
implying that he is entitled to carry on the practice
of engineering;
(d) advertise himself as a registered engineer;
(e) act in a manner so as to induce the belief that he
is a registered engineer; or
(f) sign or stamp any plans, drawings, designs, specifications or reports purporting to be acting in the
capacity of a registered engineer.
(2) A person shall be deemed to carry on the practice
of engineering if (a) he practises any branch or category of engineering;

Prohibitions unless
registered engineer.

(b) by verbal claim, sign, advertisement, letter-head,
business card, electronic means or otherwise, he
represents himself to be a registered engineer;
(c) through the use of some title, he implies that he is
a registered engineer; or
(d) subject to subsection (3) he holds himself out as
being able to perform or actually performs any
engineering service or work.
(3) For the purposes of this Act, a person not being a
registered engineer, does not carry on the practice of engineering
by reason only that he does so in the course of his duties as an
employee or agent of a registered engineer or under the direction
of a registered engineer.
Establishment and
constitution of the
Board.

### 4.

(1) There is hereby established a Board of Engineering
which shall be a body corporate.
(2) The members of the Board shall be appointed by the
Minister and shall consist of (a) four registered engineers nominated by and holding senior membership in the Association;
(b) a public officer who is registered or is eligible to
be registered as a registered engineer; and
(c) two persons appointed to represent the public
interest.
(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), for the purpose of
appointing the first Board (a) the four engineers nominated under subsection
(2)(a) shall not be registered engineers, but shall
be senior members of the Association; and
(b) the public officer appointed as a member of the
Board under subsection (2)(b) shall be a senior
member of the Association.

(4) Except in the case of the first Board, the Chairman
shall be a registered engineer, appointed by the Board from among
its members.
(5) Only citizens of or persons who are ordinarily resident
in the Commonwealth of Dominica shall be eligible to be appointed
to the Board.
(6) A person appointed to the Board under subsection
(2)(a) and (b) shall have been engaged in the practice of
engineering for at least eight years.
(7) For the purpose of subsections (2)(a) and (3) and
section 24(1), the term “senior membership in the Association”,
or “senior member of the Association” means an engineer who
holds membership as a Member of the Association under the ByLaws of the Association as distinct from an engineer who holds
membership in the Association as an Associate Member, Student
Member, Affiliate Member or Honorary Member.

### 5.

A person appointed to the Board holds office for a term
of three years but is eligible for re-appointment for a maximum of
two further consecutive terms.

Term of office.

### 6.

(1) A member of the Board other than the Chairman may
at any time resign his office by instrument in writing addressed to
the Minister and transmitted through the Chairman.

Resignation.

(2) The Chairman may at any time resign his office by
instrument in writing addressed to the Minister.

### 7.

The Minister may, after consultation with the Board,
revoke the appointment of any member of the Board who –
(a) absents himself from three consecutive meetings of
the Board without leave of the Board;
(b) is convicted of an offence under this Act or an
offence under any law of the Commonwealth of
Dominica involving dishonesty, fraud or turpitude;
(c) becomes totally or permanently incapable of performing his duties as a member of the Board;

Revocation of
appointment.

(d) is the subject of disciplinary proceedings under this
Act; or
(e) is guilty of gross misconduct in relation to the
performance of his duties.
Vacancies.

### 8.

Where a vacancy occurs in the membership of the Board,
such vacancy may be filled by the Minister in the same manner
and on the same basis on which the appointment becoming vacant
was made and the new member shall hold office for the remainder
of the period for which his predecessor was appointed.

Meetings.

### 9.

(1) The Board shall hold meetings as often as may be
necessary or expedient for the purpose of fulfilling its functions
and such meetings shall take place not less than twice in each
calendar year.
(2) Ten days notice in writing of every meeting of the
Board shall be given to each member of the Board.
(3) A quorum of any meeting of the Board shall be four
members of which at least two shall be registered engineers,
except in the case of the first Board, but in other respects the
Board may regulate its own procedure.
(4) Decisions of the Board are to be by a majority of
votes, and where voting is equal, the Chairman, in addition to his
original vote, has a casting vote.

Functions of the Board.

### 10.

The functions of the Board are (a) to assess the qualification and experience of
persons applying for registration as registered
engineers;
(b) to conduct examinations of persons applying for
registration as registered engineers;
(c) to register engineers and organizations engaging
in engineering work and to issue certificates of
registration to engineers and certificate of authorization to organizations;

(d) to monitor adherence to and investigate breaches
of the Code of Ethics;
(e) to exercise disciplinary control over registered
engineers;
(f) to publish by Notice in the Gazette for public
scrutiny, in a format to be decided by the Board,
a list of qualifications and institutions recognized
by the Board in respect of the engineering training
required by this Act;
(g) to advise the Minister respecting amendments to
this Act or the making of Regulations or Orders
to be made under this Act; and
(h) generally to regulate the practice of engineering
in accordance with this Act and Regulations
made thereunder.

### 11.

(1) There is hereby established a Disciplinary Committee
consisting of three registered engineers not being members of the
Board who shall be appointed by the Board.
(2) The Disciplinary Committee shall investigate and
report to the Board on any allegation of misconduct or breach of
the Code of Ethics by registered engineer as to whether there are
grounds for disciplinary proceedings being brought before the
Board against that registered engineer.
(3) On receipt of such report from the Disciplinary
Committee, the Board shall (a) determine whether or not disciplinary proceedings shall be brought in accordance with section
31; and
(b) if such proceedings are brought, furnish a copy of
such report to the registered engineer against
whom they are brought.
(4) The report of the Disciplinary Committee may be
used in the proceedings but the Board shall not be restricted to
evidence appearing or referred to in the report.

Disciplinary Committee.

Other Committees.

(1) The Board may appoint such other committees as it
may think fit, including an Accreditation Committee and an
Assessment Committee, to assist in the exercise of its functions.

### 31.

(1) In any disciplinary proceedings held by the Board, the
Board shall in addition to any other requirement imposed by any
Regulations under this Act –
(a) furnish two weeks minimum notice to the registered engineer against whom the misconduct or
breach of the Code of Ethics is alleged, stating the

(b) allow the registered engineer against whom the
misconduct or breach of the Code of Ethics is
alleged, the right to be heard in his defence, to be

may, where it is satisfied that an allegation of misconduct or
breach of the Code of Ethics is proved, reprimand the registered
engineer or suspend or cancel his Certificate of Registration.
