# International Maritime (Amendment) Act,2002

COMMONWEALTH OFDOMINICA
ACT No. 8 OF 2002

I assent

LS

VERNON L. SHAW
President

9th May, 2002
AN ACT TO AMEND THE INTERNATIONAL MARITIME ACT, NO. 9 OF 2000.
(Gazetted 16th May, 2002.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

(AMENDMENT) ACT 2002.

Short title.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act -

Act No. 9 of 2000.

“the Act” means the International Maritime Act, 2000; and

Act No. 12 of 2001.

“the amending Act” means the International Maritime (Amendment) Act, 2001.

Amendment of section 2
of the Act.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by repealing subsection
(2) and substituting the following:
“(2) Vessels eligible to be documented or re documented under the provisions of this Chapter include
yachts, fishing vessels and vessels built anywhere and
operated under the flag of the Commonwealth of
Dominica having to comply with (a) the International Convention on Load Lines,
1966; or
(b) any Regulations made by the Minister”.

Amendment of section 11
of the Act.

### 4.

Section 11(2) of the Act is amended by deleting the words
“by it” and substituting the words “by him”.

Insertion of section 16A
to the Act.

### 5.

The Act is amended by the insertion of the following
section immediately after section 16 Power of
Maritime
Administrator to
impose
fines.

Amendment of section 30
of the Act.

16A. Notwithstanding anything in this Act
or any other law, Regulations made under this
Act may empower the Maritime Administrator
to impose a fine up to EC150,000 dollars and
such fines shall be a lien on the ship or vessel as
the case may be.

### 6.

Section 30 of the Act as amended by section 7 of the
amending Act is further amended by repealing the words “(1) A
yacht, fishing vessel, or vessel required to comply with the
International Convention on Load Lines, 1966, built anywhere,
owned by a -” and substituting the following:

“(1) A yacht, fishing vessel or vessel
required to comply with the International
Convention on Load Lines 1966 or with any
Regulations made by the Minister, built anywhere and owned by a -”

### 7.

Section 37 of the Act is amended -

Amendment of section 37
of the Act.

(a) in subsection (1) by deleting the words “prescribe
and” and
(b) in subsection (2) by deleting the word “prescribed”
and substituting the words “obtainable from the Maritime Administrator”.

### 8.

Section 57 of the Act is amended as follows:

Amendment of section 57
of the Act.

(a) by renumbering that section as subsection (1); and
(b) by inserting the following as subsections (2) and (3) “(2) The Minister may from time to time
prescribe by Regulations safety standards for
small vessels.
(3) For the purpose of this section “small
vessels” include (a) commercial vessels under 500
tons and 24 metres or more in
length;
(b) commercial vessels under 24
metres;
(c) pleasure vessels under 24
metres.

### 9.

Sections 155, 156, 157 and 158 of Chapter 8 inserted in the
Act by section 12 of the amending Act is hereby amended by
renumbering those sections as sections 155A, 156A, 157A and
158A respectively.

Renumbering of sections
in Chapters 8 of the Act.

Repeal of section 11 of
the amending Act.

ACT 8

### 10.

Section 11 of the amending Act is hereby repealed.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 25th day of April, 2002.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS)
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
