VERNON L. SHAW

30th September, 2002
AN ACT TO AMEND THE STAMP ACT CHAPTER
68:01
(Gazetted 3rd October, 2002.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –

STAMP (AMENDMENT) ACT 2002.
(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into effect
on the 1st day of July 2002.

Short title and
Commencement.

STAMP (AMENDMENT)

ACT 14

Interpretation.
Ch. 68:01.

### 2.

In this Act, the Stamp Act is referred to as “the Act”.

Amendment of
Schedule to the Act.
Chap. 68:01.

### 3.

The Schedule to the Act is amended(a) by deleting the paragraph appearing immediately
after Heading “CONVEYANCE OR TRANSFER
ON SALE OF ANY PROPERTY (except stock
and debentures)” up to and including the word
“transferee” and substituting therefor the following:
“A Stamp Duty of 6.5% on the value of the
property real or personal, transferred shall be
paid of which 2.5% shall be paid by the transferor
and 4% by the transferee:”;
(b) by deleting the paragraph appearing immediately
after the Heading “CONVEYANCE OR
TRANSFER operating as voluntary disposition inter
vivos” up to and including the word “transferee” and
substituting therefor the following:
“A Stamp Duty of 6.5% of the value of the
property so conveyed shall be paid of which 2.5%
shall be paid by the transferor and 4% by the
transferee.”.

Application.

### 4.

(1) The amendments effected by section 3 of this Act
apply only with respect to conveyances and transfers executed on
or after the 1st day of July 2002 and it shall be the duty of the
Registrar of Titles to ensure that the proper Stamp Duty is duly
affixed, dated and endorsed by the relevant public officer.
(2) Any arrangement or agreement under which the
Stamp Duty payable by the transferor under this Act shall be
paid by the transferee shall be null and void.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 10th day of September, 2002.
