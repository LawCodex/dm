### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by the insertion of the
following definition in alphabetical order “District Conference” means the governing body of the
Church in Dominica”.
“District Secretary/Treasurer” includes the District
Secretary and District Treasurer of the Corporation
appointed by the District Conference;
“District Superintendent” means the District
Superintendent of the Corporation appointed by the
District Conference.

Amendment of
section 10 of the
Act.

### 4.

Section 10 of the Act is amended by repealing that section
and substituting the following new section:
“Legal
10.(1) All actions and proceedings by or on
Proceedings,
behalf
of or against the Church in Dominica may
etc.

be brought or defended by and in the name of
“the District Superintendent of the Pentecostal
Assemblies of the West Indies” by that description
(without naming the holder for the time being of
that office) for and on behalf of the Church and
the death or incapacity of the District
Superintendent for the time being of the District
Conference or the expiration of his term of office
or any act or thing done or suffered by him shall
not abate or affect any such action or proceeding.
(2) Any writ, notice or other process in
any such action or proceeding shall be sufficiently
served on the Church in Dominica if served
personally on the District Superintendent or the
District Secretary/Treasurer for the time being
of the District Conference or left at the office of
the District Conference.

(3) Any affidavit, statutory or other declaration
or other similar document required from or desired
by the Church in Dominica may be made by the
District Superintendent, the Assistant District
Superintendent or the District Secretary/
Treasurer for the time being of the District
Conference for and on behalf of the Church.
(4) Every District Superintendent, Assistant
District Superintendent or District Secretary/
Treasurer shall be indemnified out of the funds of
the Church against all loss, damages, costs and
expenses which he may sustain by reason of
anything done to or by him in pursuance of this
section.”.

### 5.

The Act is amended by inserting the following as section Insertion of section
13 of the Act.

13:
“Execution and 13.
subscription of
deeds etc.

All deeds of conveyance or re-conveyance,
mortgages, leases, contracts, assignments or
other deeds and documents whatsoever and
all powers of attorney and notarial instruments
required to be executed by the Pentecostal
Assemblies of the West Indies shall be
deemed sufficiently executed if sealed with
its common seal and authenticated by the
District Superintendent or District Secretary
or District Treasurer or other person or
persons appointed by the District
Superintendent or a pastor or other person
appointed by the District Conference and
such execution shall be valid and effectual in
law.”
