# 2002/2003 Appropriation Act, 2002

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 9 OF 2002
I assent

LS

VERNON L. SHAW
President
18th July, 2002
AN ACT to provide for the services of the Commonwealth of
Dominica for the year ending on the thirtieth day of June
2003\.
(Gazetted 25th July, 2002)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the 2002/2003 APPROPRIATION ACT 2002.

Short title.

Appropriation.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of two
hundred and forty-seven million, six hundred and seven thousand,
two hundred and five dollars ($247,607,205) is hereby authorised
for meeting the expenditure for services for the year ending June
30, 2003 under the Heads of Expenditure specified in the Schedule
hereto, and the sum specified in the said Schedule opposite to the
Head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum of $247,607,205
for the purposes specified in relation to that Head in the Estimates
for the said year.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 9

### 3.

The Accountant General is hereby authorised and required from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance,
to pay out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several
services specified in the said Schedule, the several monies hereby
appropriated in such manner as the said warrant shall direct
without further order or formality.

### 4.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the
1st day of July, 2002.
