### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the STABILISATION LEVY ACT 2002.
(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on

### 2.

(1) In this Act –
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Inland Revenue appointed
under section 3 of the Income Tax Act.
“gross income”(a) in the case of employees includes(i) salaries, wages and perquisites; and

Application.

### 3.

The levy imposed by this Act is payable by and in respect
of individuals only.

Administration.

### 4.

(1) The Comptroller shall be responsible for the due
administration of this Act.
(2) Any function conferred by this Act on the Comptroller is exercisable by any public officer authorised by the Comptroller for the purpose.

Imposition of levy.

### 5.

Subject to section 9(1), there shall be charged for each
year of assessment on the gross income of every individual for
that year a levy at the rate of four percent to be calculated in
accordance with section 6.

### 6.

(1) The levy payable by an individual from his employment
income shall be calculated -

Calculation of levy.

(a) in respect of the income year 2002 on his income
for the period 1st July 2002 to 31st December
2002;
(b) in respect of the income year 2003 on the income
for the period 1st January 2003 to 30th June 2003; and
(c) in respect of subsequent income years on the
income for the period as may be determined by
the Minister by Order subject to affirmative
resolution of the House of Assembly.
(2) The levy payable by an individual who is selfemployed shall be calculated (a) in respect of the income year 2002 on half his
income for the period 1st January 2002 to 31st
December 2002;
(b) in respect of the income year 2003 on half his
income for the period 1st January 2003 to 31 st
December 2003; and
(c) in respect of subsequent income years on the
income for the period as may be determined by
the Minister by Order subject to affirmative
resolution of the House of Assembly.
(3) In calculating the levy payable by a self-employed
individual (a) losses for years preceding the relevant income
year are not to be taken into account;
(b) income from dividends is the actual amount
received;
(c) income from trade, business or profession is the
net profit derived.

### 7.

(1) Every employer shall deduct or withhold the levy
every month from the emoluments paid by him to his employees

Employers to

at the rate specified in section 5 and remit it to the Comptroller
on or before the twentieth day in the following month and so on.
(2) Every employer shall when making payment under
subsection (1) furnish to the Comptroller a return in such form as
the Comptroller may direct.
(3) Without limiting the generality of subsection (2), the
return shall include the names of the employees, the emoluments
paid and the amount deducted or withheld by way of levy and
remitted.
(4) Individuals who have employment income and income
from other sources must pay the levy based on the profit from
each business activity to the Comptroller every quarter as if they
are self-employed for the purpose of the levy payable by them in
respect of the other sources of income referred to above.
Payment by selfemployed.

### 8.

(1) Every self-employed person and every individual
carrying on a trade, business,vocation or profession shall pay the
levy every quarter on or before the twentieth day in the following
month on the basis of his gross income and for the purpose of the
levy his gross income shall be estimated on the basis of his gross
income for the previous year or on the estimated gross income for
the year and so on.
(2) For the purposes of subsection (1) “quarter” is the
period of three months immediately preceding the month in which
individuals referred to are liable to remit the levy to the Comptroller under that subsection.

Exceptions.

such income is less than nine thousand dollars;
