# Supplementary Appropriation (2001/2002) Act,2002

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT 2

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 2 OF 2002

I assent

LS
VERNON L. SHAW
President.

9th May, 2002.
AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM
THE CONSOLIDATED FUND OF DOMINICA
IN EXCESS OF THE SUM PROVIDED BY LAW
FOR THE SERVICES OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA DURING THE YEAR
ENDING THE THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE 2002.
(Gazetted 16th May, 2002.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(2001/2002) ACT 2002.

Short title.

2002 SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION

Legalisation of
unforeseen
expenditure.
Schedule.

### 2.

(1) The sum of five million, eight thousand six hundred
and ninety six dollars and seventy five cents ($5,008,696.75) as
described in the Schedule was withdrawn from the Consolidated
Fund of Dominica during the financial year ended on the 30th day
of June, 2002.

ACT 2

(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen governmental expenditure in anticipation of a
grant being made by the House of Assembly authorising the issue
of the specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.

Schedule.

(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica and appropriated to the services set out in the Schedule.

(Section 2).
