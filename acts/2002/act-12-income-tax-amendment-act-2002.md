### 4.

Section 43 of the Act is amended in subsection (3) by
deleting the words “two years” and substituting the words “four
years”.

### 5.

The Third Schedule of the Act is amended as follows:
(a) by renumbering paragraph 3 as subparagraph (1) of
that paragraph;
(b) by deleting in item (b) of the renumbered
subparagraph (1) the words “ten percent” and
substituting the words “twenty five percent”; and
(c) by inserting the following immediately after
subparagraph (1).
“(2) Notwithstanding subparagraph (1) the expenses
that relate to the rental income shall be allowed as deductions in
ascertaining the tax to be deducted.”
