AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM
THE CONSOLIDATED FUND OF DOMINICA IN
EXCESS OF THE SUM PROVIDED BY LAW FOR
THE SERVICES OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF
DOMINICA DURING THE YEAR ENDING THE
THIRTIETH DAY OFJUNE 2002.
(Gazetted 2nd January, 2003.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(2001/2002)(NO. 2) ACT 2002.

Short title.

Legalisation of
unforeseen
expenditure.
Schedule.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION

ACT 20

### 2.

(1) The sum of eleven million, five hundred and three
thousand three hundred and thirty four dollars and ninety three
cents ($11,503,334.93) as described in the Schedule was withdrawn
from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during the financial year
ended on the 30th day of June, 2002.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen governmental expenditure in anticipation of a
grant being made by the House of Assembly authorising the issue
of the specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.

Schedule.

(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica and appropriated to
the services set out in the Schedule.
