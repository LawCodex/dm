# Government Housing Loans (Amendment) Act, 2002

GOVERNMENT HOUSING LOANS
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 7

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
Act No.7 OF 2002

I assent

LS

VERNON L. SHAW
President

9thMay, 2002
AN ACT TO AMEND THE GOVERNMENT HOUSING
LOANS ACT CHAPTER 23:60.
(Gazetted 16th May, 2002).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

Short title.

GOVERNMENT HOUSING LOANS
(AMENDMENT) ACT 2002.

### 2.

In this Act the Government Housing Loans Act is
referred to as “the Act”.

Interpretation.
Ch. 23:60.

Amendment of section 12
of the Act.

### 3.

Section 12(5) of the Act is amended by inserting after the
word “Chairman” the words “or any officer generally or specifically
authorised in that behalf by resolution of the Board”.

GOVERNMENT HOUSING LOANS
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 7

Passed in the House of Assembly this 25th day of April, 2002.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS)
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
