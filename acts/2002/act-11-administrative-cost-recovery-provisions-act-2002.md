AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE RECOVERY OF
ADMINISTRATIVE COST
(Gazetted 3rd October, 2002.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –

ADMINISTRATIVE COST RECOVERY
PROVISIONS ACT, 2002.

Short title.

ADMINISTRATIVE COST
RECOVERY PROVISIONS

ACT 11

(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force
on 1st July, 2002.
Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act “Administrative Costs” means the fees chargeable under this
Act;
“Government” means the Government of Dominica;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Finance.

Imposition of
Administrative
Costs.

### 3.

(1) There shall be charged, levied, collected and paid into
the Consolidated Fund such Administrative Costs incurred in
respect of the issue of certain documents by Ministries and
Departments of the Government.
(2) The Permanent Secretary or Heads of Departments,
as the case may be, of the relevant Ministries or Departments of
Government, shall be responsible for the collection of the Administrative Costs.

Schedule.

(3) Ministries or Departments of the Government set out
in Column 1 of the Schedule below shall charge, levy and collect
fees set out in Column III for the issue of documents described
in Column II of the Schedule.

Fees to be credited
to the Consolidated
Fund.

### 4.

Fees charged, levied and collected by the respective
Ministries and Departments in any month shall be credited to the
Consolidated Fund before the tenth day in the following month and
so on.

Minister may amend
the Schedule.

### 5.

The Minister may, by Order published in the Gazette,
amend or revoke the Schedule.
