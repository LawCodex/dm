Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

Short title.

(AMENDMENT) ACT 2002.

### 2.

In this Act “Member State of the Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States”
means -

“the Act” means the Immigration and Passport Act.

Amendment of section 6
of the Act.

### 3.

Section 6 of the Act is amended by inserting immediately
after subsection (3) the following as subsection (3A):
“(3A) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsections (1), (2) and (3), a citizen of or belonger to a
Member State of the Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States may establish his identity and nationality
by the use of an identity card in the form of a National
Identification Card, a Driver’s Licence, a Voter’s
Registration Card or a Social Security Card with his
photograph thereon and duly issued to him by or on
behalf of the Government of the Member State of
Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States of which
he is a citizen or to which he is a belonger and valid
for the period of his stay in Dominica; but the rights
granted shall not exceed the rights of reciprocity.”.

Amendment of section 9
of the Act.

### 4.

Section 9 of the Act is hereby amended (a) in subsection (3) by deleting the word “fifty” and
replacing it with the word “ninety”; and
(b) in subsection (4) by deleting the word “fifty” and
replacing it with the word “ninety”.

Amendment of section 19
of the Act.

### 5.

Section 19 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
as subsection (5) “(5) Notwithstanding anything contained in this
section, a citizen of or belonger to a Member State of
the Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States shall
