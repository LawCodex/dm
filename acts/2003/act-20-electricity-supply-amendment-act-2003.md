### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Electricity Supply Act
1996\.

### 3.

The main purpose of this Act is to amend the Electricity
Supply Act 1996 having regard to -

(a) the prevailing market and economic conditions and
the need to protect public interest, by providing just
and equitable mechanisms as regards the computation of costs, income, price and rates respecting the
generation and supply of electricity in Dominica; and
(b) prescribing performance target that ensures adequate and sustainable supply of electricity at competitive rates while maintaining the financial viability
of the electricity utility.

### 4.

definitions in their alphabetical order “ “adjusted electricity supply figure” means the
electricity supply that is required once the reduction in line losses have been achieved;
“end price” means the sum of CIF fuel price plus
the customs service charge plus the gross margin.”

### 7.

Schedule 4 of the Act is amended -

(a) by inserting the following new paragraphs immediately after paragraph D “E Calculating of Excess Fuel Costs
Excess fuel costs shall be determined as (i) the consumption of fuel for generation
beyond the amounts required to meet the
adjusted electricity supply figure, after consideration of line loss limits; or
(ii) the consumption of fuel for generation as a
plant efficiency rate that is less than the
efficiency target as defined in this Schedule; or
(iii) charges for fuel using a fuel price that is in
excess of the price calculated in accordance
with the formula specified in paragraph F.
But where (a) fuel consumption is greater than the consumption
of fuel that satisfies the sum of the line loss and fuel efficiency
limits and (b) the fuel price is greater than the discount factor, the
excess fuel costs shall be calculated as follows:
Excess Fuel Costs = (base fuel cost + surcharge + taxes) x actual
fuel consumption - Current Cost of Fuel x fuel consumption level
which satisfies section E, clauses (i) and (ii).

F Calculation of Current Cost of Fuel
Current cost of fuel = (end price + sales tax +
consumption tax) x discount factor
G Calculation of Adjusted Electricity Supply
Where line loss is equal to or less than 14.5% of
the [Adjusted] electricity supply the adjusted
electricity supply shall be calculated as follows:
Adjusted electricity supply = consumption +
in-station losses + line losses.”
(b) in the paragraph headed “Guidelines for Schedule 4”
by deleting subparagraph (A) and substituting the
following:
“(A) No expenses shall be taken into account for
the purpose of determining the Interim/Final Rate
of Return for any financial year unless such
expense has been reasonably and necessarily
incurred in producing the operating revenues for
the said year. Costs incurred outside the boundaries of reasonable and necessary operations shall
be disallowed. Such disallowable costs shall
include, and not be limited to, excess fuel costs
and operation costs in generation referred to in
subparagraph (2)(a) of paragraph A. For the
avoidance of doubt costs incurred in excess of the
performance targets as set out in Schedule 5A
shall not have been reasonably and necessarily
incurred in producing the operation revenues for
the relevant year.”
