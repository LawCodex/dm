# Public Sector Salaries and Allowances (Temporary) Reduction Act 2003

COMMONWEALH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 10 OF 2003

I assent

LS

VERNON L. SHAW
President

1st August, 2003
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE (TEMPORARY)
REDUCTION OF SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES OF CERTAIN PERSONS WHOSE
SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES ARE PAID OUT
OF THE CONSOLIDATED FUND.
(Gazetted 7th August, 2003).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
PUBLIC SECTOR SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES
(TEMPORARY) REDUCTION ACT, 2003

Short title.

Interpretation.

ACT 10

### 2.

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires “ allowances” means all allowances and benefits set out in the
Second Schedule paid out of the Consolidated Fund;
“Minister” means the Minister for Establishment, Personnel and
Training;
“ salaries “ means basic salaries paid out of the Consolidated Fund
and include wages;

Reduction of salaries
and allowances.

First Schedule.

### 3.

(1) Notwithstanding anything in any written law, contract, order, award or agreement within the period July 2003 to
June 30, 2005 (a) the monthly salary payable to a person who is a
holder of an office set out in the First Schedule
and to any person holding contracts of employment with the Government including a person
engaged under section 70 of the Constitution
(herein after referred to as “specified person”)
shall be five percent less than his salary payable
for the month of June 2003;
(b) the monthly salary payable to the Prime Minister,
or a Minister of Government shall be seven
percent less than the salary payable for the month
of June 2003;

Second Schedule.

(c) an allowance set out in the Second Schedule
payable to the Prime Minister, a Minister of
Government or a specified person shall be ten
percent less than the allowance payable for the
month of June 2003.

Third Schedule.

(d) an allowance set out in the Third Schedule payable to a specified person shall be five percent
less than the allowance payable for the month of
June 2003.
(e) the entertainment allowance payable to the Prime
Minister, a Minister of Government or a specified

person shall be fifty percent less than the entertainment allowance payable for the month of
June 2003.
(2) Notwithstanding anything in any written law, contract,
order, award or agreement, within the period July 1, 2003 to June
30, 2004 annual increments payable to a specified person shall
not be paid but the loss of increment in the hands of the specified
person would be the amount of money the specified person would
have been eligible but for this Act and shall not affect the specified
person in any other way.
(3) The increments of specified persons not paid during
the period specified in subsection (2) shall be restored after
June 30, 2004 but no arrears shall be paid.
(4) Notwithstanding any law or agreement where
payment is made out of the Consolidated Fund to (a) a government assisted school;
(b) a local government body; or
(c) a corporation or statutory body
and such payment is directly, wholly or, partially utilised for the
payment of salaries, allowances or other such emoluments to the
staff or employees of the institutions referred to above the amounts
payable to such institutions out of the Consolidated Fund for the
period July 1, 2003 to June 30, 2005 shall be five percent less
than the amount that would be payable but for this Act:
Provided that the five percent reduction shall only be on that part
of the amount that is normally utilised for the payment of salaries,
allowances or emoluments

### 4.

In computing the salaries and allowances for the purpose
of reduction of salaries and allowances to be made under section
3 only salaries and allowances payable from July 1, 2003 to June,
30, 2005 shall be taken into account and no other.

Computation of
salaries and
allowances.

Pensions benefits
not to be affected.

### 5.

Pensions benefits of specified persons that are based on
salaries shall be computed on the basis of salaries that would have
been applicable if this Act had not been enacted.

Exemption.

### 6.

Notwithstanding anything in any written law or any other
law this Act shall not apply to holders of offices referred to in
section 81 of the Constitution set out in the Fourth Schedule and
to Judges of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court.

Fourth Schedule.

Regulations.

### 7.

The Minister may, subject to negative resolution of the
House of Assembly make Regulations to give effect to the
provisions of this Act.

Duration.

### 8.

The provisions of this Act other than section 5 shall cease
to have effect from the date specified by Order made by the
Minister and different dates may be specified in respect of
different provisions.

Commencement.

### 9.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on July1,
2003\.
