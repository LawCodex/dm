# Treasury Bill (Amendment) Act, 2003

TREASURY BILLS (AMENDMENT)

ACT 14

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
Act NO. 14 OF 2003

I assent

LS

VERNON L. SHAW
President

1st August, 2003
AN ACT TO AMEND THE TREASURY BILLS ACT
(CHAP. 64:03)
(Gazetted 7th August, 2003.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
TREASURY BILLS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2003.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 64:03
Section 7 amended.

TREASURY BILLS (AMENDMENT)

ACT 14

### 2.

In this Act “the Act”means the Treasury Bills Act.

### 3.

Section 7 of the Act is amended by inserting after the word
“dollars” the words “or a sum of five hundred United States
dollars”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 24th day of July, 2003.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly
