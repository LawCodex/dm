1st August, 2003
AN ACT TO AMEND THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE
(PRELIMINARY INQUIRIES) ACT, 1995 (No. 14
OF 1995)
(Gazetted 7th August, 2003.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
CRIMINAL PROCEDURE (PRELIMINARY
INQUIRIES) (AMENDMENT) ACT 2003.

Short title.

Interpretation.
(Act No. 14 of 1995).

### 2.

In this Act “the Act”means the Criminal Procedure
(Preliminary Inquiries) Act 1995.

Section 2 amended.

### 3.

Section 2(1) of the Act is amended by deleting paragraph
(b) and substituting the following new paragraph.

CRIMINAL PROCEDURE
(PRELIMINARY INQUIRIES)
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 9

“(b) the Magistrate is satisfied on a submission by counsel
for the defendant or one of the defendants that the
statements do not disclose enough evidence to put
the defendant on trial by jury for the offence.”
Section 3 amended.

### 4.

Section 3(1) of the Act is amended as follows:
(1) In subsection (1) by(a) inserting at the end of paragraph (b) the word
“and”;
(b) deleting the words “evidence; and” appearing at
the end of paragraph (c) and inserting the word
“evidence.”
(c) deleting paragraph (d)
(2) In subsection (3) by deleting the words “own motion
or shall” and substituting the words “own motion or may”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 23rd day of July, 2003.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly
