# Magistrates' Code of Procedure (Amendment) Act, 2003

2003 MAGISTRATES’ CODE OF PROCEDURE ACT 7
(AMENDMENT)

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
Act NO. 7 OF 2003

I assent

LS

VERNON L SHAW
President

29th May, 2003
AN ACT TO AMEND THE MAGISTRATES’ CODE OF
PROCEDURE (AMENDMENT) ACT CHAP 4:20
(Gazetted 5th June, 2003.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
MAGISTRATES’ CODE OF PROCEDURE
(AMENDMENT) ACT 2003.

Short title.

2003 MAGISTRATES’ CODE OF PROCEDURE ACT 7
(AMENDMENT)

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act”means the Magistrates’ Code of
Procedure Act Chap 4:20.

Section 19 amended.

### 3.

Section 19 of the Act is amended in paragraph (f) by
renumbering subparagraph (iii) as subparagraph (iv) and inserting
after subparagraph (ii) the following new subparagraph:
“(iii) to try any action for the recovery of tax due and
payable to the Comptroller of Inland revenue where
the amount of arrears or amount claimed does not
exceed thirty thousand dollars;”

Passed in the House of Assembly this 30th day of April, 2003.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly
