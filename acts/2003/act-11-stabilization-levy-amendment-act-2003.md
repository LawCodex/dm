### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

Short title.

STABILIZATION LEVY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2003

### 2.

In this Act, the Stabilization Levy Act, 2002 is referred
to as “the Act”.

Interpretation.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by replacing the
definition of “self-employed persons” with the following:
“ “self-employed person” means an individual whose
income is from sources other than employment including trade, business, vocation or profession;”.

of section 5 of the Act.
