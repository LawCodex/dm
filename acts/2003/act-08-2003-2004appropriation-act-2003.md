# 2003/2004Appropriation Act 2003

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 8 OF 2003

I assent

LS
(sgd.)V. L. Shaw
President
18th July, 2003.

AN ACT to provide for the services of the Commonwealth of
Dominica for the year ending on the thirtieth day of June
2004\.
(Gazetted 31st July, 2003)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the 2003/2004 APPROPRIATION ACT 2003.

Short title.

Appropriation.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of two
hundred and fifty-seven million, seven hundred and eighty-five
thousand, three hundred and fifty-six dollars ($257,785,356) is
hereby authorised for meeting the expenditure for services for the
year ending 30th June, 2004 under the Heads of Expenditure
specified in the Schedule hereto, and the sum specified in the said
Schedule opposite to the Head is hereby appropriated out of the
said sum of two hundred and fifty-seven million, seven hundred
and eighty-five thousand, three hundred and fifty-six dollars
($257,785,356) for the purposes specified in relation to that Head
in the Estimates for the said year.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 8

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance, to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the said Schedule, the several monies hereby
appropriated in such manner as the said warrant shall direct
without further order or formality.

### 4.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the
1 day of July, 2003.
