### 2.

In this Act the Sales Tax Act is referred to as “the Act”.

### 3.

Section 57 of the Act is amended by deleting all the words
from the words “one thousand dollars” and substituting the words
“five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for two years and in the
case of a continuing offence to a fine of one hundred dollars for
each day or part thereof during which the offence continues after
a conviction is first obtained.”
