ACT TO AMEND THE CO-OPERATIVE

(Gazetted 22nd April, 2004)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

Short title.

CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2003.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Co-operative Societies
Act, 1996.

Interpretation.
Act No. 15 of 1996.

Amendment of section 5.

### 3.

Section 5(2) of the Act is amended in paragraph (b) by
inserting after the word “societies” the words “other than credit
unions”.

Insertion of new section

### 5A.

4\. The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
immediately after section 5.
“Supervision”

CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 21

5A(1) Notwithstanding anything in any other
provisions of this Act, the Minister
r e sponsible for Finance shall be
responsible for the supervision of credit
unions.
(2) Where there is any conflict between
subsection (1) and any other provisions
of the Act or Regulations made under
the Act, subsection (1) shall prevail.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 30th day of December, 2003.
