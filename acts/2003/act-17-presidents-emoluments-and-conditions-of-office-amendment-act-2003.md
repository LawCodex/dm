I Assent

LS
N. J. O. LIVERPOOL
President

28th November, 2003
AN ACT TO AMEND THE PRESIDENT’S EMOLUMENTS
AND CONDITIONS OF OFFICE ACT, CHAPTER
1:12.
(Gazetted 4th December, 2003).

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
PRESIDENT’S EMOLUMENTS AND CONDITIONS
OF OFFICE (AMENDMENT) ACT 2003.

Short title.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act the President‘s Emoluments and Conditions of
Office Act is referred to as “the Act”.

Chap. 1:12.

Amendment of section 8
of the Act.

PRESIDENT’S EMOLUMENTS
AND CONDITIONS OF OFFICE
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 17

### 3.

Section 8 of the Act is amended by (a) numbering the section as subsection (1); and
(b) inserting the following as subsection (2) –

“(2) Where a residence is not provided in
accordance with subsection (1), the President
shall be paid a housing allowance at the rate of
twenty-four thousand dollars per annum.”
Commencement.

### 4.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the
1st day of November, 2001.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 18th day of November, 2003.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS)

Clerk of the House of Assembly.
