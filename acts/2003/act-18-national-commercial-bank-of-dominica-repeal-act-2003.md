28th November, 2003
AN ACT TO REPEAL THE NATIONAL COMMERCIAL BANK OF DOMINICA ACT (CHAP 74: 02)

(Gazetted 4th December, 2003).

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the NATIONAL COMMERCIAL BANK OF DOMINICA
(REPEAL) ACT 2003.

Short title.

Commencement.

### 2.

This Act shall come into operation on such date as the
Minister may by Order appoint hereinafter referred to as the
“appointed date”.

Interpretation.

NATIONAL COMMERCIAL BANK

ACT 18

### 3.

In this Act ‘the Act” means the National Commercial Bank Act Chap 74:02.
“Bank” means the National Commercial Bank of Dominica
established under section 3 of the Act.
“Minister” means the Minister for Finance;

Act No. 21 of 1994.

Repeal of Chap.
74:02.
When Order to be
made.

“successor company” means the company that would become
incorporated under the Companies Act as the National
Bank of Dominica Limited.

### 4.

The National Commercial Bank Act is hereby repealed .

### 5.

Before making an order referred to in section 2 the
Minister shall satisfy himself that (a) a company by the name the National Bank of
Dominica Limited has been incorporated as a limited
liability Company under the Companies Act 1994;
(b) the Certificate of Incorporation has been issued
under section 8 of the said Act to that Company; and
(c) the object of the company incorporated is to take
over the operations carried on by the Bank.

Savings.

### 6.

Notwithstanding anything in this Act or in any other Law(a) The Directors, the General Manager the Deputy
General Manager, the Secretary of the Board and
the staff of the Bank on the date this Act comes into
operation shall continue to be the Directors, the
General Manager, the Deputy General Manager,
the Secretary and Staff of the successor company
under the same terms and conditions of appointment, engagement or service as the case may be.

(b) all property of whatever description which immediately before the appointed date belonged to the Bank
or held by trustees on its behalf shall vest in the
successor company on the appointed date.
(c) all rights, debts and liabilities which immediately
before the appointed date vested in, imposed or
enforceable against the Bank shall be transferred to
the successor company on the appointed date and
shall be discharged and satisfied by the successor
company.
(d) the shareholders of the Bank on the appointed date
shall for all purposes be the shareholders of the
successor company.
(e) all actions pending in any court instituted by the Bank
or against the Bank on the appointed date may be
continued by the successor company or against the
successor company, as the case may be.
(f)

any claims by the Bank against any person or any
claim by any person against the Bank on the appointed
date may be pursued by the successor company or
against the successor company, as the case may be.

(g) all shares in any enterprise, whether in an enterprise
set up for the purpose of promoting the development
of a money market or securities market or for the
purpose of generating additional capital for the
promotion of the objects of the Bank or of improving
the financial mechanism for the financing of economic development in Dominica or any other
Caricom Territory, or any other enterprise, which
immediately before the appointed date were held by
the bank or held by trustees on its behalf shall be
transferred to the successor company on the
appointed date.

Licence under
Banking Act.

### 7.

Notwithstanding anything in the Banking Act or in any
other law the successor company shall for all purposes be deemed
to be licensed under the Banking Act to carry on banking business
until a licence is issued under the Banking Act and accordingly the
successor company shall be subject to the Banking Act.

### 8.

Notwithstanding anything in the Act or in section 2 or in
any other law, the Directors of the Bank shall proceed to establish
a company by the name the National Bank of Dominica Limited
under the Companies Act after November 17, 2003.
