### 3.

INCOME TAX
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 12

Section 28 of the Act is repealed.

### 4.

Section 29 of the Act is amended by inserting a new
subsection as follows:
“(6) This section shall not apply to a commercial
bank licensed to carry on banking business under the
Banking Act, 1991.”

### 5.

Section 48 of the Act is amended by inserting a new
subsection as follows:
“(3) For the purposes of this section, a reference
to “shares” shall not include a reference to shares
purchased in the Dominica Electricity Services Limited.”

Commencement.

### 6.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the
1st day of July, 2003.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 24th day of July, 2003.
