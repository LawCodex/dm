23:03.
(Gazetted 28th November, 2013)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
PUBLIC AND POLICE SERVICES COMMISSIONS
(REMUNERATION, PRIVILEGES AND OFFENCES)
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013.

Short title and
commencement.

ACT 18

PUBLIC AND POLICE SERVICE COMMISSIONS
(REMUNERATION, PRIVILEGES AND OFFENCES)
(AMENDMENT)
(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into operation
on the 1st day of November, 2011.

Interpretation.
Chap. 23:03

Amendment of the First
Schedule.

### 2.

In this Act the Public and Police Services Commissions
(Remuneration, Privileges and Offences) Act is referred to as
“the Act”.

The First Schedule of the Act is repealed and replaced by
the following:

Chairman
Deputy Chairman
Other members

$1,200.00 per month
