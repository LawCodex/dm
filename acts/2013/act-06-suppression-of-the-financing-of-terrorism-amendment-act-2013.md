# Suppression of the Financing of Terrorism (Amendment) Act, 2013

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 6 OF 2013

I assent

LS
E. WILLIAMS
President

10th April, 2013
AN ACT TO AMEND THE SUPPRESSION OF THE
FINANCING OF TERRORISM ACT, NO. 3 OF
2003\.

(Gazetted 18th April, 2013.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
SUPPRESSION OF THE FINANCING OF
TERRORISM (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013.

Short title.

Interpretation.
No. 3 of 2003.

### 2.

In this Act the Suppression of the Financing of Terrorism
Act, 2003 is referred to as “the Act”.

Amendment of
section 2.

SUPPRESSION OF THE FINANCING
OF TERRORISM (AMENDMENT)

ACT 6

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended (a) in the definition of “terrorist” (i) in paragraph (b) by deleting the word “acts” and
substituting the word “act”;
(ii) in paragraph (c) by deleting the word “terrorists”
and substituting the word “terrorist”.
(b) in the definition of “terrorist act” (i) in subparagraph (x) of paragraph (a) by deleting
the words “, or the funding of the activities of the
group or terrorist in any way,”;
(ii) in paragraph (b) by deleting the word “contention”
and substituting the word “convention”.

Amendment of
section 4.

### 4.

Section 4 of the Act is amended by (a) deleting the words “in order to carry out a terrorist
act.” and substituting the symbol “-” ;
(b) inserting the following new paragraphs “(a) in order to carry out a terrorist act;
(b) by a terrorist group; or
(c) by a terrorist.”

Amendment of
section 12A.

### 5.

Section 12A of the Act is amended by deleting the figure
“(1)” and substituting the figure “12 (1)”.

### 6.

Section 19A of the Act is amended in subsection (2) —

Amendment of
section 19A.

(a) by deleting the word “groups” and substituting the
word “grounds”;
(b) in paragraph (b) by deleting the letter “(a)” and
substituting the letter “(b)”.

### 7.

Section 19E of the Act is amended by inserting the figure
“19A” between the words “section” and “or”.

Amendment of
section 19E.

### 8.

Section 35 of the Act is amended in subsection (2) by
deleting the word “of” appearing between the words “law” and
“otherwise” and substituting the word “or”.

Amendment of
section 35.

### 9.

Section 36 of the Act is amended in subsection (4) deleting -

Amendment of
section 36.

(a) the words “Eastern Caribbean Central Bank” and
substituting the words “Financial Services Unit”;
(b) the words “Unit, Bank or Commissioner of Police”
and substituting the words “Unit or Financial Services
Unit”.

### 10.

Section 47 of the Act is amended (a) in subsection (1) by deleting (i) the word “person” and substituting the word
“persons”;
(ii) the words “such funds or assets” and substituting
the words “funds related to a terrorist or terrorist
group, including funds which are the subject of a
freezing order,”;
(b) in subsection (2) –

Amendment of
section 47.

(i) by deleting the word “subsection 1” and substituting
“subsection (1)”;
(ii) in paragraph (b) by deleting the word “institutions”
and substituting the word “instructions”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 11th day of March, 2013.

VERNANDA RAYMOND (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly (Ag.).
