BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may cited as the –
VEHICLES AND ROAD TRAFFIC
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 46:50.

### 2.

In this Act the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act is referred to
as “the Act”.

Amendment of
section 2.

(AMENDMENT)

ACT 23

### 3.

Section 2 of Act is amended –
(a) in the definition of “driving licence” by inserting the
words “or a driving permit recognised” after the word
“issued”;
(b) by inserting the following definition in proper alphabetical
order““Member State of the Organisation of the Eastern
Caribbean States” means –
(a) Antigua and Barbuda;
(b) Dominica;
(c) Grenada;
(d) Montserrat;
(e) Saint Kitts and Nevis;
(f) Saint Lucia;
(g) Saint Vincent and the Grenadines;”.

Insertion of section
49A.

### 4.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
49A immediately after section 49 –
“Recognition of
OECS driving
permits.

49A. (1) A valid driving permit issued by another
member state of the Organisation of Eastern
Caribbean States is a valid driving licence for the
purposes of this Act.

VEHICLES AND ROAD TRAFFIC
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 23

(2) A driving permit recognised under
subsection (1) only authorises the person in
respect of whom the permit is issued to drive a
motor vehicle of the class specified in the permit.
(3) In this section “driving permit” means
a permit that is issued in a Member State of the
Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States by the
counterpart authority of the Licensing Authority
and which is the equivalent of a driving licence
issued under this Act.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 28th day of October, 2013.
