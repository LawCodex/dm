BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –

Short title.

CRIMINAL LAW AND PROCEDURE
(AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT 2013.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Criminal Law and
Procedure Act, Chap. 12:01.

Interpretation.

Amendment of section
2 of the Act.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
definition in the proper alphabetical order -

section 2 of the Mental Health Act;”.

### 4.

Section 31C of the Act is amended in subsection (4) by
deleting the words “or section 31G”.

Amendment of section
31D.

### 5.

Section 31D of the Act is amended by deleting the words
“in accordance with section 31G”.

Amendment of section
31F.

### 6.

Section 31F of the Act is amended in subsection (1)(a) by
deleting the figure “31B” and substituting the figure “31A”.

Amendment of section
36A.

### 7.

Section 36A of the Act is amended in
(a) subsection (6) by deleting –
(i) the figure “(5)” and substituting the figure“(1)”;
(ii) the words “is approved” and substituting the
words “has special experience in the diagnosis
and treatment of mental disorder”.
(b) in subsection (9) by deleting the figure (7) and
substituting the figure “(8)”;
(c) in subsection (10) by deleting the figure “(7)” and
substituting the figure “(8)”;
(d) in subsection (11) by deleting the figure “(7)” and
substituting the figure “(8)”.

Amendment of section
36F.

### 8.

immediately after the word “practitioners” –
