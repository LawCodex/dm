### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Protection of Witnesses
Act 2013.

13\.

### 3.

Section 13 of the Act is amended (a) in subsection (1)(a) by deleting the figures “16, 17, 18,
19, 20, 21, or 24” and substituting “16, 17, 18, 19, 20
or 21”;
(b) in subsection (2) by deleting the figures “16, 17, 18,
19, 20, 21 or 24” wherever they appear and substituting
“16, 17, 18, 19, 20 or 21”.

Insertion of new section
27\.

### 4.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
27 immediately after section 26 –
“Live link
evidence from
outside the
Commonwealth
of Dominica.

27\. (1) The court may permit evidence to be
given by means of technology, such as the live link
that permits the virtual presence of the witness before
the court and that permits the court and the parties to
the proceedings to hear, examine and cross-examine
the witness, if the witness is outside the
Commonwealth of Dominica.
(2) Evidence given under subsection (1) shall be
given as though the witness was physically before
the court and the law relating to contempt of court
with respect to a refusal by the witness to answer a
question or to produce a document applies to such
evidence.
(3) Where on trial evidence has been given in
accordance with subsection (1), the judge shall give
the jury a warning, if any, that the judge considers

was given by means of technology, such as the live
