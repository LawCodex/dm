AN ACT TO AMEND THE MARRIAGE ACT CHAPTER
35:01
(Gazette 28th November, 2013.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the MARRIAGE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 35:01.

MARRIAGE (AMENDMENT)

ACT 21

### 2.

In this Act the Marriage Act is referred to as “the Act”.

Amendment of
section 2.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by deleting the definition of
the word “Schedule”.

Amendment of
section 6.

### 4.

Section 6 of the Act is amended by repealing subsection (4)
and replacing it with the following “(4) Every marriage officer may act as such anywhere within
the State.”.

Repeal and
replacement of
section 39.

### 5.

Section 39 of the Act is repealed and replaced as follows “Power of Minister
to grant marriage
licences.

39\. (1) Subject to the restrictions and conditions
mentioned in sections 40 and 42, the Minister may,
on payment of the application fee specified in
Schedule 2, grant a licence to marry without
publication of banns or notice to marry under this
Act.
(2) Where either of the applicants for a marriage
licence has not had his usual place of abode in the
State of Dominica in accordance with section 42
(2) (e), the Minister may grant a licence under
subsection (1) on payment of the application fee
and the additional fee specified in Schedule 2.
(3) Where the applicants under subsection (2)
are desirous of having their application processed
on a weekend or public holiday, the application may
be processed on a weekend or public holiday on
payment of the weekend and public holiday
processing fee specified in Schedule 2.
(4) A licence granted under this section shall

MARRIAGE (AMENDMENT)

ACT 21

have the effect of authorizing any marriage
officer to solemnise or celebrate the marriage
to which the licence relates within three (3)
months from the date of such licence anywhere
within the State.”.

### 6.

Section 61 (5) and (8) of the Act is amended by deleting
the words “in the Schedule” wherever they appear and replacing
them with the words “in Schedule 1”.

### 7.

Section 64 of the Act is repealed and replaced as follows “Celebration by
Registrar General of
Marriages or Registrar
of a void marriage.

Amendment of
section 61.

Repeal and replacement
of section 64.

64\. Any marriage officer, being the Registrar
General of Marriages or Registrar, who
knowingly and wilfully celebrates any marriage
in this Act declared to be null and void commits
an offence and is liable on conviction to
imprisonment for two years.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 28th day of October, 2013.
VERNANDA RAYMOND (MRS)
