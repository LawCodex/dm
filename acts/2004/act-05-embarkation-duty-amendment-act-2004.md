AN ACT TO AMEND THE EMBARKATION DUTY
ACT (Chap. 70:05)

(Gazetted 25th November, 2004.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
EMBARKATION DUTY (AMENDMENT) ACT 2004.

Short title and
commencement.

EMBARKATION DUTY
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 5

(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force
on July 1, 2004.
Interpretation.
Chap. 70:05.

### 2.

In this Act the Embarkation Duty Act is referred to as “the
Act”.

Amendment of
Section 4 of the
Act.

### 3.

Section 4 of the Act is hereby amended as follows:
(a) by deleting subsection (1) and substituting the
following new subsection:
“(1) Subject to subsection (2), there shall be
charged, levied and paid by the agent, captain
or master of any vessel whether a steamship
sailing vessel or other vessel, or aircraft in
respect of every person over twelve years of
age boarding the steamship, sailing vessel or
other vessel, or aircraft with or without ticket
for a destination outside of Dominica, a duty (in
this Act referred to as an “embarkation duty”)
of forty-five dollars in respect of Dominican
nationals and fifty-five dollars in respect of nonnationals.”

Passed in the House of Assembly this 22nd day of September, 2004.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
