AN ACT TO AMEND THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE
(PRELIMINARY INQUIRIES) ACT ( ACT NO.
14 0F 1995)
(Gazetted 17th June, 2004.)
Be it enacted by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of Dominica
as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the CRIMINAL PROCEDURE (PRELIMINARY
INQUIRIES) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2004.

Short title.

2004 CRIMINAL PROCEDURE (PRELIMINARY ACT 1
INQUIRIES) (AMENDMENT)

Interpretation.
Act No. 14 of 1995.
Repeal and replacement
of section 2 of the Act.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Criminal Procedure
(Preliminary Inquiries) Act, 1995.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is hereby repealed and replaced by
the following new section.
“Committal without
consideration of
evidence

2.(1) A Magistrate holding a preliminary inquiry
may, where all the evidence before the court
consists of written statements tendered to the
court under section 3, with or without exhibits,
commit a defendant for trial of the offence
charged without consideration of the contents
of those statements.
(2) Subsection (1) shall not apply where (a)

a defendant or one of the defendants
is not represented by a counsel; or

(b)

the Magistrate is satisfied on a submission by counsel for the defendant or one
of the defendants that the statements
do not disclose enough evidence to put
the defendant on trial by jury for the
offence.”

Repeal
Act No. 9 of 2003.

### 4.

Section 3 of the Criminal Procedure (Preliminary
Inquiries) (Amendment) Act 2003 is hereby repealed.

Commencement.

### 5.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into effect on
7th August, 2003.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 27th day of May, 2004.
