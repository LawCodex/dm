AN ACT TO REPEAL THE STABILIZATION LEVY
ACT 2002 (ACT NO. 13 OF 2002)
(Gazetted 25th November, 2004)
Be it enacted by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(a) This Act may be cited as theSTABILIZATION LEVY (REPEAL) ACT 2004.

Short title and
Commencement Date.

STABILIZATION LEVY
(REPEAL)

ACT 6

(b) This Act shall come into force on April 1, 2005.
Interpretation.
(Act No. 13 of 2002).

Repeal of Act.

Saving.

### 2.

In this Act ‘the Act’ means the Stabilization Levy Act
2002\.

### 3.

The Act is hereby repealed.

### 4.

Notwithstanding section 3 any sum which immediately
before the commencement of this Act becomes due and payable
by any person shall be recovered as if the Act has not been
repealed.

Passed in the House of Assembly the 23rd day of September, 2004.
