# Money Laundering (Prevention) (Amendment) Act, 2004

MONEY LAUNDERING PREVENTION
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 4

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 4 OF 2004

I assent

LS

N. J. O. LIVERPOOL
President

17th, November 2004
AN ACT TO AMEND THE MONEY LAUNDERING
(PREVENTION) ACT, 2000 (ACT NO. 20, 2000).

(Gazetted 25th November, 2004.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
MONEY LAUNDERING (PREVENTION)
(AMENDMENT) ACT 2004.

Short title and
commencement.

MONEY LAUNDERING PREVENTION
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 4

(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force
on July 1, 2004.
Interpretation.
Act No.20 of 2000.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Money Laundering
(Prevention) Act, 2000.

Amendment of
Section 2(1) of the
Act.

### 3.

The Act is amended in section 2(1) by deleting the
definition of “Minister” and substituting the following definition:
“ “Minister” means the Minister responsible for the
subject of Legal Affairs;”

Passed in the House of Assembly this 21st day of September, 2004.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
