# 2004/2005 Appropriation Act, 2004

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 2 OF 2004

I assent

LS

N. J. O. LIVERPOOL
President
27th July, 2004
AN ACT to provide for the services of the Commonwealth of
Dominica for the year ending on the thirtieth day of June
2005\.
(Gazetted 5th August , 2004)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the 2004/2005 APPROPRIATION ACT 2004.

Short title.

Appropriation.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

2004

APPROPRIATION

ACT 2

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of two
hundred and sixty-seven million, two hundred and forty seven
thousand, four hundred and forty eight dollars ($267,247,448) is
hereby authorised for meeting the expenditure for services for the
year ending 30th June, 2005 under the Heads of Expenditure
specified hereto, and the sum specified in the said Schedule
opposite to the Head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum
for the purposes specified in relation to the Head in the Estimates
for the said year.

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance, to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the said Schedule, the several monies hereby
appropriated in such manner as the said warrant shall direct
without further order or formality.

### 4.

This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of July, 2004.
