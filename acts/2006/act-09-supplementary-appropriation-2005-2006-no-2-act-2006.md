AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM
THE CONSOLIDATED FUND OF DOMINICA
IN EXCESS OF THE SUM PROVIDED BY LAW
FOR THE SERVICES OF THE COMMONWEALTH
OF DOMINICA DURING THE YEAR ENDING
THE THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE 2006.
(Gazetted 14th December, 2006.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(2005/2006) (No. 2) ACT 2006.

Short title.

Legalisation of
unforeseen expenditure.
Schedule.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION

ACT 9

### 2.

(1) The sum of forty-three million, four hundred and
ninety-five thousand, four hundred and sixty-nine dollars and
fourteen cents ($ 43,495,469.14) as described in the Schedule was
withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during the
financial year ending on the 30th day of June, 2006.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen governmental expenditure in anticipation of a
grant being made by the House of Assembly authorising the issue
of the specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of July, 2005 and appropriated to the
services set out in the Schedule.
