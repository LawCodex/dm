### 2.

In this Act“the Act” means the Public Works Corporation Act 2006.

### 3.

Section 29 of the Act is amended as follows(a) in subsection (3) by deleting the words “as provided for
in the Protection of Employment Act Chap 89:02” and
substituting the words “as provided in the following
subsections.
(b) by inserting the following subsections“(4) Subject to subsection (5)and (6) the redundancy
benefit payable to an employee where the employee has been continuously employed in the
public works
garage for a period of not less
than three years shall be calculated as follows:
(a) if the employees services does not exceed
five years, the payment shall be equal to one
weeks’ pay for each year the employee was
employed plus two weeks’ pay for each year
in excess of three years the employee was
employed.
(b) if the employees services exceeds five years
but does not exceed ten years, the payment
shall be equal to nine weeks pay plus two
weeks pay for each year in excess of
five years the employee was so employed.
(c) if the employees services exceeds ten years,
the payment shall be equal to nineteen weeks
pay plus three weeks pay for each year in
excess of ten years that the employee was so
employed.
(5) Where, in determining the amount of the
redundancy benefit payable to an employee, a
period of continuous employment occurs that is
less than a full year, that period shall -

(a) if it is not more than three months, count as
one-quarter of a year;
(b) if it is more than three months but not more
than six months, count as one half of a year;
(c) if it is more than six months but not more than
nine months, count as three quarters of a

(d) if it is more than nine months, count as one
year,
of continuous employment, and the employee
shall receive a redundancy benefit in respect of
that period equal to the product of the applicable
fraction of a year and the amount of redundancy
benefit that the employee would have been
entitled to receive in respect of the year in which
that period occurred if he had been employed for
that full year.
(6) A redundancy benefit payable to an employee
pursuant to this section shall not exceed an
amount that is equal to fifty-two weeks pay of
that employee.”

### 4.

The amendment made to the Act by section 3 shall for all
purposes be deemed to have come into force on the date of
commencement of the Act.

Retrospective effect to
section 3 of this Act.
