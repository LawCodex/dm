AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A
STATUTORY CORPORATION TO UNDERTAKE
PUBLIC WORKS FROM PUBLIC AND PRIVATE
SECTOR AND PROVIDE QUALITY SERVICE WITH
COMMERCIAL EFFICIENCY.

(Gazetted 7th December, 2006.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as thePUBLIC WORKS CORPORATION ACT 2006.

Short title.

Commencement.

Interpretation.

### 2.

This Act shall come into force on such date as the
Minister may by Order appoint herinafter referred to as the
“appointed date”.

### 3.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires“Board” means the Board of the Public works Corporation;
“Chairman” means the Chairman of the Board;
“Corporation” means the Public works Corporation;
“General Manager” means the General Manager of the Corporation;
“financial year” means the period of twelve months beginning
from the first day of July in any year;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for public works;
“public works” includes civil work, mechanical work and
maintenance work and any other work assigned by the
Minister;
“Secretary” means the Secretary of the Board.

Establishment.

### 4.

(1) There is hereby established a Corporation to be
known as the Public Works Corporation.
(2) The Corporation shall be a body corporate with
perpetual succession and a common seal, capable of suing and
being sued in its corporate name and shall have the power to enter
into contracts and acquire, dispose of or otherwise deal with
property whether moveable or immovable.
(3) All documents, other than those required by law to be
under seal , made by, and all decision of the Corporation may be
signified under the hand of the Chairman and the Secretary or any
other member or an employee of the Corporation authorised to
act in that behalf.

Service of Document.

### 5.

Service upon the Corporation of any notice, order or other
document shall be effected by delivering such document or
sending it by post addressed to the Secretary at the office of the
Corporation.

### 6.

The Corporation shall have a seal which shall be
authenticated by the signature of the Chairman or the Deputy
Chairman and the Secretary of the Corporation.

### 7.

The objectives of the Corporation shall be to –

45

Seal.

Objectives.

(a) undertake public works and deliver quality service
at reasonable rates so as to operate as a viable entity;
(b) provide training in public works for trainees assigned by the Dominica State College;
(c) do any other work the corporation may undertake in
order to make optimum use of its resources.

### 8.

(1) The affairs of the Corporation shall be managed by
a Board of Directors consisting of seven members appointed by
the Minister as follows:
(a) the Chief Technical Officer who shall be an
ex officio member; and
(b) six other persons;
(2) In making his appointment of members referred to in
paragraph (b), the Minister shall have regard to having persons
in the Board who would collectively have experience and sound
qualifications in as many as possible of the subject of public
works, industry, commerce, accountancy, economics, engineering or law.
(3) The Minister shall appoint the Chairman and Vice
Chairman of the Board from among the members who are
appointed under paragraph (b) of subsection (1).
(4) The Board shall appoint a Secretary to the Board.
(5) The Board may invite one or more persons to attend
any particular meeting of the Board for the purpose of assisting
or advising the Board.

Board.

Disqualification.

### 9.

A person shall be disqualified from being a member of the
Board if -

ACT 7

(a) he is an unrehabilitated insolvent; or
(b) he has been convicted of a serious crime.
Term of Office of
members of the Board.

### 10.

(1) Members of the Board other than the ex officio
member shall serve for three years and may be reappointed.
(2) A member of the Board other than the ex officio
member may resign at any time by letter addressed to the
Minister.
(3) Any member appointed in the place of the member
who has resigned shall serve for the unexpired term of the
member who has resigned but may be reappointed.

Remuneration.

Procedure of Meetings.

### 11.

Members of the Board may be paid such allowance as the
Minister may determine.

### 12.

(1) The Chairman or in his absence the Deputy Chairman shall preside at the meetings of the Board.
(2) The Board may establish its own procedure for
meetings and the conduct of its affairs except that it must meet
at least once in every two months and submit a report of such
meeting to the Minister every six months.

Quorum.

### 13.

The quorum for any meeting of the Board shall be five.

Appointment of General
Manager.

### 14.

The Minister shall appoint a General Manager of the
Corporation on the recommendation of the Board. The General
Manager shall be responsible for the day to day management of
the business of the Corporation.

Functions of the General
Manager.

### 15.

(1) The functions of the General Manager shall be to(a) carry out the business of the Corporation with
commercial efficiency for the benefit of the
Corporation and its customers;
(b) secure most favourable arrangements in
accepting orders for public work;

(c) purchase equipment and other consumable items
on favourable terms;
(d) arrange the storage of consumable items without
incurring waste;
(e) fulfill all work commitments with efficiency and
provide satisfactory service;
(f) maintain inventory of the assets of the
Corporation that may be vested in it in pursuance
of this Act or acquired thereafter; and
(g) any other duties that may be assigned from time
to time by the Board.

### 16.

The Corporation may appoint and employ at such
remuneration and on such terms and conditions such officers as
it deems necessary for the proper carrying out of its functions
under this Act.

### 17.

(1) Subject to the State Lands Act on the appointed date the
Corporation shall become the statutory lessee of the land and
buildings of the public works garage without any further assurance.
(2) The Minister may by affirmative resolution in
Parliament terminate the lease but before terminating the lease
the Minister shall provide alternative accommodation to the
Corporation unless the Corporation is to be wound up.
(3) On the appointed date the plant and equipment,
trucks and vehicles belonging to the public works garage shall be
vested in the Corporation.
(4) The value to be ascribed shall be as approved by the
Director of Audit.
(5) On the appointed date the assets vested in the Corporation under subsection (3) shall be removed from the record of
assets of the Government.

Appointment of officers.

Vesting of Property in the
Corporation.

Funds of the Corporation.

### 18.

The Funds of the Corporation shall consist of –
(a) such money as may accrue to the Corporation from
the carrying out its business activities;
(b) any money which may be borrowed by the
Corporation for the expansion of the business activity of the Corporation from lending institutions in
Dominica, from the Government or other sources
approved by the Government.
(c) money that may be donated for road construction or
maintenance by international agencies; and
(d) money that may be appropriated by Parliament.

Advances.

### 19.

For the purpose of enabling the Corporation to defray
initial expenses and expenditure properly chargeable to capital
account and for providing the Corporation with working capital,
the Minister may in consultation with the Minister for Finance
within five years beginning with the commencement of this Act
make advances to the Corporation specifying conditions as to
interest and repayment.

Temporary investment of
funds.

### 20.

(1) The Corporation may from time to time temporarily
invest with the approval of the Minister for Finance funds not
required to be expended in the meeting of its obligations or in the
performance of its functions.
(2) All interests from such investment shall be paid to the
credit of the Corporation’s account.

Borrowing powers.

### 21.

(1) Subject to this Act the Corporation may from time to
time for the purposes of this Act raise loans(a) from the Government; or
(b) with the consent of the Minister for Finance from
any other source.
(2) The Corporation shall not borrow any money in
excess of five hundred thousand dollars in any financial year
without the written approval of the Minister for Finance.

(3) Where the Corporation applies to the Minister for
Finance for the approval for the taking of a loan the Minister for
Finance shall not grant his approval except with the approval of
Parliament obtained by affirmative resolution.
(4) Without prejudice to section 22 the power of the
Corporation to borrow under this section shall not be exercised
unless a proposal in writing is first submitted through the
Minister for approval of the Minister for Finance showing –
(a) the amount of the proposed loan;
(b) the rate of interest to be paid on the loan;
(c) the source or sources from which the loan is to be
obtained;
(d) the purposes to which the money is to be applied;
and
(e) the manner in which the loan is to be repaid.

### 22.

The Corporation may with the consent of the Minister for
Finance from time to time borrow by way of temporary loan or
overdraft from a bank or otherwise, any sum which it may
temporarily require-

Temporary borrowing.

(a) for the purpose of defraying expenses pending the
receipt of revenues recoverable by it in respect of the
period of account in which those expenses are
chargeable; or
(b) for the purpose of defraying, pending the receipt of
money due in respect of any loan authorized to be
raised under section 21 (1), expenses intended to be
defrayed by any such loan.

### 23.

(1) With the approval of the House of Assembly the
Minister may guarantee, in such manner and on such conditions
as he thinks fit, the payments of the principal and interest of any
authorized borrowing of the Corporation.

Guarantee of borrowing
of Corporation.

(2) Where the Minister for Finance is satisfied that there
has been default in the payment of any principal moneys or
interest guaranteed under this section, he shall direct the repayment out of the Consolidated Fund of the amount in respect of
which there has been such default.
(3) The Corporation shall pay to the Accountant General
at such times and in such manner as the Minister may direct
payments of such amounts as may be so directed in or towards
repayment of any sums issued in fulfillment of any guarantee
given under this section, and payments of interest on what is
outstanding for the time being in respect of any sums so issued at
such rate as the Minister may direct and different rates of interest
may be directed as regards different sums and as regards interests
for different periods.
Reserve Funds.

### 24.

The Corporation shall establish a Reserve Fund which
shall be applied in accordance with any direction which may
from time to time be given to the Corporation by the Minister.

Bank Accounts.

### 25.

(1) All monies accruing to or received by the Corporation
shall be paid into a bank account.
(2) All payments by the Corporation shall be made by
cheque except for petty disbursements not exceeding a sum to be
fixed by the Board.
(3) Cheques drawn against any banking account of the
Corporation shall be signed and countersigned by any two
persons authorized by the Board and notified to the Bank.

Accounts.

### 26.

(1) The Corporation shall keep proper accounts and
other records in respect of its operations and shall cause an annual
statement to be prepared in respect of each financial year.
(2) Prior to the commencement of each financial year,
a budget shall be prepared by the General Manager and
approved by the Board in consultation with the Minister.

### 27.

(1) The Accounts of the Corporation shall be audited
annually by a qualified auditor appointed by the Board.

Audit.

(2) The Auditor appointed by the Board shall comply with
the provisions of the Audit Act 1994 or any that may replace it.

### 28.

(1) Within six months of the end of each financial year
the Corporation shall submit audited accounts and a copy of the
statement of accounts to the Minister together with -

Submission of Accounts.

(a) a report of the activities of the Corporation
during the year;
(b) a report by the Chairman on the state of the
Corporation;
(c) a report by the General Manager on the business
operation of the Corporation for the year.
(2) The Minister shall submit the reports to Parliament
within two months of receipt of the reports.

### 29.

(1) The employees of the public works garage may
exercise the option which is irrevocable within fifteen days of the
appointed date as to whether –

Exercise of option.

(a) to be employed under the Corporation; or
(b) to terminate their employment under the public
works garage.
(2) The employees who exercise their option to be
employed under the Corporation shall be offered employment
under the Corporation under such terms and conditions as may be
determined by the Board.
(3) The employees who exercise the option to terminate
their services under subsection 1(b) shall be paid redundancy
payment as provided for in the Protection of Employment Act.
Chap 89:02.

### 30.

Public works undertaken by the Corporation or work in
progress which remain not completed at the appointed date shall
for all purposes be deemed to be undertaken by the Corporation.

Work in progress.

### 31.

(1) Notwithstanding anything in this Act the employees
and members of the Corporation or members of the Board shall
not engage directly or indirectly in any business transactions with
the Corporation.
