AN ACT TO AMEND THE DOMINICA HOSPITALS
AUTHORITY ACT, 2021 (NO. 8 OF 2021).

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Government of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
DOMINICA HOSPITALS AUTHORITY
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2024.

Short title.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act the Dominica Hospitals Authority Act is
referred to as “the Act”.

Insertion of new
section 55.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section 55 after section 54.

DOMINICA HOSPITALS
AUTHORITY (AMENDMENT)

ACT 13

“Repeal of Roseau 55. The Roseau Hospital Ordinance is hereby
Hospital
repealed.”.
Ordinance.
