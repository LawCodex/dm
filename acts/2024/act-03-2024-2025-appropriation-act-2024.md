# 2024/2025 Appropriation Act 2024

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 3 OF 2024.
I assent

LS
SYLVANIE BURTON
President
8th August, 2024.
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE THIRTIETH DAY OF
JUNE, 2025.
(Gazetted 9th August, 2024.)
BE IT ENACTED BY THE Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the2024/2025 APPROPRIATION ACT 2024.

Short title.

Appropriation.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of one
billion, four hundred eighty-six million, nine hundred and fifty-one
thousand , seven hundred and ninety-three dollars ($1,486,951,793)
is hereby authorised for meeting the expenditure for services for
the year ending June 30, 2025 under the head of expenditure
specified in the Schedule, and the sum specified in the Schedule
opposite to the head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum for
the purposes specified in relation to the head of the estimates for
the said year.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 3

### 3.

The Accountant General is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance, to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the Schedule, the several monies appropriated in the
manner as the said warrant shall direct without further order of
formality.

### 4.

This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st day
of July 2024.
