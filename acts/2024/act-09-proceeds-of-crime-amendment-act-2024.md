# Proceeds of Crime (Amendment) Act 2024

PROCEEDS OF CRIME

ACT 9

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 9 OF 2024
I assent

SYLVANIE BURTON
President

24th December, 2024
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE AMENDMENT OF
THE PROCEEDS OF CRIME ACT, CHAP. 12:29.
(Gazetted 3rd January, 2025)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows1. This Act may be cited as thePROCEEDS OF CRIME (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2024.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap.12:29.

### 2.

In this Act, the Proceeds of Crime Act is referred to as “the
Act”.

Amendment of section 63
A. (1).

### 3.

Section 63A. (1) of the Act is amended by inserting the
word “US$” immediately before the words “ten thousand dollars”.

PROCEEDS OF CRIME

ACT 9

Passed in the House of Assembly this 16th day of December, 2024.
