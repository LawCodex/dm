# Supplimentary Appropriation Act 2024

2024 SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT 4

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 4 OF 2024.
I assent

SYLVANIE BURTON
President
24th December, 2024
AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM
THE CONSOLIDATED FUND OF DOMINICA
IN EXCESS OF THE SUM PROVIDED BY LAW
FOR THE SERVICES OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA DURING THE YEAR
ENDING THE THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE 2024.
(Gazetted 3rd January,2025.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(2023/2024) ACT, 2024.

Short title.

2024 SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT 4

Legislation
Unforeseen.

### 2.

(1)The sum of one hundred and three million, six hundred
and twenty one thousand, nine hundred and twenty-two dollars
and sixty-four cents ($103,621,922.64) as described in the Schedule was withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica
during the financial year 2023/2024 ending on the 30th day of June,
2024\.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen Government expenditure in anticipation of the
granting of an appropriation by the House of Assembly for the
issue of a specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of January, 2024 and appropriated to
the service set out in the Schedule.
