AN ACT TO AMEND THE INCOME TAX ACT,

## CHAPTER 67:01.

(Gazetted 9th October, 2014.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
INCOME TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014.
(2) Sections 4, 5, 6 and 7 shall come into force on the 1st
day of January, 2015.

Short title and
commencement.

Interpretation.
Chap. 67:01.

### 2.

In this Act, “Act” means the Income Tax Act .

or any other period which the Cabinet may prescribe by
Order published in the Gazette.
