### 2.

In this Act the Police Pensions Act is referred to as “the

### 3.

Section 22 of the Act is amended (a) in subsection (2) by deleting the words “sixty years”
and substituting the words “relevant age”;
(b) by inserting the following new subsection (3) –
“(3) For the purposes of this section “relevant age”
means (a) in the case of a person who opts for the
reduced age benefit under section 27A of
Social Security Act, the age at which the
person opts to receive the reduced age benefit;

Chap. 31:01.

(b) in a case other than under paragraph (a), the
age set out in Column 2 of the Schedule
attained within the corresponding applicable
period set out in Column 1 of the Schedule.”.
