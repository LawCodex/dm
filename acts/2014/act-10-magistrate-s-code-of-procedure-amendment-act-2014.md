### 4.

Section 223 of the Act is amended –
(a) by deleting subsection(1) and substituting the following:
“(1) The Chief Justice may make rules for
regulating and prescribing the procedures and practice
to be followed in magistrates’ courts in matters not
provided by this or any other Act relating thereto.
(1A) Without prejudice to the generality of
subsection (1) the Chief Justice may make rules –
(a) respecting the giving of security under this
Act;
(b) prescribing the forms to be used under this
Act or any other Act requiring forms to be
prescribed for the magistrates’ court;
(c) regulating the form of the account to be

rendered by Magistrates or Clerks of the
Court of fines, fees and other sums required
by them and providing for the discontinuance
of any existing account rendered unnecessary

(d) regulating the procedure on appeals by special
case or otherwise under this Act;
(e) respecting any other matter in relation to
which Rules are authorised or required to be
made under or for the purpose of carrying
into effect this Act or the functioning of the
Magistrate’s Court under any other Act.
(1B) Where the Minister gives the Chief
Justice written notice that he thinks it is expedient for
rules made under this section to include provision that
would achieve a purpose specified in the notice the
Chief Justice shall make such rules as he considers
necessary to achieve the specified purpose.
(1C) Rules made under subsection (1B) must
be made within a reasonable period after the Minister
gives notice to the Chief Justice.
(1D) The Chief Justice may nominate a Judge
of the High Court or the Chief Magistrate to
exercise his functions under this section.”;
(b) in subsection (3) by deleting the words “Rules of
