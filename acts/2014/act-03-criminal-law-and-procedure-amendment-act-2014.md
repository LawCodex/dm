AN ACT TO AMEND THE CRIMINAL LAW AND
PROCEDURE ACT, CHAP. 12:01
(Gazetted 3rd April, 2014)
BE IT ENACTED BY the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

Short title.

CRIMINAL LAW AND PROCEDURE
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014.

### 2.

In this Act the Criminal Law and Procedure Act is
referred to as “the Act”.

Interpretation.
Chap. 12:01.

Insertion of section 13A.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
13A immediately after section 13 “Controlled
delivery.

CRIMINAL LAW AND PROCEDURE
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 3

13A. (1) For the purpose of gathering evidence
to identify a person involved in the commission of an
offence or to facilitate a prosecution for an offence,
the minister may, by Regulations, provide for (a)

money or property that authorised
officers reasonably suspect has been, is
being or may be used to commit an
offence under the Act, to enter, leave or
move through Dominica; and

(b)

the protection of authorised officers from
criminal and civil liability for acts
performed under paragraph (a) in good
faith in the excercise of their duties.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of
subsection (1) (a), an authorised officer shall not
exercise the power referred to in that subsection
unless the authorised officer has reasonable grounds
to believe that a person has committed, is committing
or is about to commit an offence.
(3) An authorised officer does not commit an
offence if (a)

the authorised officer is engaged in
investigation of a suspected offence;
and

(b)

the offence involves money or property
that the authorised officer reasonably
suspects has been, is being or may be
used to commit an offence.

CRIMINAL LAW AND PROCEDURE
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 3

(4) In this section, “authorised officer” means (a)

the Commissioner of Police;

(b)

the Comptroller of Customs;

(c)

a police officer authorised by the
Commissioner of Police for a provision
of this section;

(d)

a customs officer authorised by the
Comptroller of Customs for a provision
of this section; or

(e)

a person authorised, in writing, by the
Minister for a provision of this section.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 19th day of March, 2014.
