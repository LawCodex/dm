AN ACT TO AMEND THE CUSTOMS ACT 2010 (NO.
20 OF 2010).
(Gazetted 9th October, 2014.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the CUSTOMS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014.

Short title.

Interpretation.
No. 20 of 2010.
Amendment of section 9
of the Act.

CUSTOMS
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 9

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Customs Act.

### 3.

Section 9 of the Act is amended by deleting subsection (4)
and substituting the following:
“(4) Nothing in subsection (2) prevents the disclosure
of any document or information where the disclosure is
authorised by the Comptroller (a) to the Comptroller of Inland Revenue Division for
use by him and officers of the Inland Revenue
Division (i) in the course of and for the purpose of carrying
out their duties under the Income Tax Act or
any other tax enactment; and
(ii) in accordance with a Memorandum of
Understanding with the Inland Revenue
Division; or
(b) in accordance with subsection (5).”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 22nd day of September, 2014.
