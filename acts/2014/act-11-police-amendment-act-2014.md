BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
POLICE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 14:01.

Amendment of the Act.

POLICE (AMENDMENT)

ACT 11

### 2.

In this Act the Police Act is referred to as “the Act”.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
7A immediately after section 7 –
“Retiring age
of police
officers.

7A. (1) A police officer shall vacate his office
when he attains the age of sixty years.
(2) Despite subsection (1) a police officer who
attains the age of sixty years may be re-employed
beyond the age of sixty years but in any event the
officer shall not be re-employed beyond the age of
sixty-five years.”.
