SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION

ACT 19

### 2.

(1) The sum of thirty-eight million, five hundred and
ninety-five thousand, three hundred and thirty-two dollars and
eighty-five cents ($38, 595,332.85) as described in the Schedule
was withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during
the financial year ending on the 30th day of June, 2015.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen government expenditure in anticipation of a
grant being made by the House of Assembly authorising the issue
of the specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of July, 2014 and appropriated to the
services set out in the Schedule.
