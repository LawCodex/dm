AN ACT RESPECTING THE IMPOSITION OF A LEVY
IN RESPECT OF VILLAS IN APPROVED VILLA

(Gazetted 27th November, 2015.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
RESIDENTIAL LEVY ACT, 2015.

Short title.

Interpretation.

RESIDENTIAL LEVY

ACT 15

### 2.

(1) In this Act –
“approved villa development” means a tourism real estate
development which (a) contains at least two villas;
(b) has an investment value of not less than three million
dollars; and
(c) has been approved by Cabinet;
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Inland Revenue;
“levy” means the levy imposed by section 5;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Finance;
“villa” means a holiday dwelling which consists of at least one
bedroom.

Act No. 7 of 2005.

(2) Except as otherwise expressly provided expressions
used in this Act have the same meanings as in the Value Added Tax
Act.

Application.

### 3.

The levy imposed by this Act is payable in respect of each
villa in an approved villa development.

Administration.

### 4.

(1) The Comptroller is responsible for
administration of this Act.

(2) Any function conferred by this Act on the Comptroller
is exercisable by any public officer authorised by the Comptroller
for the purpose.
Imposition of levy.

### 5.

(1) Subject to subsection (2) the owner of villa shall be
charged for each calender year from the date of completion of a

villa in an approved villa development a levy of two thousand
dollars per calendar year.
(2) The owner of a villa referred to in subsection (1) shall
pay the levy by the 31st December of the calendar year of
completion of the villa.

### 6.

Notwithstanding any thing in the Income Tax Act, the levy
paid shall not be deducted in arriving at the income tax payable
under the Income Tax Act.

The Collection of Taxes Act and the Value Added Tax
