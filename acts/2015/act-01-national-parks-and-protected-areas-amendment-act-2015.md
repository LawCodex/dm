AN ACT TO AMEND THE NATIONAL PARKS AND
PROTECTED AREAS ACT, CHAP.42:02.
(Gazetted 19th March, 2015.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the NATIONAL PARKS AND PROTECTED AREAS
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015.

Short title.

Interpretaion.
Ch.42:02.

### 2.

In this Act, the National Parks and Protected Areas Act
is referred to as “the Act”.

Repeal and replacement
of section 4.

NATIONAL PARKS AND
ACT 1
PROTECTED AREAS (AMENDMENT)

### 3.

Section 4 of the Act is repealed and replaced as follows:
“National
parks.

### 4.

(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), the lands
described in the Schedule constitute the national parks of
Dominica.

Power to
amend
Schedule.

(2) The President may by Order amend the Schedule (a) by adding to any park, any State lands and any
lands leased to the State, by altering the
description of the park therein to include any
lands, added to a park pursuant to this
paragraph, and any lands so added may be
contiguous to or separate from the park;
(b) by altering the description of the Cabrits
National Park by varying the boundaries of
the park; or
(c) by including therein a description of any area
designated as a national park.

Leased
land
excluded
upon
expiry of
lease.

(3) Upon expiry of the term of any lease to the State
of any lands added to a park pursuant to subsection (2)(a),
such lands shall cease to be part of the park and the
President may thereupon by Order amend the Schedule
by altering the description of the park therein to exclude
such lands.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 20th day of Februar, 2015.
