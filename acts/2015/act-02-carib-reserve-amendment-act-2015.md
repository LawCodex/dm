AN ACT TO AMEND THE CARIB RESERVE ACT

## CHAPTER 25:90.

(Gazetted 19th March, 2015.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

Short title.

CARIB RESERVE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015.

### 2.

In this Act the Carib Reserve Act is referred to as “the
Act.”

Interpretation.
Chap 25:90.

Repeal and replacement
of section 1.

### 3.

Section 1 of the Act is repealed and replaced by the
following:
“Short title.

CARIB RESERVE
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 2

1\. (1) This Act may be cited as the KALINAGO TERRITORY ACT.”
(2) For the avoidance of doubt this Act, formerly
referred to as the Carib Reserve Act shall be referred
to as the Kalinago Territory Act.

Amendment of the Act.

### 4.

The Act is amended by deleting the words (a) “carib” in the long title and wherever it appears
and substituting the word “kalinago”; and
(b) “Reserve” wherever it appears and substituting
the word “Territory”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 20th day of February, 2015.

VERNANDA RAYMOND (MRS.)
