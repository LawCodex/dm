### 5.

Section 26 of the Act is amended in subsection (3) by
deleting paragraph (c) and substituting the following:
“(c) in respect of a supply or import received which
is used for the making of both taxable and exempt
supplies, the amount calculated according to the
following formula -AXB/C
where A is a total amount of input tax payable in respect
of supplies and imports received during the period,
less the sum of the input tax accounted for under
paragraphs (a) and (b) of this subsection and the
input tax disallowed under subsection (2);
B is equal to the total value of taxable supplies
made by the taxable person during the period;
C is equal to the total value of all supplies made by
the taxable person during the period.”.
