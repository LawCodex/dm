AN ACT TO AMEND THE EXEMPT INSURANCE
ACT, 1997 (NO. 14 OF 1997).

(Gazetted 30th July, 2015)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the EXEMPT INSURANCE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015.

Short title.

EXEMPT INSURANCE
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 7

Interpretation.
Act No. 14 of 1997.

### 2.

In this Act the Exempt Insurance Act is referred to as “the
Act”.

Repeal and replacement
of section 41 of the Act.

### 3.

Section 41 of the Act is repealed and replaced by the
following:
“41. A person shall not disclose any information
relating to any application of a prospective licensee or
to the affairs of a licensee other than information that
forms part of the public record in the possession of the
Registrar of Companies except (a)

for the purpose of the performance of
his function under this Act or the Income
Tax Act;

(b)

when lawfully required to do so by
an order of a court of competent
jurisdiction; or

(c)

under the provisions of any law of
Dominica.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 28th day of July 2015.

EUNIE JOHN (MRS.)
