# International Exempt Trust (Amendment) Act, 2015

INTERNATIONAL EXEMPT TRUST
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 8

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 8 OF 2015.
I assent

LS
CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President
30th July, 2015
AN ACT TO AMEND THE INTERNATIONAL
EXEMPT TRUST ACT, 1997 (NO. 10 OF 1997).

(Gazetted 30th July, 2015.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

Short title.

INTERNATIONAL EXEMPT TRUST
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015

### 2.

In this Act the International Exempt Trust Act 1997 is
referred to as “the Act”.

Interpretation.
No. 10 of 1997.

Repeal and replacement
of section 39 of the Act.

### 3.

Section 39 of the Act is repealed and replaced by the
following:

INTERNATIONAL EXEMPT TRUST
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 8

“39. The register shall not be open for inspection
except when (a)

a trustee of a trust in writing authorises
a person to inspect the entry of that
trust on the register;

(b)

the inspection is authorised under the
provisions of a law of Dominica.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 28th day of July, 2015.

EUNIE JOHN (MRS.)
