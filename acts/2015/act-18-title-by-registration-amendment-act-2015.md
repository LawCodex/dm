27th November, 2015
AN ACT TO AMEND THE TITLE BY REGISTRATION
ACT (CHAP 56:50).
(Gazetted 27th November , 2015.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
TITLE BY REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT) ACT 2015.

Short Title.

Interpretation.
Ch. 56:50.

### 2.

In this Act the Title by Registration Act is referred to as
“the Act”.

Amendment of
section 2.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
new definition immediately after the definition of “Form” –

TITLE BY REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 18

““new qualifying project” means a new real estate
development or other new development project
with a minimum investment of three million
dollars that has been approved by Cabinet as a
qualified project for tax reliefs and in relation to
which the developer has a valid licence under
the Hotels Aid Act, Chap. 85:04 or the Fiscal
Incentives Act, Chap. 84:51;”.
Amendment of the
Fourth Schedule to the
Act.

### 4.

The Fourth Schedule of the Act is amended by –
(a) adding the following as paragraph three of the
schedule –
“On every Memorandum of Transfer of land from (a) an individual to a company
where the individual owns
at least 25% of the shares
of that company; and
(b) a first company to a second
company where the first
company owns at least 25%
of the shares of the second
company,
a judicial fee of

1% of the value
of the land;”;

(b) adding the following as paragraph four of the schedule –

TITLE BY REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 18

“On every Memorandum of Transfer of land for
a new qualifying project,
a judicial fee of

1% of the value
of the land;”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 26th day of November, 2015.
