### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
OFFSHORE BANKING
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2019.

Short title.

Interpretation.
No. 8 of 1996.

### 2.

In this Act the Offshore Banking Act is referred to as the
“Act”.

Amendment of
section 57.

### 3.

Section 57 of the Act is amended in –
(a) subsection (1) by deleting the word “No” and substituting the words “Subject to section 59A, no”;
(b) subsection (2) by deleting the word “No” and substituting the words “Subject to section 59A, no”;
(c) subsection (3) by deleting the word “No” and substituting the words “Subject to section 59A, no”.

Amendment of
section 58.

### 4.

Section 58 of the Act is amended in (a) subsection (1) by deleting the word “No” and substituting the words “Subject to section 59A, no”;
(b) subsection (2) deleting the words “is exempt” and
substituting the words “is, subject to section 59A,
exempt”;
(c) subsection (3) by deleting the word “No” and substituting the words “Subject to section 59A, no”.

Amendment of
section 59.

### 5.

Section 59 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
new subsection (3):
“(3) This section is subject to section 59A.”.

Amendment of the Act.

### 6.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
59A after section 59:
“Limits on
exemptions.

59A. Sections 57, 58 and 59(1) apply only to a
company who was licensed on or before the 31
December, 2018 until -

(a) the date on which the licence expires; or
(b) 31st December, 2021,
whichever is earlier.”.

### 7.

The Act is amended by repealing sections 60, 61, and 62.

Amendment of the Act.
