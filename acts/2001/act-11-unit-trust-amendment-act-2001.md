# Unit Trust (Amendment) Act 2001

VERNON L. SHAW
President.

AN ACT TO AMEND THE UNIT TRUST ACT, 1999 (ACT
NO.1 OF 1999).

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the UNIT TRUST (AMENDMENT)

ACT 2001.

### 2.

In this Act, the Unit Trust Act, 1999 is referred to as "the
Act".
Insertion of sections
5A, 58 and 5C of
Act.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting immediately after section
5 the following new sections as section 5A, 5B and 5C "Licensing
requirements.

SA. (1) A person shall not apply
under section 5 for registration of a unit trust
scheme unless he is licensed in accordance
with the provisions of this Act.
(2) An application for a licence
to register a unit trust scheme shall be made
to the Superintendent in such form as he may
prescribe and shall be accompanied by the
following:
(a) a certified copy of the

memorandum and articles of association or
any other instrument
under which the applicant is organised;
(b) the address of the app licant's head office;
(c) the names and addresses
of the members of the
Board of Directors of
the applicant;
(d) a statement of the name,
address, qualifications
and experience of the
applicant's Chief Executive Officer;
( e) the location of the prin-

cipal and other places of
business where the ap-

plicant proposes to do
business;
(I) such financial data as
the Superintendent may
require; and
(g) such additional informa-

tion as the Superintendent may require.
Grant or
refusal of
licence.

### 5B.

(l) When the Superintendent is
satisfied that it is proper to do so, he may
grant or refuse a licence under this Act.
(2) In granting a licence under
this Act, the Superintendent shall have regard to(a) the validity of documents

submitted;
(b) the financial

standing
and reputation of the applicant;

(c) the character and experience of the shareholders, directors and senior
managers of the applicant;
(d) the adequacy

of the
capital structure of the
applicant; and

(e) any other matters as he

may determine from time
to time.
(3) It is a condition of a licence
granted under this Act that the license shall
not-

(a) without prior notice in

writing to the Superintendent, add to or change
its shareholders; or
(b) without prior approval

in writing from the Superintendent, add to or
change its directors or
senior managers.
Persons
debarred from
management.

### 5C.

(1) Any person (a) who has been a director

of, or directly concerned
in the management of a
financial
institution
which has had its licence
revoked;
(b) who has been sentenced

by a court in any country to a term of imprisonment for an offence
involving dishonesty; or
(c) who has been or is or

becomes bankrupt, suspends payment to or
compounds with his
creditors,
shall not without the express authorisation of the
Superintendent be a shareholder, director, manager,
unit trust representative or other employee of any unit
trust scheme.
(2) A person who contravenes the provisions of subsection (1) commits an offence and is
liable on summary conviction to a fine of twenty- five
thousand dollars.".

4\. The Act is amended by inserting the following as Part
VII"PART VII
CAPITAL AND RESERVE
Minimum capital
of a unit trust
not
scheme.

41\. (1) A unit trust scheme shall

be registered unless on commencing
business its capital issued and paid up in cash
and not repaid is not less than five million
Eastern Caribbean dollars or its equivalent in
other convertible currency and documentary evidence is shown on commencement
of business to the satisfaction of the Superintendent that such sum, as the case may be,
is freely available in liquid form.

(2) Every registered unit trust
scheme shall maintain its affairs in such a
way that the total of its paid-up capital
together with its reserves of accumulated
profits less accumulated losses amount to
not less than three million Eastern Caribbean
dollars or its equivalent in other convertible
currency.
(3) The Superintendent may
give directives to any registered unit trust
scheme whose capital resources do not
comply with the requirements of this section
to comply with the requirements within such
time as may be specified in the directives.
(4) When deemed appropriate
by the Superintendent a registered unit trust
scheme may be required to increase its fully
paid-up capital to such greater sum as is
considered necessary having regard to the
nature of that unit trust scheme.

Insertion of Part VII
of the Act.

Maintenance
of reserve fund.

42\. (1) Subject to subsection (2),
every registered unit trust scheme shall after
its first year of operation maintain a reserve
fund and shall, out of its net profits each year
and before any dividend is declared transfer
to that fund a sum equal to not less than
twenty-five percent of such profits wherever the amount of the reserved fund is less
than the issued paid-up capital of the scheme.
(2) Subsection (1) shall not apply to any registered unit trust scheme with
respect to which it is proved to the satisfaction of the Superintendent that the aggregate
paid-up capital and reserves of the institution
are adequate in respect of its business.".
