# Edward Oliver Leblanc (Pension) Act 2001

EDWARD

OLIVER LEBLANC
(PENSION)

VERNON L. SHAW
President

AN ACT TO AUTHORISE
THE PAYMENT OF AN
AMOUNT BY WAY OF PENSION TO EDWARD
OLIVER LEBLANC FOR HIS SERVICES
TO
THE COMMONWEALTH
OF DOMINICA

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

EDWARD OLIVER LEBLANC
(PENSION)
Authority to pay
pension to Edward
Oliver Leblanc.

### 2.

Notwithstanding anything contained in any law relating
to the granting of pensions or gratuities, on the coming into force
of this Act there shall be payable out of the Consolidated Fund
(a) a monthly pension of two thousand five hundred

dollars to Edward Oliver Leblanc in lieu of any
pension due to him on the date of the passing of this
Act, in respect of his services to the Commonwealth
of Dominica; and
(b) after his death, a sum of one thousand dollars a month

to his wife, if she survives him.
Pensions not assignable
or attachable.

### 3.

The pension granted under this Act shall not be assignable
or transferable, or liable to be attached, sequestered, or levied
upon, for or in respect of any debt or claim whatsoever other than
a debt due to the Government.

### 4.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into operation on
the 1st day of April, 2001.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
