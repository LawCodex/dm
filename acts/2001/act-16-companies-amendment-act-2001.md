# Companies (Amendment) Act, 2001

VERNON L. SHAW
President
19th September, 2001
AN ACT TO AMEND THE COMPANIES ACT (NO. 21 OF
1994).

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 2.

In this Act the Companies Act, 1994 is referred to as "the
Act",

Interpretation.
Act No. 21 of 1994.

Insertion of section 9A of
the Act.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section immediately after section 9:
"Annual
fee.

9A. A company whose name is on the Register of Companies shall pay the prescribed annual company fee on or before the 2nd day of
April of each year after its incorporation."

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
