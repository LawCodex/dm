# 2001/2002 Appropriation Act 2001

VERNON L. SHAW
President

2Sth July, 2001
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH
OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING THE THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE,
2002\.

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the 2001/2002

APPROPRIATION

ACT 2001.

### 2.

(1) The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of
three hundred and forty-five million, four hundred and fifty seven
thousand, nine hundred and seventy-four dollars ($345,457,974) is
hereby authorised for meeting expenditure for services for the
year ending 30th June, 2002 under the Schedule hereto, and the
sum specified in the said Schedule appointed out of the said sum
of three hundred and forty-five million, four hundred and fifty
seven thousand, nine hundred and seventy-four
dollars
($345,457,974) for the purposes specified in relation to the Head
in the Estimates for the said year.
Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and required from time to time upon Warrant of the Minister for Finance,
to payout of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several
services specified in the Schedule, the several monies appropriated in such manner as the said Warrant shall direct without
further order or formality.

### 4.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the
1st day of July, 2001.

(Sections 2 & 3).
