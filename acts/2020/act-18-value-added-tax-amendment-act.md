AN ACT TO AMEND VALUE ADDED TAX ACT, 2005
(No. 7 of 2005).
(Gazetted 24th December, 2020.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
VALUE ADDED TAX
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020.

Short title and
commencement.

VALUE ADDED TAX
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 18

(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st
day of August, 2020.
Interpretation. No. 7 of
2005\.

### 2.

In this Act the Value Added Tax Act is referred to as
“the Act”.

Amendment of section 28

### 3.

Section 28 of the Act is amended by inserting the
following new subsections (5) and (6) after subsection (4):
“(5)Notwithstanding subsection (1) and
subject to subsection (6), interest due on tax that
became due and payable in respect of periods before
2019 is waived if all the principal taxes owed are paid
between the 1st day of August, 2020 and the 31st day
of January, 2021.
(6) Subsection (5) does not apply to any
interest paid before the 1st day of August, 2020.”

Amendment of section
41\.

### 4.

Section 41 of the Act is amended by inserting the
following new subsections (6) and (7) after subsection (5):
“(6)Notwithstanding subsection (4) and
subject to subsection (7), any penalty payable in
respect of tax that became due and payable in
respect of periods before 2019 is waived if all the
principal taxes owed are paid between the 1st day of
August, 2020 and the 31st day of January, 2021.

VALUE ADDED TAX
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 18

(7) Subsection (6) does not apply to any
penalty paid before the 1st day of August, 2020.”

Passed in the House of Assembly this 16th day of December, 2020.
