The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section 8A immediately after section 8:
“Temporary
reduction in
stamp duty.

8A. Notwithstanding section 8(1), from
the 1st day of September, 2020, to the 31st day of
October, 2021, there shall be charged on the
conveyance or transfer (a)

(b)

on sale of any property (except stock
and debentures),
(i)

a stamp duty of 4.5% on the
value of the real property
transferred of which 2.5% shall
be paid by the transferor and
2% by the transferee;

(ii)

a stamp duty of 6.5% on the
value of the personal property
transferred of which 2.5% shall
be paid by the transferor and
4% by the transferee;

operating as a voluntary disposition
inter vivos, a stamp duty of 4.5% of
the value of the property so conveyed
of which 2.5% shall be paid by the
transferor and 2% by the transferee
except that where the transfer is
between parent and child a stamp
duty of 2% of the value of the property
