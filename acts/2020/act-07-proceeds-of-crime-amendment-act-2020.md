AN ACT TO AMEND THE PROCEEDS OF CRIME
ACT, CHAP. 12:29.

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:
(Gazetted 20th February, 2020.)

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
PROCEEDS OF CRIME
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020.

Short title.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, the Proceeds of Crime Act is referred to as
“the Act”.

Amendment of
section 2.

PROCEEDS OF CRIME
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 7

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
definition in appropriate alphabetical order:
““Authority” means the Authority established under section 7(1)
or (2) of the Money Laundering (Prevention) Act, 2011;”.

Amendment of
section 60.

### 4.

Section 60 of the Act is amended –
(a) in subsection (2), by inserting the words “the Banking
Supervisory Authority and” immediately after the
words “consult with”;
(b) in subsection (3) –
(i) in paragraphs (a) and (b) by deleting the word
“Unit” and substituting the word “Authority”;
(ii) in paragraph (c) by deleting the word “Unit”
where it first occurs and substituting the word
“Authority”;
(c) in subsection (8) by deleting the word “Unit” and
substituting the word “Authority”;
(d) by repealing subsection (9) and replacing it with the
following:
“(9) An administrative penalty collected by (a) the Unit by virtue of subsection (8) shall be
paid into the Asset Forfeiture Fund established under section 36 (1) of the Money
Laundering (Prevention) Act 2011; and

(b) the Central Bank by virtue of subsection (8),
shall be retained for use by the Central Bank
for the purposes of its functions.”;
(e) by inserting the following new subsection (10) immediately after subsection (9):
“(10) In this section “Banking Supervisory
Authority” means the Money Laundering Supervisory
Authority established under section 7(2) of the Money
Laundering (Prevention) Act, 2011.”.
