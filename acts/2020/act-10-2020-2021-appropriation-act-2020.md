# 2020-2021 Appropriation Act 2020

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 10 OF 2020.

I assent

LS

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President
6thAugust, 2020
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE THIRTIETH DAY OF
JUNE, 2021.
(Gazetted 6th August, 2020.)
BE IT ENACTED BY THE Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the2020/2021 APPROPRIATION ACT 2020.

Short title.

Appropriation.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of nine
hundred and forty two million, one hundred and seventy nine
thousand, nine hundred and forty dollars ($942,179,940) is hereby
authorised for meeting the expenditure for services for the year
ending June 30, 2021 under the head of expenditure specified in
the Schedule, and the sum specified in the Schedule opposite to
the head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum for the
purposes specified in relation to the head of the estimates for the
said year.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 10

### 3.

The Accountant General is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance, to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the Schedule, the several monies appropriated in the
manner as the said warrant shall direct without further order of
formality.

### 4.

This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st day
of July 2020.
