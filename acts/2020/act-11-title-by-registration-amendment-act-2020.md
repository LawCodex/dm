AN ACT TO AMEND THE TITLE BY REGISTRATION
ACT, CHAP. 56:50.
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:
(Gazetted 30th October, 2020).

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
TITLE BY REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020.

Short title,
commencement and
duration.

TITLE BY REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 11

(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st
day of September, 2020 and expires on the 31st day of October,
2021\.
Interpretation.
Chap. 56:50.

### 2.

In this Act the Title by Registration Act is referred to
as “the Act”.

Insertion of a new
section 163A.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section 163A immediately after section 163:
“Temporary
reduction in
solicitor’s fee.

Insertion of a new
section 166A.

163A. Notwithstanding section 163, from the 1st
day of November, 2020 to the 31st day of October,
2021, for a first certificate of title and transfer
subsequent to a first certificate of title, a solicitor shall
charge a fee not exceeding 2.5% of the sale value of
the property.”.

### 4.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section 166A immediately after section 166:
“Temporary
reduction in
judicial fee.

166A. Notwithstanding section 166, from the 1st
day of September, 2020 to the 31st day of October,
2021 the judicial fee payable on every memorandum
of transfer of land shall be 1% of the value of the
land.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 26th day of October, 2020.
DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly
