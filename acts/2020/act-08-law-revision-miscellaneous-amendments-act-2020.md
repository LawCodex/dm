# Law revision (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2020

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 8 OF 2020.
I assent

CHARLES A. SAVARIN
President
19th February, 2020
AN

ACT
TO
AMEND
MISCELLANEOUS
ENACTMENTS AND TO MAKE OTHER
MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS FOR THE
PURPOSES OF THE REVISION OF THE LAWS
OF DOMINICA.
(Gazetted 20th February, 2020.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the —
LAW REVISION (MISCELLANEOUS
AMENDMENTS) ACT 2020,
and shall come into operation on such a day as the Minister may
by Order appoint.

Short title and
commencement.

Amendments.
Schedule.

### 2.

(1) The Chapter numbers specified in the first column of
the Schedule are the new or corresponding chapter numbers of the
written laws revised by the Law Revision Commission on the
revision date by virtue of their powers under the Law Revision
Act.
(2) The Laws specified in the second column of the
Schedule are hereby amended in the manner specified in the third
column of that Schedule.

Chap. 3:02.

LAW REVISION
ACT 8
(MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS)

(3) The provisions of the statutory instruments amended
in the Schedule notwithstanding such amendments by Act of
Parliament, may be hereafter amended or revoked in the same
manner and by the same authority as immediately before such
amendment.
Acts done in relation to
diplomatic or consular
officers.

### 3.

Where any law of Dominica enacted before the
commencement of the Commonwealth of Dominica Constitution
Order required or authorised any act or thing to be done by, before,
or in relation to, a diplomatic agent or consular officer of the United
Kingdom, then any such act or thing done or purported to be done
under and in accordance with that law at any time between the
commencement of the said Order in Council and the coming into
operation of this section shall be deemed to be and always to have
been fully valid and effectual for all purposes if done by, before,
or in relation to, a diplomatic agent or consular officer either of the
United Kingdom or of Dominica.

Inclusion of
Amendments in the
2017 Revised Edition.

### 4.

(1) The amendments specified in the Schedule shall be
incorporated in the 2017 Revised Edition of the written laws of
Dominica.
(2) In this section the expression “the 2017 Revised
Edition” means the written laws of Dominica revised under the
Law Revision Act up to 1st January 2017.
