AN ACT TO AMEND THE EASTERN CARIBBEAN
CENTRAL BANK ACT, CHAPTER 74:01.
(Gazetted 30th October, 2020.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the EASTERN CARIBBEAN CENTRAL BANK
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 74:01.

### 2.

In this Act, the Eastern Caribbean Central Bank Act
is referred to as “the Act”.

Insertion of a new
section 4A.

EASTERN CARIBBEAN
CENTRAL BANK (AMENDMENT)

ACT 15

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section 4A immediately after section 4 “Inconsistency
with other
legislation.

### 4A.

In the event of any inconsistency between
the provisions of this Act and the operation of any
other enactment, the provisions of this Act shall
prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 26th day of October, 2020.
DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
