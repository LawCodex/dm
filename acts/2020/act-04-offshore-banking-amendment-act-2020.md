AN ACT TO AMEND THE OFFSHORE BANKING
ACT, 1996 (No. 8 of 1996).
(Gazetted 20th February, 2020.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
OFFSHORE BANKING
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020.

Short title.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act the Offshore Banking Act is referred to as the
“Act”.

Repeal and replacement
of section 59A.

OFFSHORE BANKING
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 4

### 3.

Section 59A of the Act is repealed and replaced as
follows:
“Limits on
exemptions.

59A. (1) Sections 57, 58 and 59(1) apply only to
a company who was licensed on or before the 31st
day of December, 2018 until –
(a) the date on which the licence expires; or
(b) 30th June, 2021,
whichever is earlier.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), sections
57, 58 and 59(1) do not apply to –
(a) intellectual property assets acquired or
newly created by a company referred to
in subsection (1) before the 31st day of
December, 2018; and
(b) income derived from –
(i) the intellectual property assets referred to in paragraph (a);
(ii) specific assets acquired by a company referred to in subsection (1)
before the 31st day of December, 2018;
(iii) specific projects commenced by a
company referred to in subsection (1)
before the 31st day of December, 2018.

OFFSHORE BANKING
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 4

(3) In this section “intellectual property
asset” means an intellectual property right including
a copyright, design right, patent and trademark and
includes technical know-how, service marks, brand
or image rights, performers rights.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 11th day of February, 2020.

DANIEL JAMES
