AN ACT TO AMEND EXCISE TAX ACT, 2005 (No. 8 of
2005).
(Gazetted 24th December, 2020.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
EXCISE TAX
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020.

Short title and
commencement.

EXCISE TAX
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 19

(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st
day of August, 2020.

### 2.

In this Act the Excise Tax Act is referred to as “the
Act”.

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new
section 16A after section 16:

Interpretation.
No. 8 of 2005.

Insertion of new section
16A.

“16A. (1) Notwithstanding section 16 and subject
to subsection (2), interest due on tax that became due
and payable in respect of periods before 2019 is
waived if all the principal taxes owed are paid
between the 1st day of August, 2020 and the 31st day
of January, 2021.
(2) Subsection (1), does not apply to any
interest paid before the 1st day of August, 2020.”.

### 4.

Section 17 of the Act is amended by inserting the
following new subsections (7) and (8) after subsection (6):
“(7) Notwithstanding subsection (5) and
subject to subsection (8), any penalty payable in
respect of tax that became due and payable in
respect of periods before 2019 is waived if all the
principal taxes owed are paid between the 1st day of
August, 2020 and the 31st day of Janaury, 2021.

Amendment of section
17\.

EXCISE TAX
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 19

(8) Subsection (7) does not apply to any
interest paid before the 1st day of August, 2020.”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 16th day of December, 2020.
