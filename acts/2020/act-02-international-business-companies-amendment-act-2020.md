### 2.

In this Act the International Business Companies Act is
referred to as the “Act”.

### 3.

Section 109 of the Act is amended (a) in subsection (1) by deleting the words “subsection
(1A)” and substituting the words “subsections (1A)
and (1B)”;
(b) by inserting the following new subsection (1B) immediately after subsection (1A);
“(1B) Subsection (1) does not apply to (a) intellectual property assets acquired or newly
created by a company referred to in subsection (1) before the 31st day of December,
2018; and
(b) income derived from (i) the intellectual property assets referred to
in paragraph (a);
(ii) specific assets acquired by a company
referred to in subsection (1) before the
31st day of December, 2018;
(iii) specific projects commenced by a company referred to in subsection (1) before
the 31st day of December, 2018.”;
(c) by inserting the following new subsection (4)
after subsection (3):
“(4) In this section “intellectual property asset”
means an intellectual property right including a
