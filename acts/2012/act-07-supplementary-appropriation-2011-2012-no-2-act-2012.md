AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM
THE CONSOLIDATED FUND OF DOMINICA
IN EXCESS OF THE SUM PROVIDED BY LAW
FOR THE SERVICES OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA DURING THE YEAR
ENDING THE THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE 2012.
(Gazetted 10th January, 2013.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(2011/2012) (NO.2) ACT 2012.

Short title.

Legalisation of
unforeseen expenditure.
Schedule.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION

ACT 7

### 2.

(1) The sum of ninety three million, thirty two thousand,
three hundred and ninety six dollars and thirty two cents
($93,032,396.32), as described in the Schedule was withdrawn
from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during the financial year
ending on the 30th day of June, 2012.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen government expenditure in anticipation of a
grant being made by the House of Assembly authorising the issue
of the specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of July, 2011 and appropriated to the
services set out in the Schedule.

(Section 2 and 3).
