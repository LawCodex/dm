AN ACT TO AMEND THE MARRIAGE ACT, CHAPTER
35:01.
(Gazetted 15th March, 2012)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
MARRIAGE (AMENDMENT) ACT 2012.

Short title.

### 2.

In this Act the Marriage Act is referred to as “the Act”.

Interpretation.

## Chapter 35:01.

Repeal and replacement
of section 39.

MARRIAGE
(AMENDMENT)

### 3.

The Act is amended by repealing section 39 and replacing
it with the following –

of

39\. (1)The Minister may, subject to the restriction in this
Act, and on payment of the application fee specified in
Schedule 2, grant a licence to marry without publication
banns or notice of marriage under this Act.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), where either
of the applicants for a marriage licence has not had his
usual place of abode in Dominica in accordance with
section 42(e) the Minister may grant a licence to marry
without publication of banns or notice of marriage on
payment of the application fee and the additional fee
specified in Schedule 2.

(3) Where applicants under subsection (2) are
desirious of having their application processed on
a
weekend or public holiday the application may be
processed
on a weekend or public holiday on payment
of the
weekend and public holiday processing fee specified
in
Schedule 2.
Amendment of
section 42.

### 4.

Section 42 of the Act is amended –
(a) in subsection (2)(a) by inserting the word “ages,”
immediately before the word “rank”;
(b) by deleting subsection (3) and substituting the
following “(3) The petition must be –
(a)

in Form G set out in Schedule 1;

(b) signed by both applicants;
(c) accompanied by the relevant documents
specified in Schedule 3 and any other
evidence of the statements made in the
petition that may be prescribed.”

### 5.

Section 54 of the Act is amended in subsection (2) by
deleting the words “one hundred dollars” and substituting the
words “ three hundred dollars”.

54\.

### 7.

The Act is amended by deleting the words “in the

43, 46, 48, 49, 56, 57, 58 and 62 and substituting the words “in
Schedule 1”.

## SCHEDULE 3

DOCUMENTS TO ACCOMPANY APPLICATION
1\. Passport
2\. Birth certificate
3\. Non-marriage certificate or a statutory declaration
attesting to the marriage status of the applicants
4\. Decree Absolute if one of the parties is divorced
5\. Death certificate if first spouse deceased
6\. Deed Poll if name changed
7\. Evidence of consent if one of the applicants is below
18
8\. Authenticated translation of any required document
which is not in English
9\. A notarised copy of items 3 to 7 excluding the
statutory declaration required at item 3”.
