AN ACT TO AMEND THE PENSIONS ACT,

## CHAPTER 23:80

(Gazetted 31st January, 2013).

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
PENSIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2012.

Short title and
commencement.

PENSIONS (AMENDMENT)

ACT 10

(2) This Act is deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of March, 2012.
Interpretation.
Chap. 23:80.

### 2.

In this Act the Pensions Act is referred to as “the Act”.

Amendment of
section 22

### 3.

Section 22 of the Act is amended (a) in paragraph (b) of section (1) by deleting the words “sixty years” and
substituting the words “relevant age”;
(b) in subsection (2) by deleting the words “sixty years” and substituting the
words “relevant age”.
(c) by inserting the following new subsection (3) –
“(3) For this section “relevant age” means -

Chap. 31:01.

(a) in the case of a person who opts for the reduced age benefit under
section 27A of Social Security Act, the age at which the person
opts to receive the reduced age benefit;
(b) in a case other than under paragraph (a), the age set out in Column
2 of the Schedule attained within the corresponding applicable
period set out in Column 1 of that Schedule.”.
