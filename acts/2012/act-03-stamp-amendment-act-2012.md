AN ACT TO AMEND THE STAMP ACT CHAPTER
68:01.
(Gazetted 8th March, 2012)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the STAMP (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2012.

Short title.

Interpretation
Chap 68:01.
Amendment of section 2
of the Act.

STAMP
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 3

### 2.

In this Act, the Stamp Act is referred to as “the Act”.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting the following
directly before the definition of the word “C.I.F. value”A “child” for the purposes of this Act includes an adopted child.

Amendment of the Act.

### 4.

The Schedule to the Act is amended by deleting the
paragraph appearing immediately after the heading
“CONVEYANCE OR TRANSFER” operating as voluntary
disposition “inter vivos” and substituting the following:
“A Stamp Duty of 6.5% of the value of the property
so conveyed shall be paid of which 2.5% shall be paid
by the transferor and 4% by the transferee except
that where the transfer is between parent and child a
stamp duty of 2% of the value of the property so
conveyed shall be paid by the transferor and the
transferee shall pay no stamp duty.”

Passed in the House of Assembly this 22ndday of February, 2012.
