### 1.

This Act may be cited as the TRADE AND PROFESSIONAL LICENCES
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2005

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 92:01.

### 2.

In this Act, the Trade and Professional Licences Act is
referred to as “the Act”.

### 3.

TRADE AND PROFESSIONAL
LICENCES (AMENDMENT)

ACT 14

Section 2 of the Act is amended:
(a)

by inserting in alphabetical order the following
definitions:

“ “Community” means the Caribbean Community including the
CARICOM Single Market and Economy;
“Member State” means a Member State of the Community
including an Associate Member within the meaning of
Article 231 of the Treaty;
“Treaty” means the Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas establishing
the Caribbean Community including the CARICOM Single Market and Economy signed at Nassau, The Bahamas
on the 5th day of July, 2001.
“Professional” means a person practising a profession set out in
Schedule A as an independent professional.”
(b)

by repealing paragraph (b) in the definition of “nonresident individual” and substituting the following
new paragraphs:
“(b) a Community national, and the children of
such persons whether born in the relevant
Member State or not;
(c) notwithstanding paragraph (b) a child of a
Community national who is not born in the
relevant Member State must be a citizen of
that Member State;
(d) a person born outside the State but ordinarily resident therein for at least five years
immediately preceding the application for
a licence under this Act;

(c) by replacing paragraph (b) in the definition of
“non-resident” with the following:
“(b)

“a company”, a company incorporated
anywhere outside the Community or a
company whose registered office is situated outside the Community.”

### 4.

Section 3 of the Act is amended in subsection (1) by
replacing the word “State” with the word “Community”.

### 5.

“and shall date from the 1st January, April, June or October and
the last day of March, June, September or December as the case
may be”.

### 6.

“State” with the word “Community”.
