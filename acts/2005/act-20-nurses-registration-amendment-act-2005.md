# Nurses Registration (Amendment) Act, 2005

NURSES REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 20

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
Act No. 20 OF 2005

I assent

LS
N.J.O. Liverpool
President

23rd January, 2006
AN ACT TO AMEND THE NURSES REGISTRATION
ORDINANCE, CAP. 152.
(Gazetted 2nd February, 2006)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the NURSES REGISTRATION (AMENDMENT)
ACT 2005.

Short title.

NURSES REGISTRATION
(AMENDMENT)

Interpretation.
Cap. 152.

### 2.

In this Act the Nurses Registration Ordinance is referred
to as “the Ordinance”.

Insertion of Section
1A of the Ordinance.

### 3.

The Ordinance is amended by inserting immediately after
section 1 the following new section :
“Interpretation.

ACT 20

1A. In this Ordinance “Community” means the Caribbean
Community including the CARICOM Single Market and Economy established by the Treaty;
and
“Treaty” means the Revised Treaty of
Chaguaramas establishing the
Caribbean Community including the CARICOM Single Market and Economy signed at
Nassau, The Bahamas on the
5th day of July 2001.”

Amendment of
Section 7 of the
Ordinance.

### 4.

Section 7 of the Ordinance is amended in subsection (1)
by inserting the words “or in the Community” immediately after
the words “United Kingdom”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 20th day of December, 2005.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly
