# Customs (Control and Management) (Amendment) Act, 2005

CUSTOMS (CONTROL AND MANAGEMENT) ACT 13
(AMENDMENT)

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT No.13 OF 2005

I assent

LS
.

N. J. O. Liverpool
President

23rd January, 2006
AN ACT TO AMEND THE CUSTOMS (CONTROL
AND MANAGEMENT) ACT, CHAPTER 69:01.

(Gazetted 2nd February, 2006).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the CUSTOMS (CONTROL AND MANAGEMENT)
(AMENDMENT) ACT 2005

Short title.

CUSTOMS (CONTROL AND MANAGEMENT) ACT 13
(AMENDMENT)

Interpretation.
Chap. 69:01

### 2.

In this Act the Customs (Control and Management) Act
is referred to as “the Act”.

Amendment of section 35
of the Act.

### 3.

Section 35 of the Act is amended by repealing subsection
(6) and replacing it with the following:
“(6) Clearance granted under subsection (1) shall
become null and void if (a) fourteen days after the granting of a clearance
the pleasure craft cleared has not left the waters
of Dominica; or
(b) ninety-six hours after the granting of a clearance
the vessel cleared not being a pleasure craft
cleared has not left the waters of Dominica; or
(c) ninety-six hours after the granting of a clearance
the aircraft cleared has not taken off for a
destination outside of Dominica.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 19th day of December, 2005

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
Clerk of the House of Assembly.
