# 2005/2006 Appropriation Act,2005

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT No. 5 of 2005

I assent

LS

N. J. O. LIVERPOOL
President

19th August, 2005.
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE THIRTIETH DAY OF
JUNE 2006.
(Gazetted 25th August 2005).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the 2005/2006 APPROPRIATION ACT 2005.

Short title.

Appropriation.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of two
hundred and seventy five million, six hundred and sixty seven
thousand, two hundred and thirty nine dollars ($275,667,239) is
hereby authorised for meeting the expenditure for services for the
year ending 30th June, 2006 under the Heads of Expenditure
specified hereto, and the sum specified in the said Schedule
opposite to the Head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum
for the purposes specified in relation to the Head in the Estimates
for the said year.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 5

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and required from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance,
to pay out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several
services specified in the said Schedule, the several monies hereby
appropriated in such manner as the said warrant shall direct
without further order or formality.

### 4.

This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of July, 2005.
