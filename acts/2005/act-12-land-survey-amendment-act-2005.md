AN ACT TO AMEND THE LAND SURVEY ACT,

## CHAPTER 53:04

(Gazetted 2nd February, 2006).

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the LAND SURVEY (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2005.

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 53:04.

### 2.

In this Act the Land Survey Act is referred to as “the
Act.”

Amendment of section 2
of the Act.

LAND SURVEY
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 12

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by inserting in alphabetical order the following definitions:
“Community” means the Caribbean Community including the
CARICOM Single Market and Economy established by
the Treaty; and
“Treaty” means the Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas establishing
the Caribbean Community including the CARICOM Single Market and Economy signed at Nassau, The Bahamas,
on the 5th day of July, 2001.

Amendment of section 6
of the Act.

### 4.

Section 6 of the Act is amended by repealing subsection
(2) and substituting the following new subsections:
“ (2) For the purposes of subsection (1) “licensed
surveyor” includes a surveyor licensed to practice in any
Member State of the Community.”
(3) A fee, as may be prescribed by the Minister, shall
be payable to the State in respect of the grant of a licence
to a surveyor.”
Passed in the House of Assembly this 20th day of December, 2005.
