AN ACT TO AMEND THE APPROPRIATION (ROYAL
MERCHANT BANK AND FINANCE COMPANY
LTD) ACT 1999.
(Gazetted 24th February, 2005)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the APPROPRIATION (ROYAL MERCHANT BANK
AND FINANCE COMPANY LTD)
(AMENDMENT) ACT 2005.

Short title.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act the Appropriation (Royal Merchant Bank and
Finance Company Ltd) Act 1999 is referred to as “the Act”.

Act No. 19 of 1999.

APPROPRIATION (ROYAL
MERCHANT BANK AND FINANCE
COMPANY (LTD) (AMENDMENT)

ACT 4

Amendment of
section 3 of the Act.

### 3.

Section 3 of the Act is amended by deleting the words “the
amounts due in accordance with section 5 shall be charged on and
paid out of the National Loans Fund with recourse to the
Consolidated Fund” and substituting the words “Parliament shall
appropriate funds from the Consolidated Fund to the sinking
fund”.

Amendment of
section 5.

### 4.

The Act is amended by repealing section 5 and substituting
the following new section:
“ Payment of
taxes and
dividends.

Chap. 67:01.
Act No. 4 of 1994.

### 5.

From the Commencement of this Act.
(a)

all taxes and dividends payable
to the Government of Dominica
by Cable and Wireless (Dominica)
Ltd; and

(b)

all taxes payable to the Government of Dominica by Dominica
Electricity Services Ltd,

shall be paid to the Comptroller of Inland
Revenue as provided for in the Income
Tax Act or to the Accountant General
as provided for in the Finance (Administration) Act.”.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 27th day of January, 2005.
ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
