AN ACT TO AMEND THE PUBLIC SERVICE ACT
(Chap 23:01)

(Gazetted 24th February, 2005.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –

Short title.

PUBLIC SERVICE (AMENDMENT) ACT 2005.

### 2.

In this Act the Public Service Act is referred to as “the
Act”.

Interpretation.
Chap. 23:01.

Repeal and
replacement for
section 13 of the
Act.

### 3.

The Act is amended by repealing section 13 and replacing
it with the following new section:
“Retiring age of
public officers.

PUBLIC SERVICE
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 1

13\. (1) A public officer who is in the nursing
or teaching profession shall vacate his office on
attaining the age of sixty years.
(2) A public officer who is not in either
of the professions referred to in subsection (1)
shall vacate his office during the period set out
in Column III of the Schedule below
corresponding to the period during which he now
qualifies to retire as set out in Column I of the
Schedule so however, that the postponement of
his retirement shall not exceed the number of
years set out in Column II of the Schedule.
(3) A public officer may exercise to
opt to retire at fifty five years in accordance
with the existing provisions of the law or as
provided for in subsection (1) in the case of those
public officers belonging to the nursing and
teaching profession or as provided for in
subsection (2) in the case of other public
officers.
(4) The option referred to in subsection (3)
(a)
shall be made in the
form set out in the
Fourth Schedule obtainable from the Chief
Personnel Officer, Establishment,Personnel
and Training Department
within 60 days from the
date of commencement
of this Act and returned
to the Chief Personnel
Officer, Establishment,
Personnel and Training;
and

(b)

is irrevocable.

(5) A person who joins the Public
Service after the comencement of this Act shall
vacate his office on attaining the age of sixty
years.
