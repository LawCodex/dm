AN ACT TO AMEND THE PENSIONS ACT (Chap 23:80)

(Gazetted 24th February, 2005.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
PENSIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT 2005.

### 2.

In this Act the Pensions Act is referred to as “the Act”.

Short title.
Interpretation.
Chap. 23:80.

PENSIONS
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 3

Amendment of
section 6 of the Act.

### 3.

The Act is amended in section 6 by repealing paragraph (a) and
substituting the following new paragraph:
“(a) on or after attaining the age of fifty five years or, in
special cases, with the approval of the Cabinet, fifty
years; but in any event an officer shall not commence
to receive payments by way of pension in respect of
service under the Government before he attains the
age of fifty five years.”

Amendment of
Section 8 of the
Act.

Chap. 23:01.

### 4.

The Act is amended in section 8 by repealing paragraphs
(a), (b) and (c) and substituting the following:
(a) (i) at any time after he attains the age of sixty years
if he belongs to the nursing or teaching profession;
and as provided for in section 13 (2) of the Public
Service Act as amended by the Public Service
(Amendment) Act, 2005 if he does not belong to
the nursing or teaching profession;
(ii) at any time after he attains the age of sixty
years if he joins the Public Service after the
commencement of this Act.
(b) in special cases, with the approval of the Cabinet at
any time after he attains the age of fifty years.

Amendment of
section 22 of the
Act.
Commencement.

### 5.

The Act is amended in section 22(1) (b) by deleting the
words “fifty years” and substituting the words “fifty five years”

### 6.

This Act shall come into effect fifteen days after it
receives the assent of the President.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 27th day of January, 2005.
ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
