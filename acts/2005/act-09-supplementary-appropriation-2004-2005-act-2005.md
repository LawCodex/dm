AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM

DOMINICA DURING THE YEAR ENDING THE
THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE 2005.
(Gazetted 2nd February, 2006.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(2004/2005( NO. 2) ACT 2005.

Short title.

Legalisation of
unforeseen expenditure.
Schedule.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT 9

### 2.

(1) The sum of sixteen million, three hundred and sixty
four thousand, seven hundred and forty-nine dollars and seventeen cents ($16,364,749.17) as described in the Schedule was
withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during the
financial year ending on the 30th day of June, 2005.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen Government expenditure in anticipation of a
grant being made by the House of Assembly authorising the issue
of the specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of July, 2005 and appropriated to the
services set out in the Schedule.
