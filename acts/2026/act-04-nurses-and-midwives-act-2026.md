### 3.

(1) There is established a Council to be known as the
Dominica Nurses and Midwives Council.
(2) The Council shall be a body corporate to which
section 55 of the Interpretation and General Clauses Act applies.
(3) The First Schedule applies with respect to the
constitution and proceedings of the Council.

### 4.

(1) The Council shall (a) register as nurses and midwives persons who
comply with the requirements of this Act as to
registration;
(b) issue licences to nurses and midwives who comply
with the requirements of this Act as to licensing;
(c) enrol as practical nurses persons who comply
with the requirements of this Act as to enrolment;
(d) establish standards of proficiency necessary to
be registered or enrolled as a nurse or midwife;

Midwives Council.

Council.

(e) establish standards of education and training
necessary to achieve the standards and proficiency
it establishes under paragraph (d);
(f) determine and regulate the standards of practice,
competence, conduct and ethics of nurses and
midwives;
(g) make recommendations for Regulations
prescribing qualifications necessary in respect of
nurses and midwives;
(h) make recommendations for Regulations
prescribing requirements for the continuing
professional education of nurses and midwives;
(i) make recommendations for Regulations
prescribing a Code of Ethics and Conduct for the
guidance of nurses and midwives and standards

(j) monitor adherence to and investigate breaches of
this Act and Regulations or Code of Ethics and

recommendations made under this subsection may –

have not practised for or during a prescribed

(5) When the Council makes recommendaions under
subsection (4) it shall establish the standards to be met in relation

### 8.

(1) The Council shall appoint a Complaints Committee in
connection with one or more matters or for a fixed period of time
as the Council considers appropriate.

(2) The Complaints Committee shall consist of three
members of the Council.
(3) The Council shall appoint one of the members of the
Complaints Committee as chairperson.
(4) Where a vacancy occurs in the Complaints Committee
or where a member is unable to attend meetings of the Committee
or to participate in an investigation, the Chairman of the Council
may appoint another member of the Council to fill the vacancy or
replace the absent member.
(5) The replacement of a member of the Complaints
Committee does not invalidate or in any respect affect the validity
of an investigation.
(6) Subject to section 51(5), the Complaints Committee is
responsible for the conduct of preliminary investigations into any (a) matter concerning the ability of a nurse or midwife

responsibilities; or
(b) matter concerning the professional misconduct of
a nurse or midwife; and
(c) complaint respecting the conduct of a nurse or
midwife, that is referred to the Committee by the
Council.
(7) The Complaints Committee, on the conclusion of an
investigation shall report, in writing, its findings and decision and
the reasons for its decision to the Council and –
(a) in the case of a preliminary investigation, may
recommend that –

(i) no further investigation shall be carried out; or
(ii) the matter be referred, in whole or in part, to
the Disciplinary Committee;
(b) in any case, other than a preliminary investigation,
may direct that –
(i) the matter be referred, in whole or in part, to
the Disciplinary Committee; or
(ii) such other steps, as it considers appropriate in
the circumstances, be taken.
Third Schedule.

Disciplinary Committee.

(8) The Third Schedule applies with respect to the

### 9.

(1) The Disciplinary Committee shall consist of two
members of the Council and one registered nurse nominated by
the Dominica Nurses Association who is not a member of the
Council and who has been a registered nurse for a continuous
period of at least seven years.
(2) The Disciplinary Committee shall –
(a) hear allegations of professional misconduct or
incompetence made in respect of a nurse or
midwife;
(b) hear other matters referred to it by the Council;
(c) recommend to the Council appropriate measures
to be imposed in cases of proven professional
misconduct, incompetence or professional
incapacitation;
(d) perform any other duties related to disciplinary

matters, assigned to it by the Council; and
(e) on the conclusion of a hearing or an investigation,
report in writing, its findings and decision and the
reasons for its decision to the Council.
(3) The Disciplinary Committee shall conduct
investigations respecting –
(a) complaints referred to it by the Council following
a preliminary investigation by the Complaints
Committee; and
(b) any allegation that a nurse or midwife has engaged
in professional misconduct.
(4) A person who is a member of the Complaints
Committee is not eligible for appointment as a member of the
Disciplinary Committee.
(5) Where the investigation by the Disciplinary Committee
is to be undertaken by way of a formal hearing, the Council may
engage the services of an attorney-at-law of not less than seven
years standing or a former member of the Judiciary to assist the
Disciplinary Committee where assistance is considered necessary.
(6) The Fourth Schedule applies with respect to the
function and procedure of the Disciplinary Committee relating to
a formal hearing.

### 17.

(1) A person qualifies for registration as a nurse where –
(a) he –
(i) has completed the prescribed course of training

or the Caribbean Community recognised by
the Council;

(a)(i); and
(iii) he has successfully completed the regional
examination required for registration as a
nurse; or
(b) he holds a bachelor’s degree in nursing that is
recognised by the Council as providing a standard

that required under this Act; and
(c) he has successfully completed the regional
examination required for registration as a nurse
or any other examination that the Council considers
necessary to establish that the applicant possesses
satisfactory nursing training and experience; and
(d) he satisfies the Council that he –
(i) is medically fit to practise nursing;

recognise –

(1)(a) or (1)(b).

### 19.

(1) A person qualifies for registration as a midwife if he

(i) has completed a prescribed course of training

(b) he has successfully completed any other training

(b) he satisfies the Council that he –
(i) is medically fit to practise midwifery;
(ii) is a fit and proper person to practise midwifery;
(iii) can read, write, speak and understand the
English language; and
(c) he is at least eighteen years of age.

## PART III

LICENCE

Licence required.

### 34.

(1) A person shall not practise nursing unless he is a
licensed nurse.
(2) A person shall not practise midwifery unless he is a
licensed midwife.
(3) A person who contravenes subsection (1) or (2) is
guilty of an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine

of ten thousand dollars.
(4) Subsection (1) does not apply to a person who provides
a nursing service while undergoing a course in nursing if the
service is provided (a) under the supervision of a licensed nurse; and
(b) in an organisation approved by the Council.
(5) Subsection (2) does not apply to a person who attends
a woman at childbirth while undergoing a course in midwifery if
the attendance is carried out (a) under the supervision of a licensed midwife; and
(b) in an organisation approved by the Council.
(6) Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to —
(a) a licensed medical practitioner;
(b) licensed nurse practitioner;
(c) a person rendering assistance in an emergency,
without hire, gain or hope of reward, where the
services of a licensed medical practitioner, licensed

### 45.

A nurse or midwife shall in the discharge of his professional
responsibilities, conduct himself in a manner that is in accordance
with the generally recognised –
(a) duty and responsibility of a nurse or midwife; and
(b) professional ethics.

Prohibition of

### 46.

(1) A nurse or midwife shall not –
(a) do any act or thing that is contrary to the generally
recognised duty and responsibility of the nursing
or midwifery profession;

(b) engage in behaviour that undermines the honour
and integrity of the nursing or midwifery profession;
(c) engage in behaviour that is contrary to the ethics
of the nursing or midwifery profession .
(2) Without limiting the generality of subsection (1), the
behaviour referred to in subsection (1) includes –
(a) a wilful or reckless betrayal of a professional
confidence;
(b) the impersonation of another nurse or midwife;
(c) falsely holding out to the public directly or indirectly,
that he is registered, enrolled or licensed under
this Act;
(d) the holding out directly or indirectly by a nurse to
the public that he is specially qualified in any
particular special branch of nursing unless he has
taken a special course in that branch of nursing or
satisfied the requirements of section 18 and has
been registered as an advanced practice nurse in
the particular special branch of nursing or
midwifery;
(e) doing or failing to do any act or thing in connection
with his professional practice, the doing of which
or the failure to do such act or thing is
unprofessional or discreditable;
(f) a contravention of this Act or Regulations made
under this Act or the Code of Ethics.
(3) For the purposes of subsection (2)(a), any disclosure
which is legally justifiable or required for the treatment of a patient

is deemed not to be a wilful or deliberate betrayal of a professional
confidence.

(2) The nurse or midwife shall be permitted to give
evidence, call witnesses and make submissions orally or in writing
in his own behalf.
(3) The Disciplinary Committee may call additional
witnesses and may adjourn the proceedings to another convenient
time or place.
(4) The testimony of witnesses shall be taken under oath,
which the Chairperson of the Disciplinary Committee is authorised
to administer, and there shall be a full right to examine, crossexamine and re-examine witnesses.
(5) The Disciplinary Committee may issue summons for
the purposes of procuring the attendance of witnesses, the giving
of testimony and the production of documents before the
Disciplinary Committee.

(6) The rules of evidence in a hearing, and the proceedings
and penalties in the case of disobedience to any summon, shall be
the same as in civil cases in the Supreme Court.
7\. The Disciplinary Committee shall have a record of the
proceedings and the evidence –
(a) taken in shorthand, transcribed and certified by a
professional transcriber; or
(b) recorded by a sound recording machine, and
transcribed and certified by a professional
transcriber.
8\. Where, having heard the evidence in support of the
charges, the Disciplinary Committee is of the opinion that the
evidence is insufficient, it may dismiss the complaint without
calling on the nurse or midwife for his defence.
9\. On the conclusion of the hearing the Disciplinary
Committee shall submit a report to the Council in accordance with
section 52.

OATH OF SECRECY

without due authority, in any manner whatsoever publish or
communicate any facts or expressions of opinion based on such
facts that come to my knowledge by reason of my position as a
