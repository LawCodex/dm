# Eastern Caribbean Citizenship by Investment Regulatory Authority Agreement Act (Commencement) Order 2026

2026 EASTERN CARIBBEAN CITIZENSHIP S.R.O.14
BY INVESTMENT REGULATORY
AUTHORITY AGREEMENT

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS NO. 14 OF 2026.
ORDER
MADE by the Minister under section 1(2) of the Eastern
Caribbean Citizenship by Investment Regulatory
Authority Agreement Act, 2025 (Act No. 8 of 2025.)
(Gazetted July 23rd, 2026)

### 1.

This Order may be cited as the —

Short title.

EASTERN CARIBBEAN CITIZENSHIP BY
INVESTMENT REGULATORY AUTHORITY
AGREEMENT ACT (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER,
2026\.

### 2.

In this Order the Eastern Caribbean Citizenship by
Investment Regulatory Authority Agreement Act, 2025 is referred
to as “the Act”.

### 3.

The Act is deemed to have come into operation on the 1st
day of July, 2026.

Interpretation.
Act No. 8 of 2025.

Commencement.

Made this 22nd day of July, 2026.

ROOSEVELT SKERRIT
Minister responsible for the Citizenship
by Investment Programme
