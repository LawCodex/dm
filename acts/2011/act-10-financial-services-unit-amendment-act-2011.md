### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –

FINANCIAL SERVICES UNIT (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2011.

Short title.

Interpretation
Act No.18 of 2008.

### 2.

In this Act “the Act” means the Financial Services Unit
Act, 2008.

### 3.

Section 1 of the Act is amended by (a) deleting subsection (2) and renumbering subsection
(3) as subsection (4);
(b) inserting the following new subsections (2) and (3) “(2) Subject to subsection (3), this Act does not apply
to commercial banks.

Act No. 8 of 2011.

Amendment of section 2.

(3) This Act applies to commercial banks to the extent
necessary to ensure the compliance of commercial
banks with the Money Laundering (Prevention) Act
2011, the Suppression of the Financing of Terrorism
Act 2003, any Act, Regulation, Code or Guidelines
relating to the Money Laundering (Prevention) Act
2011 or the Suppression of the Financing of Terrorism
Act 2003.”

### 4.

Section 2 of the Act is amended in subsection (1) by
deleting the definition of (a) “Financial Intelligence Unit” and substituting the
following “Financial Intelligence Unit” means the unit
established under section 3 of the
Financial Intelligence Unit Act 2011;”
(b) “regulated person” and substituting the following “regulated person” means a financial institution
or person carrying out a scheduled business as
set out in the Schedule to the Prevention of
Money Laundering (Prevention) Act 2011;”

### 5.

Section 5 of the Act is repealed and replaced as follows –

Repeal and replacement
of section 5.

“5. The Director of the Financial Services
Unit shall be a public officer who shall be
responsible for the general administration of
this Act.”

### 7.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
10A –

Director’s power to
delegate.

Amendment of section
22\.

“10A. The Director may delegate to an officer of
the Unit, any power or duty imposed on the
Director by this Act or any other enactment, but
not his power of delegation.”

### 8.

Section 22 of the Act is amended in subparagraph (ii) of
subsection (1)(a) by deleting –
(a) the words “Money Laundering (Prevention) Act
2000” wherever they appear and substituting the
words “Money Laundering (Prevention) Act, 2011”;
(b) the word “Guidelines” and substituting the words
guidelines or guidance notes”.

Amendment of section
27\.

### 9.

Section 27 of the Act is amended in subparagraph (c) of
subsection (3) by deleting –
(a) the words “Money Laundering (Prevention) Act
2000” wherever they appear and substituting the
words “Money Laundering (Prevention) Act, 2011”;
(b) the word “Guidelines” and substituting the words
“guidelines or guidance notes”.

### 10.

Section 31 of the Act is amended by inserting the words
“or guidance notes” immediately after the word “guidelines”
wherever it appears in section 31 including in the marginal notes.

Amendment of section
31\.

### 11.

Section 32 of the Act is repealed and replaced by the
following -

Repeal and replacement
of section 32.

“Director may seek
assistance and
share and request
information”.

32\. (1) In discharging his functions under the
Act the Director may –
(a) seek assistance, share or request
information from the Central Bank
subject to a confidentiality agreement
and a memorandum of understanding;or
(b) seek assistance, share or request
information, from other regulatory
authority including a foreign regulatory
authority.
(2) Information shared or requested under
subsection (1) is for the purpose of
determining whether a regulated person
is in compliance with this Act.
(3) In this section “regulatory authority” and
“foreign regulatory authority” have the
meaning assigned to them by section 2
of the Exchange of Information Act, 2001.”
