APPROPRIATION

ACT 4

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 4 OF 2011

I assent

E. I. WATTY
Ag. President

18th July, 2011
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE 30TH DAY OF JUNE,
2012\.
(Gazetted 21st July, 2011)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the-

Short Title.

2011/2012 APPROPRIATION ACT 2011.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of four
hundred and thirty-two million, five hundred and ninety-eight

Appropriation.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.
Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 4

thousand, three hundred and eighty-four dollars ($432,598,384) is
hereby authorized for meeting the expenditure for services for the
year ending June 30th, 2012 under the Head of Expenditure
specified hereto, and the sum specified in the said Schedule
opposite to the Head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum
for the purposes specified in relation to the Head of the Estimates
for the said year.

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance, to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the Schedule, the several monies appropriated in the
manner as the said warrant shall direct without further order or
formality.

### 4.

This Act, is deemed to have come into force on the 1st day
of July 2011.
