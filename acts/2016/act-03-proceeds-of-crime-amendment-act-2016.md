### 5.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section
63A after the heading “PART V MISCELLANEOUS”“Seizure and
detention of
cash on import
or export.

63A. (1) A customs officer or a police officer
may seize and, in accordance with this Part, detain any
cash which is being imported into or exported from
Dominica if its amount is not less than ten thousand
dollars and he has reasonable grounds for suspecting
that it is recoverable cash.
(2) On informing a person of the provisions of
subsection (1) a customs officer or a police
officer may require the person to sign a declaration
as to the amount of cash being imported into or
exported from Dominica.
(3) For the purposes of section 66, if the person
who is importing or exporting the cash signs a
declaration under subsection (2) that is untrue in any

mentioned particular, the Magistrate shall receive
this untrue declaration as prima facie evidence of
the matter mentioned in section 66(3)(a).
(4) In this section “export” in relation to cash,
includes its being brought to any place in Dominica
for the purpose of being exported.”.
