AN ACT RESPECTING THE IMPOSITION OF A LEVY
FOR THE MAINTENANCE OF HIGHWAYS.
(Gazetted 4th December, 2025.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the –
HIGHWAY MAINTENANCE LEVY ACT, 2025.
(2) This Act is deemed to have come into force on the 1st
day of October, 2025.

Short title and
commencement.

Interpretation.

HIGHWAY MAINTENANCE LEVY

ACT 13

### 2.

In this Act –
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Inland Revenue;
“levy” means the levy imposed by section 3.

Imposition of levy.

### 3.

A person who owns a vehicle of the class specified in
Column 1 of the Schedule shall pay the corresponding levy
specified in Column 2 of the Schedule.

Payment of levy.

### 4.

(1) A person referred to in section 3 shall pay the levy at
the time of payment of the motor vehicle licence fee under the
Vehicles and Road Traffic Act and prior to payment of the motor
vehicle licence fee.

Chap. 46:50.

(2) The levy is payable to the Comptroller.
Consequential
amendment.
Chap. 46:50.

### 5.

The Vehicle and Road Traffic Act is amended by inserting
the following new section 111A immediately after the heading
“Part IX General”:
“Offence not to
pay highway
maintenance
levy.

111A. (1) If the owner of any motor vehicle or
trailer keeps for use or allows to be kept for use a
vehicle or trailer in respect of which he has not paid
or otherwise fails or omits to pay the highway maintenance levy the owner, driver or other person in
charge of the vehicle commits an offence and is liable
on summary conviction to a fine of five hundred
dollars and to imprisonment for three months.
(2) In this section “highway maintenance levy”
means the levy payable under section 3 of the Highway
Maintenance Levy Act 2025.”.
