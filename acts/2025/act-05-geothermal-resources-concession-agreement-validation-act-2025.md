# Geothermal Resources Concession Agreement (Validation) Act 2025

GEOTHERMAL RESOURCES
DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
(VALIDATION)

ACT 5

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 5 OF 2025.
I assent

LS
SYLVANIE BURTON
President
27th June, 2025
AN ACT TO VALIDATE AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN
THE GOVERNMENT OF DOMINICA AND THE
GEOTHERMAL POWER COMPANY OF
DOMINICA
(2023) LIMITED DATED
DECEMBER 5th 2023.
(Gazetted 30th June, 2025.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Government of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
GEOTHERMAL RESOURCES CONCESSION
AGREEMENT (VALIDATION) ACT, 2025.

Short title.

Validation.

### 2.

The Geothermal Resource Concession Agreement dated
December 5th 2023 made between the Government of Dominica,
and the Geothermal Power Company of Dominica (2023) Limited,
respecting geothermal resource development at Laudat and
matters ancillary thereto is hereby validated, confirmed and
declared to have been lawfully executed and the agreement and
undertakings mentioned therein are binding upon both parties.

Commencement.

GEOTHERMAL RESOURCES
DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
(VALIDATION)

ACT 5

### 3.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the
5th day of December, 2023.
Passed in the House of Assembly this 25th day of June, 2025.

DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly
