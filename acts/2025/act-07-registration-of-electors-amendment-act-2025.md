# Registration of Electors (Amendment) Act 2025

REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS

ACT 7

COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT NO. 7 OF 2025.

I assent

SYLVANIE BURTON
President
29th August, 2025.
AN ACT TO AMEND THE REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS ACT, 2025 (ACT NO. 1 OF 2025).
(Gazetted 29th August, 2025.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025.

Short title.

Interpretation.
No. 1 of 2025.

### 2.

In this Act the Registration of Electors Act is referred to
as “the Act”.

Repeal and replacement
of section 50 (1).

### 3.

Section 50 (1) of the Act is repealed and replaced as
follows –
“Confirmation
of registration
required.

REGISTRATION OF ELECTORS

ACT 7

50\. (1) The Commission shall by order, within

180 days of the effective date declare a period, being
not less than twelve months, to be the confirmation
period for the purpose of this Part and may, in like
manner, alter the commencement date and extend the
confirmation period so declared, so, however, that
the period initially declared together with any extension thereof shall not exceed fifteen months.”

Passed in the House of Assembly this 29th day of August , 2025.

DANIEL JAMES
Clerk of the House of Assembly
