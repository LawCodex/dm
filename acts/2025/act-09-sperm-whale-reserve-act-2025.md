## PART II

SPERM WHALE RESERVE

Sperm Whale Reserve.

### 4.

(1) A Sperm Whale Reserve is hereby established within
the maritime zones of Dominica, as depicted in Schedule 1, which
contains geographic coordinates for the Reserve and fixes the
Reserve’s boundaries.
(2) The purposes of the Reserve are to -

(a) protect the marine ecosystem and living resources
particularly sperm whales and sperm whale habitats in the maritime zones of Dominica; and
(b) regulate all activities related to viewing and interacting with sperm whales and other cetaceans in
the maritime zones of Dominica.
(3) It is prohibited for any person to conduct a swim-withthe-whales activity or whale-watching activity including an activity with any other cetacean anywhere in the maritime zones of
Dominica except within the Sperm Whale Reserve.
(4) Any person who engages in an activity in contravention of subsection (3) commits an offence and is liable to the fixed
penalty specified in Schedule 4.

### 5.

(1) The area of the Reserve defined by the nine nautical
miles top and bottom limits to the sixteen nautical miles top and
bottom corners is designated a sperm whale sanctuary.

Sperm Whale Sanctuary

(2) Water sports or other tourism activities is prohibited in
the sperm whale sanctuary.
(3) Water sports or other marine tourism activities is
permitted from the sea shore that is 0 nautical miles which is an
area designated outside the sperm whale santuary.
(4) Any person who engages in an activity in contravention of subsection (2) commits an offence and is liable to the fixed
penalty specified in Schedule 4.

### 6.

(1) The Minister may, by Regulations and in consultation
with the Board, and the Minister responsible for Maritime Affairs,
change the boundaries of the Reserve to expand its area in
furtherance of the purposes of this Act.

Consultation to change
boundaries.

(2) Any change of the boundaries of the Reserve to expand
its area shall ensure that the safety of navigation is not compromised.

(1) The Sperm Whale Reserve Board is hereby established
for the purposes of the governance and management of the
Reserve.
(2) The Board shall consist of ten members as follows (a) a person from an institution involved in scientific
research or academia with expertise in cetaceans
(b) a person from the private sector engaged in
swimming with the whales or whale watching
activities;
(c) a representative from the Association of Fisheries Cooperatives;
(d) a representative from the Ministry responsible for
Finance;
(e) a representative from the Ministry responsible for
the Environment;
(f) the Chief Executive Officer of the Dominica Air
and Sea Port Authority;
(g) the Chief Fisheries Officer;
(h) the Chief Technical Officer of the Ministry responsible for Tourism; and

(3) Members of the Board appointed under subsection (1)

the Chairperson of the Board.

Members who is not designated as Chairperson as the Deputy

## PART VII

PROTECTION OF SPERM WHALES
Division 1—Protection of Whales

### 21.

(1) It is prohibited to domesticate, fish, gather, habituate,
harass, hunt, possess, remove or trade a sperm whale or any other
cetacean or products and parts derived therefrom within the
maritime zones of Dominica.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1) the Minister may
grant permission to carry out any of the activities outlined in

Protection of
sperm whales
in maritime
zones.

subsection (1) for the purpose of conducting scientific research.
(3) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an
offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of 500,000 dollars or
ten years imprisonment.
(4) It is prohibited to conduct any tourism activity or
professional media activities in the maritime zones of Dominica
except in accordance with this Act and the Fisheries Act.
(5) A person who contravenes subsection (4) commits an
offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of 500,000 dollars or
ten years imprisonment.
Notification of
authorities.

### 22.

(1) Any person who becomes aware of any activity
contrary to section 4(3), 26(1), 26(2), 27(1), 29(2) or 30(2)
anywhere within the maritime zones of Dominica shall immediately notify the Reserve Office.
(2) A person who fails to notify the Reserve Office of an
activity listed in subsection 22(1) commits an offence and is liable
on conviction to a fine of 10,000 dollars or ten yearsimprisonment.
Division 2
Prohibitions within the Reserve

Activities prohibited
within the Reserve.

### 23.

(1) Except for emergencies for the safety of life and
property at sea, search and rescue and safety of navigation, or
with the express permission of the Minister, it is prohibited for any
person to do any of the following within the Reserve (a) approach a cetacean or engage in a swim with the
whales activity with a sperm whale or other
cetacean while the cetacean is nursing or birthing;
(b) approach a cetacean where the cetacean is or
shows signs of becoming alarmed or disturbed

including where the cetacean exhibits prolonged
diving or evasive swimming patterns, rapidly
changes direction or speed, or splashes its tail in
a side-to-side motion;
(c) attach a camera, any other media device or tag,
except for a non-invasive tag placed on a sperm
whale or any other cetacean as part of a scientific
research activity being conducted under a valid
permit;
(d) cause a cetacean to be separated from a group of
other cetaceans, or cause any members of the
group to be scattered, except for the safety,
security or health of the cetacean;
(e) cause an adult cetacean to be separated from a
calf;
(f) cause a vessel except a registered local fishing
vessel to traverse within 50 meters of a whale at
a speed greater than 10 knots;
(g) cause the passage of a cargo vessel, cruise ship,
motor yacht, sailing yacht with a motor or engine
running, or any vessel over 100 gross tonnage or
greater than 60 feet long, except where granted
a permit for scientific purposes under the Fisheries Act;
(h) chase, physically contact, offer food or feed a
sperm whale or any other cetacean;
(i) cut off the path of a cetacean or prevent a
cetacean from leaving the vicinity of the Reserve;

(j) dump or discharge garbage, any harmful or noxious substance, sewage or any other type of
waste;
(k) engage in any tourism activity, professional media
activity, or scientific research activity without
having an enabled Automatic Identification System or other equivalent digital vessel monitoring
system and Very High Frequency Radio onboard
the vessel;
(l) engage in any tourism activity, scientific research
activity, or professional media activity without a
valid permit;
(m) facilitate or engage in a whale watching activity,
swim with the whales activity or an activity with
any other cetacean contrary to this Act;
(n) for a tourism operator, to conduct any activity
