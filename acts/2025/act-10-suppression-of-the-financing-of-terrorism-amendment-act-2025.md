BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows :

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the –
SUPPRESSION OF THE FINANCING OF
TERRORISM (AMENDMENT) ACT 2025.

Short title.

2025 SUPPRESSION OF THE FINANCING ACT 10
OF TERRORISM (AMENDMENT)

Interpretation.
Chap. 73:04.

### 2.

In this Act the Suppression of the Financing of Terrorism
Act is referred to as “the Act”.

Insertion of new
section 11D

### 3.

The Act is amended by inserting immediately after section
11C the following new section 11D:
“Procedure
on country
request for
designation.

11D. (1) Where the Competent Authority receives a request for designation from another country
and is satisfied according to the applicable legal principles that the requested designation is supported on
reasonable ground or has a reasonable basis to suspect
or believe that the proposed designee meets the criteria
for designation as required by the United Nations
Security Council Resolution 1373 the Competent Authority shall recommend to the Minister that an order be
made under section 11 in respect of the person.
(2) Where the Competent Authority makes
a recommendation under subsection (1), the Minister
shall make a prompt determination whether he is
satisfied, based on the Competent Authority’s recommendation, that the proposed designee meets the criteria for designation in United Nations Security Resolution 1373.”.

Amendment of
section 14.

### 4.

Section 14 of the Act is amended in subsection (1) by
deleting paragraph (a) and substituting the following:
“(a) in respect of property or interest situated in
Dominica or elsewhere held:
(i) by or on behalf of terrorist or terrorist group
designated under section 11(2);

(ii) for the benefit of a terrorist or terrorist group
designated under subsection 11(2) by a person owned or controlled, directly or indirectly,
by a person or persons designated as a terrorist or terrorist group under section 11(2);”.
