Unforeseen.

### 2.

(1) The sum of one hundred and eight million, five hundred
and ten thousand, one hundred and twenty-three dollars and
ninety-seven cents ($108,510,123.97) as described in the Schedule
was withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during
the Financial Year 2024/2025 ending on the 30th day of June, 2025.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen Government expenditure in anticipation of the
granting of an appropriation by the House of Assembly for the
issue of a specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from June 2024 and appropriated to the services set
out in the Schedule.
