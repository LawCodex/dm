# Supplementary Appropriation (1996/97) Act, 1996

C. A. SORHAINDO
President

AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM THE
CONSOLIDATED FUND OF DOMINICA IN EXCESS OF
THE SUM PROVIDED BY LAW FOR THE SERVICES OF
THE COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA DURING THE
YEAR ENDING THE THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE, 1997.

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(1996/97) ACT 1996.

### 2.

(1) The sum of two million, two hundred and three thousand, two hundred and eighty dollars ($2,203,280.00) as described
in the Schedule was withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica during the financial year ended on the 30th day of June,
1997\.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to defray
unforeseen governmental expenditure in anticipation of a grant
being made by the House of Assembly' authorising the issue of the
specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica and appropriated to the services set out in the Schedule.
