# 1996/97 Appropriation Act, 1996, 1996

c.A. SORHAINDO
President.

30th July, 1996.
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE YEAR ENDING ON
THE THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE, 1997.
(Gazetted 8th August, 1996.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of
$332,564,090 dollars is hereby authorised for meeting expenditure
for services for the year ending 30th June, 1997, under the Schedule
hereto, and the sum specified in the said Schedule appointed out of
the said sum of $332,564,090 dollars for the purposes specified in
relation to the Head in the Estimates for the said year.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

### 3.

The Accountant General is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon Warrant of the Minister for Finance to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the Schedule the several monies hereby appropriated in
such manner as the Warrant shall direct without further order or
formality.

### 4.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st
day ofJuly, 1996.
