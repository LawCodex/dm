### 1.

This Act may be cited as the INVEST DOMINICA AUTHORITY ACT, 2007.

### 2.

For the purposes of this Act “Authority” means the Invest Dominica Authority established by section 3;
“Board” means the Board of Directors established by
section 5;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Industry;

Functions of the
Authority.

### 3.

(1) There is hereby established an Authority to be known
as the Invest Dominica Authority.
(2) The Authority is a body corporate within the meaning
of Part IX of the Interpretation and General Clauses Act.

### 4.

(1) The Authority shall assist the Government in the
formulation and execution of policies and programmes to attract
investment into Dominica. In fulfillment of this mandate the
Authority will:
(a) formulate strategies and programmes to promote
Dominica as a jurisdiction in which to invest;
(b) execute strategies, programmes and activities to
promote Dominica as a jurisdiction in which to
invest;
(c) provide information to investors and prospective
investors, domestic and foreign, on the legal, financial,
real property, industrial relations requirements and
other matters relating to the establishment of business enterprises in Dominica;
(d) compile and disseminate information on available investment opportunities in Dominica;
(e) provide local and foreign investors and prospective investors with information on potential joint
venture partnerships;

(f) liaise with government agencies, departments
and authorities to identify and secure suitable land
that may be required by investors and prospective investors to implement investment projects;
(g) establish procedures consistent with this Act to
process applications for investment projects and
investment incentives efficiently;
(h) provide services to assist investors and prospective investors, domestic and foreign, to obtain
such certificates, approvals, authorizations or
permits as may be required by law to establish
and operate business enterprises;
(i) process applications for investment incentives
consistent with the law and policy of the Government;
(j) advise on ways to improve the industrial and
investment climate of Dominica;
(k) assist in the promotion of international financial
business; and
(l) do such things as may be necessary for the proper
performance of its functions.

### 5.

(1) There is hereby established a Board of Directors of the
Authority which is responsible for the general administration of
the Authority.
(2) The Schedule has effect with respect to the
constitution of the Board and otherwise in relation thereto.

office; and
(b) the chairperson in respect of the office of the chairperson,

### 7.

(1) A director of the Board who has any interest in a
company or concern with which the Authority proposes to make
a contract or transact any other business shall disclose to the
Authority the particulars of that interest; and details of the
disclosure shall be recorded in the minutes taken at the meeting
at which the disclosure is made.
(2) A director referred to in subsection (1) shall not take
part in any deliberation or discussion of the Board relating to that
contract or business.

Appointment of
Executive Director.

### 8.

(1) The Board shall with the approval of the Minister
appoint an Executive Director.
(2) The Executive Director is subject to the directions of
the Board and is responsible to the Board for the execution of its
policy and the management of the Authority.

Appointment of
employees, remuneration
and pensions.

### 9.

(1) The Board may, subject to subsection (2) employ, at
such remuneration and on such terms and conditions as it thinks
fit, such persons as it considers necessary for the proper
discharge of the functions of the Authority.
(2) The Board shall not assign remuneration to the post
of Executive Director without prior approval in writing of the
Minister.

Directions of Minister.

Funds of the Authority.

### 10.

The Minister may, give to the Board, directions of a
general nature in respect of the policy to be followed by the Board
in the performance of its functions under this Act, and the Board
shall comply with the directions.

### 11.

(1) The funds of the Authority shall consist of monies(a) voted by Parliament for use by the Authority;

(b) accruing from the operations of the Authority;
(c) payable to a property vested in the Authority;
(d) borrowed by the Authority; and
(e) as may become available from any other source.
(2) The funds of the Authority shall be applied towards
(a) the payment of salaries, wages, fees, allowances,
pensions and gratuities of persons employed or
formerly employed by the Authority; and
(b) meeting the obligations and discharging the
functions of the Authority and matters incidental
to those obligations and functions.
(3) The Board may not, except with the prior approval
of the Minister in writing,
(a) invest the funds of the Authority in securities;
(b) dispose real property of the Authority; or
(c) lend money, charge any of the Agency’s assets or
guarantee the performance of any obligation by
another person.

### 12.

The Authority is exempt from the payment of income tax
and stamp duty.

Exemption.

The Authority may with the consent in writing of the
Minister responsible for Finance, borrow money from

as the Minister responsible for Finance approves for the -

Guarantee.

### 14.

(1) With the approval of the House of Assembly, the
Minister of Finance may guarantee, in such manner and on such
conditions as he thinks fit, the payments of the principal and
interest of any authorised borrowing by the Authority.
(2) Where the Minister of Finance is satisfied that there
has been default in the payment of any principal moneys or
interest guaranteed under this section, he shall direct the
repayment out of the Consolidated Fund of the amount in respect
of which there has been such default.

Fees.

Accounts and audit.

### 15.

The Authority may charge such fees for any service
performed by the Authority as are determined by the Board.

### 16.

(1) The Authority shall keep accounts of its transactions.
(2) The accounts of the Authority shall be audited annually
by an auditor appointed by the Board with the approval of the
Minister.
(3) The Director of Audit may at any time, and shall on
the direction of the Minister responsible for Finance, carry out an
investigation into, or special audit of, the accounts of the Authority.

Access by Director
of Audit or auditor.

### 17.

(1) A director, officer or employee of the Authority shall
afford the Director of Audit or other auditor appointed under
section 16 access to all books, documents, cash and securities of
the Authority, and on request shall give the Director of Audit or
auditor any information within the knowledge of that director,
officer or employee that relates to the operations of the Authority.
(2) A person who fails to comply with this section
commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine
of one thousand dollars or imprisonment for 3 months or both.

Reports.

### 18.

(1) The Authority shall, as soon as possible after the
expiration of the financial year and in any case not later than
31st December in each year, submit to the Minister a report
containing a -

(a) detailed account of the performance of the
Authority’s activities and operations during the
preceding financial year; and
(b) statement of audited accounts of the Authority.
(2) The Minister shall lay before Parliament a copy of the
report referred to in subsection (1) as soon as practical after
receiving that report.

### 19.

The financial year of the Authority shall be a period of 12
months ending at midnight on 30th June.

Appointment of Board.

## SCHEDULE

1\. (1) The Board of Directors shall comprise of 7 persons
appointed by the Minister.
(2) In making the appointment of members of the Board,
the Minister shall have regard to persons who would collectively
have experience or sound qualifications in tourism, law, economics, industry, business and any other area of relevant expertise.
(3) The Minister shall appoint a director of the Board to
be Chairman and another director to be Deputy Chairman.
(4) In the case of the inability of any director to act, the
Minister may appoint a person to act temporarily in the place of
that director.
(5) Every director is, on the expiration of the period of
appointment, eligible for re-appointment for a further period.

Term of office.

2\. A director holds office for a term of 2 years unless he
resigns or has his appointment revoked before the end of that
term, but (a) a person who is appointed to fill a vacancy created by
the death, resignation or removal from office of a
former director shall hold office only for the
unexpired portion of the term of that former director;

(b) every director is, on the expiration of the term of
his appointment, eligible for re-appointment for a
further term.
3\. The Minister may, in writing, grant leave of absence
to a director, and may appoint a person to act temporarily in the
place of that director.
4\. A director other than the Chairman may resign his
office, by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister,
transmitted through the Chairman; and from the date of the
receipt of the instrument by the Minister that director ceases to be
a director of the Authority.
5\. The Chairman may at any time by instrument in
writing addressed to the Minister resign his office, and from the
date of the receipt of such instrument by the Minister the
Chairman ceases to be Chairman and, if the instrument so
specifies, ceases to be a director of the Authority.
6\. Where the Chairman is absent from Dominica or is for
any reason unable to act, the Deputy Chairman, may at any time
call a special meeting of the Board, and is required to call such a
meeting within 7 days after receiving a notice to do so by any 3
directors of the Board.
7\. Five directors constitute a quorum.

and where the voting is equal the Chairman, in addition to his

Location of meetings.

Minutes.

11\. Minutes of each meeting are to be kept by the
Secretary or other person appointed by the Board for the purpose,
and are to be confirmed by the Board at its next meeting and

Invitation to attend

12\. The Board may invite persons to attend any of its
meetings for the purpose of assisting or advising it respecting any
matters with which it is dealing, but a person so invited does not

Board to regulate
proceedings.

13\. Subject to this Schedule, the Board may regulate its
own proceedings.

Documents

14\. All documents made by, and all decisions of, the
Board shall be signified under the hand of the Chairman or any
director authorised to act in that behalf or by the Secretary.

Seal

15\. (1) The seal of the Authority shall be kept in the
custody of the Chairman or the Deputy Chairman or of such
officer of the Authority as the Agency may approve, and may be
affixed to instruments pursuant to a resolution of the Authority
and in the presence of the Chairman or Deputy Chairman and of
any other director.
(2) The seal of the Authority shall be authenticated
by the signature of the Chairman or Deputy Chairman and of the
other director in whose presence it was affixed, and such seal
shall be officially and judicially noticed.
(3) All documents, other than those required by law
to be made under seal, made by, and all decisions of, the
Authority may be signified under the hand of the Chairman or
Deputy Chairman.

Appointment of
committees.

16\. (1) The Board in consultation with the Minister may
appoint committees to assist with the proper discharge of its
functions subject to such conditions or restrictions as the Board
imposes.

(2) The number of members of a committee
appointed by the Board and the terms of office of members shall
be fixed by the Board.
(3) A committee appointed by the Board under this
paragraph may include persons who are not directors but such
persons shall not comprise more than one-third of the membership of the committee.
