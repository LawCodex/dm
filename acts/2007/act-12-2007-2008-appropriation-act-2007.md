I assent

23rd August, 2007.
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE THIRTIETH DAY OF
JUNE 2008.
(Gazetted 30th August, 2007).
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the 2007/2008 APPROPRIATION ACT 2007.

Short title.

Appropriation.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.

Schedule.

Commencement.

2007

APPROPRIATION

ACT 12

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of three
hundred and forty-two million, six hundred and nineteen
thousand, five hundred and one dollars ($342,619,501) is hereby
authorised for meeting the expenditure for services for the year
ending 30th June, 2008 under the Heads of Expenditure specified
hereto, and the sum specified in the said Schedule opposite to the
Head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum for the purposes
specified in relation to the Head in the Estimates for the said year.

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and
required from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for
Finance, to pay out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the
several services specified in the said Schedule, the several
monies hereby appropriated in such manner as the said warrant
shall direct without further order or formality.

### 4.

This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the
1 day of July, 2007.
