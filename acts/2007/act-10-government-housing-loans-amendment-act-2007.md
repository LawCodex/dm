AN ACT TO AMEND THE GOVERNMENT HOUSING
LOANS ACT, CHAPTER 23:60.

(Gazetted 10th May, 2007.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the GOVERNMENT HOUSING LOANS
(AMENDMENT)) ACT, 2007.

Short title.

Amendment of section 2
of Chap. 23:60.

GOVERNMENT HOUSING LOANS
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 10

### 2.

Section 2 of the Government Housing Loans Act, is
amended by deleting the definiton of “employee” and substituting the following:
“ “employee” means a Government employee
(a) on the permanent and pensionable establishment; or
(b) who has been in continuous service for the
prescribed period although not in the permanent and pensionable establishment”.

Passed in the House of Assembly this 2nd day of April, 2007.

ALEX F. PHILLIP (MRS.)
