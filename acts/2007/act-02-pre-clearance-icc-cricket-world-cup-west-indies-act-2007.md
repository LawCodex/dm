## PART I

PRELIMINARY

### 1.

This Act may be cited as theShort title.

PRE-CLEARANCE (ICC CRICKET WORLD CUP
WEST INDIES 2007) ACT, 2007.

Interpretation.

Chap. 69:01

ACT 2

### 2.

In this Act –
“Customs Officer” means a customs officer within the meaning
of the Customs (Control and Management) Act;
“CWC 2007” means the ICC Cricket World Cup West Indies 2007;
“CWC 2007 Inc.” means ICC Cricket World Cup West Indies
2007 Inc., a company incorporated in the territory of the
British Virgin Islands, or its authorised representatives;
“GCC” means Global Cricket Corporation Pte Limited, a
company incorporated in Singapore;
“goods” includes baggage, livestock, merchandise, money and
stores but does not include anything prescribed by Regulations made under paragraph 16 (2) (a);
“ICC” means the International Cricket Council, a company
limited by guarantee and incorporated in the territory of the
British Virgin Islands;
“IDI” means ICC Development (International) Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of the territory of the
British Virgin Islands;

Chap. 18:01.

“Immigration officer” means an immigration officer within the
meaning of the Passport and Immigration Act;
“local officer” means the competent Dominican officer for the
purposes of immigration, customs, public health, food
inspection or plant or animal health;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Customs;
“pre-clearance area” means an area designated by the Minister
under section 5;

“pre-clearance laws” means the law of a Specified State with
respect to the admission of a person to or the importation
of goods into that Specified State;
“pre-clearance officer” means a person authorised by a Specified
State to perform pre-clearance duties in Dominica for the
purposes of the admission of a person to or the importation
of goods into that Specified State;
“prescribed passenger” means a person specified in Schedule I;

Schedule I.

“Specified State” means a State specified by the Minister by
Order; and
“WICB” means the West Indies Cricket Board Inc., a company
incorporated in the territory of the British Virgin Islands.

### 3.

The Prime Minister may by Order restrict any immunity
or privileges under this Act in relation to a Specified State where
in his opinion the immunity or privileges exceeds those accorded
to Dominica by that State.

Reciprocity.

## PART II

ADMINISTRATION OF PRE-CLEARANCE LAWS

### 4.

(1) Pre-clearance laws may be administered in Dominica
in a pre-clearance area with respect to (a) a prescribed passenger seeking admission to a
Specified State; and
(b) goods to be imported into a Specified State by a
prescribed passenger.
(2) Penalties under pre-clearance laws may be enforced
by pre-clearance officers in Dominica.

Pre-clearance laws.

(3) Nothing in this Act precludes a local officer from
enforcing the laws of Dominica in a pre-clearance area.

## PART III

PRE-CLEARANCE AND INTRANSIT AREAS
Pre-clearance areas.

### 5.

The Minister may designate by Order –
(a) any area as a pre-clearance area where pre-clearance officers may pre-clear prescribed passengers
and their goods for entry into a Specified State;
(b) all or part of a pre-clearance area as an “intransit
area”, for the pre-clearance of prescribed passengers and their goods that are in transit through
Dominica by air to a Specified State.

Access to pre-clearance
area.

### 6.

(1) A person who is not –
(a) a prescribed passenger destined for a Specified
State; or

Schedule II.

(b) a person, or a member of a category of persons,
specified in Schedule II,
shall not enter a pre-clearance area.
(2) A person, other than a person specified in Schedule
II, who enters a pre-clearance area shall report to a pre-clearance
officer without delay.

Schedule II.

(3) A pre-clearance officer may order a person, other
than a person specified in Schedule II, who is in a pre-clearance
area to report to him or to leave the area.

(4) A person ordered to leave a pre-clearance area
under subsection (3) shall report without delay to an immigration officer and a customs officer for examination.

(5)

A person who (a) enters a pre-clearance area when not authorised
to do so; or
(b) fails to comply with subsection (2) or (4),

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine
of five thousand dollars.

### 7.

(1) A prescribed passenger intending to travel to a
Specified State through an intransit area, shall report without
delay on arrival in Dominica to a pre-clearance officer at the
intransit area.

Arrival in intransit area.

(2) A pre-clearance officer may defer clearance of any
prescribed passenger who fails to report to a pre-clearance
officer in accordance with subsection (1).

### 8.

(1) Subject to subsection (2), a prescribed passenger
may, at any stage of the pre-clearance process, leave the preclearance area without departing for a Specified State.

Right to leave preclearance area.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply where a pre-clearance officer informs a passenger that the officer suspects on
reasonable grounds that the passenger has committed an offence
under section 12 or 13.
(3) A prescribed passenger, whether travelling from
Dominica or in transit through Dominica, who leaves a preclearance area without departing immediately for a Specified
State is subject to the laws of Dominica relating to the departure
of persons and the exportation of goods from Dominica.

## PART IV

POWERS OF PRE-CLEARANCE OFFICERS

### 9.

(1) A pre-clearance officer may exercise the powers
and perform the duties under this Act only in a pre-clearance area.

Powers of pre-clearance
officer.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), a pre-clearance
officer may, outside a pre-clearance area, examine any aircraft
that is subject to pre-clearance, and any goods that are in, or that
are to be loaded onto that aircraft.
(3) After an examination described in subsection (2),
the pre-clearance officer may request that a prescribed passenger,
or anything that is in or that is to be loaded onto the aircraft, be sent
to a pre-clearance area to be dealt with in accordance with this
Act.
Assistance of local
officer.

### 10.

A pre-clearance officer may request a local officer to
assist in exercising the powers of search, examination, seizure and
detention conferred on the pre-clearance officer under the preclearance laws, and the local officer is authorised to exercise
those powers.

Deferral or refusal of
clearance.

### 11.

(1) A pre-clearance officer may, in his discretion, defer
clearance onwards to a Specified State of any prescribed passenger
or of any goods.
(2) Where clearance is deferred under subsection (1),
the laws of Dominica relating to the departure of persons or the
exportation of goods from Dominica shall apply.
(3) Where a prescribed passenger, does not satisfy a preclearance officer that –
(a) he can be admitted to; or
(b) that his goods can be imported into,
a Specified State in accordance with pre-clearance laws, the
officer may refuse to pre-clear the prescribed passenger or the
goods.

(4) Where clearance is refused under subsection (3), the
competent pre-clearance officer shall immediately inform the
competent local officer of the refusal and supply all data relevant
to that decision.

## PART V

OFFENCES AND PUNISHMENT

### 12.

(1) A person who makes an oral or written statement to
a pre-clearance officer with respect to the pre-clearance of that
person or any goods for entry into a Specified State that the person
knows to be false or deceptive or to contain information that the
person knows is false or deceptive commits an offence and is
liable on summary conviction to a fine of five thousand dollars.

False or deceptive
statements.

(2) A term of imprisonment may not be imposed for
default of payment of a fine imposed under subsection (1).
(3) A complaint for an offence under subsection (1) may
be made at anytime.

### 13.

A person who resists or wilfully obstructs -

Obstruction of officer.

(a) a pre-clearance officer or a local officer in the
execution of the officer’s duty; or
(b) any person lawfully acting in aid of such an officer,
commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction/on
conviction on indictment] to imprisonment for six months.

## PART VI

CIVIL LIABILITY AND IMMUNITY

### 14.

(1) No action or other proceeding of a civil nature may
be brought against a pre-clearance officer in respect of anything
that is done or omitted to be done in good faith by the officer under
this Act.

Immunity from suit.

Chap. 7:80

(2) A pre-clearance officer is not a servant of the State
for the purposes of the State Proceedings Act.
(3) For the purposes of this section, the term “preclearance officer” includes any person who assists a pre-clearance
officer at the officer’s request, but does not include a local officer.

Decisions not
reviewable.

### 15.

The decision of a pre-clearance officer to refuse preclearance, or to refuse the admission of persons to or the
importation of goods into the Specified State is not subject to
judicial review in Dominica.

## SCHEDULE I

Persons who may be Pre-Cleared
(i) members of participating teams as determined by IDI;
(ii) officials, staff and advisers of CWC 2007 Inc., WICB,
ICC, IDI and GCC;
(iii) sponsors appointed by GCC and approved by IDI in
relation to CWC 2007;
(iv) suppliers of products or services appointed as such for
CWC 2007 by CWC 2007 Inc. or GCC;
(v) umpires and match referees appointed as such for CWC
2007\.
(vi) members of the media accredited as such by GCC and
approved by IDI in relation to CWC 2007; and
(vii) aircraft crew members.
(viii) the spouses of the persons identified in (i) to (iv) above.

## SCHEDULE II

Persons and Categories of Persons who may enter a
Pre-clearance Area
For the purposes of section 6(1) (b), the following persons and
categories of persons may enter a pre-clearance area (a) a person who is (i) required to enter the pre-clearance area to
perform functions of their employment; and

(ii) the holder of a restricted area pass for the
airport.
(b) a person who is required to enter the pre-clearance
area in order to perform maintenance or repair of the
airport facilities or emergency services at the airport;
(c) any other person who (i) has permission from the airport operator to
enter the pre-clearance area; or
(ii) is the holder of a restricted area pass for the
airport and has a legitimate requirement to
enter the pre-clearance area at the time in
question.
