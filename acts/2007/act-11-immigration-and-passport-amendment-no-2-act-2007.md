COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA
ACT No. 11 OF 2007

I assent

LS
N. J. O. Liverpool
President
2nd May, 2007
AN ACT TO AMEND THE IMMIGRATION AND
PASSPORT ACT, CHAPTER 18:01.

(Gazetted 10th May, 2007.)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the IMMIGRATION AND PASSPORT
(AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT, 2007

Short title.

Interpretation.
Chap. 18:01.

### 2.

In this Act, the Immigration and Passport Act is referred
to as “the Act”.

Amendment of section 2.

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by deleting the definitions of “advanced passenger information” and “passenger”.

Amendment of section
12A.

IMMIGRATION AND PASSPORT
(AMENDMENT)

ACT 11

### 4.

Section 12 A of the Act is amended (a) by replacing subsections (1) and (2) with the
following:
“(1) In this section “advanced passenger information” means the
information in respect of a passenger, crew member or other occupant transported in the vessel;
“ competent authority” means such person or
entity as the Minister may by Order prescribe;
“domestic space” means the countries listed in
Schedule 2;
“passenger” means any person not being a bona
fide member of the crew, travelling or seeking to
travel on a vessel.
(2) This section applies to a vessel which(a) is expected to arrive in Dominica; or
(b) has left or is expected to leave Dominica
(3) The Master of a vessel to which subsection (2)
applies, shall, at such time as provided in subsection (4), provide to the competent authority in
such form and manner as may be approved by the
Minister, the relevant advance passenger information data set out in Schedule 1, in respect of
the vessel and each person on board.

(4) Where (a) subsection 2 (a) applies, the data referred to
in subsection (3) shall be provided in the case
of
(i)

a commercial aircraft no later than 15
minutes after departure from the last
port of call;

(ii) a private aircraft, no later than 30
minutes from the last port of call;
(iii) a ship arriving from outside the domestic space, no later than 24 hours prior to
arrival; and
(iv) a ship arriving from a destination within
the domestic space, no later than 1 hour
prior to arrival of the vessel from the
last port of call;
(b) subsection (2) (b) applies, then, subject to
subsection (5), the data referred to in subsection (3) shall be provided no later than 15
minutes after departure from Dominica.
(5) Subsection 4(b) does not apply to the departure
of a vessel from Dominica to a destination within
the domestic space.”
(b) in subsection (4) by deleting the word and figure
“subsection (1)” and substituting the words and
figures “subsections (3) or (4)”;
(c) by renumbering subsections (3) and (4) as (6) and (7).
