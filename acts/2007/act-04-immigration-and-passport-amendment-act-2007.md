AN ACT TO AMEND THE IMMIGRATION AND
PASSPORT ACT, CHAPTER 18:01.
(Gazetted 1st February, 2007.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the -

Short title.

IMMIGRATION AND PASSPORT
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2007

### 2.

In this Act, the Immigration and Passport Act is referred
to as “the Act”.

Interpretation.

Amendment of section 2.

### 3.

Section of the Act is amended by inserting, in the appropriate alphabetical order, the following definitions“ “advance passenger information” means the information in
respect of a passenger, crew member or other occupant
in the vessel;”
“ “passenger” means any person not being a bona fide member
of the crew, travelling or seeking to travel on a vessel;”

Insertion of new section
12A.

### 4.

The Act is amended by inserting immediately after
section 8 the following new section:
“Duty to
provide
advance
passenger
information.

12A. (1) Prior to departure of a vessel from the
last port of call for Dominica, the master shall
provide to the immigration authority at the intended port of arrival in Dominica, in such form
and manner as may be approved by the Minister, the data set out in the Schedule in respect of
the vessel and each person on board.
(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to a ship
not exceeding 100 net tons.
(3) Where a master, intentionally or recklessly –
(a) fails to transmit the data in accordance
with subsection (1); or
(b) transmits incomplete or false data,
the master commits an offence and
is liable to a fine of US$100,000.00.
(4) The Minister may waive the requirements of subsection (1) in such circumstances
and on such conditions as the Minister may
prescribe.
