Interpretation

### 2.

In this Act
“Authority” means the Discover Dominica Authority established by section 3;
“Board” means the Board of Directors established by section 5;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Tourism.

## PART II

Functions of the
Authority.

### 3.

(1) There is hereby established an Authority to be known
as the Discover Dominica Authority.
(2) The Authority is a body corporate within the meaning
of Part IX of the Interpretation and General Clauses Act.

### 4.

The functions of the Authority are to (a) promote, assist and facilitate the efficient development
of tourism;
(b) assign and implement suitable marketing strategies
for the effective promotion of the tourism industry;
(c) encourage the establishment of amenities and facilities
appropriate for the proper engagement of Dominica as
a tourist destination;
(d) carry out market intelligence in order to inform the
needs of the tourism industry;
(e) manage, license and supervise the Tourism licensing
programme and other Tourism services in accordance with the Tourism (Regulation and Standards)
Act 2005;
(f) identify training needs for the tourism service
industry; and

(g) do all such other things that in the opinion of the
Authority, would facilitate the proper discharge of
its functions or would be incidental or conducive
thereto.

### 5.

(1) The affairs of the Authority shall be managed by a
Board which shall be responsible for the execution of the policies
of the Authority and for the general administration of the Authority.
(2) The Schedule has effect with respect to the constitution
of the Board and otherwise in relation thereto.

### 11.

With effect from the commencement of this Act (a) any contract entered into by or on behalf of the
National Development Corporation in relation to

### 13.

The Authority may charge such fees for services of the
Authority as are determined by the Board for any service
performed by it.

### 14.

The funds of the Authority shall be applied towards the(a) payment of salaries, wages, fees, allowances,

(b) meeting of obligations and the performance of any

Borrowing powers and

### 16.

(1) The Authority may, with the approval of the Minister
responsible for Finance, borrow money from the Government of
Dominica or any person by way of loan, overdraft or otherwise
upon the terms with respect to the borrowing, issue, transfer and
interest as the Minister responsible for Finance approves for(a) the provision of working capital; or
(b) the performance of the functions of the Authority
under this Act.
(2) The Authority may, with the approval of the Minister,
raise additional working capital.

Guarantee.

### 17.

(1) With the approval of the House of Assembly, the
Minister of Finance may guarantee in such manner and on such
conditions as he thinks fit, the payments of the principal and
interest of any authorised borrowing by the Authority.
(2) Where the Minister of Finance is satisfied that there
has been default in the payment of any principal moneys or
interest guaranteed under this section, he shall direct the repayment
out of the Consolidated Fund for the amount in respect of which
there has been such default.

Disposal of capital assets

### 18.

The Authority shall not, without the prior approval in
writing of the Minister responsible for Finance (a) dispose of its capital assets; or
(b) apply the proceeds of a disposal to which paragraph
(a) refers, to any undertaking.

Accounts and audit.

### 19.

(1) The Authority shall keep accounts of its transactions
in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles and
such accounts shall be audited annually by an auditor appointed
by the Authority with the approval of the Minister.

(2) The Director of Audit may at any time and shall on
the direction of the Minister responsible for Finance, carry out an
investigation or audit of the accounts of the Authority.

### 20.

A director, officer or employee of the Authority shall
afford the Director of Audit or other auditor appointed under
section 19, access to all books, documents, cash and securities of
the Authority and, on request, give to the Director of Audit or
auditor any information within the knowledge of that director or
employee that relates to the operation of the Authority.

### 23.

The Minister may appoint such persons or establish such
committees as he deems fit to carry out or assist the Authority in
carrying out its functions.

### 24.

A person who applies to the Minister for a review of the
decision of the Authority to refuse a licence to that person or to
suspend or to cancel that person’s licence and who is aggrieved
by the decision of the Minister in respect of the refusal, suspension or cancellation may appeal against the Minister’s decision
to a Judge in Chambers whose decision shall be final.

### 25.

The Authority shall keep a register of all tourist
accommodation in respect of which licences have been granted
under this Act.

### 26.

(1) The Authority shall prepare and submit to the Minister an annual Tourism Marketing Plan for the conduct of
tourism promotion.
(2) The Plan referred to in subsection (1) shall (a) serve as a guide for structuring the programme of
tourism promotion and visitor service activities
for the succeeding year; and
(b) specify the aims of the programme and outline the
methods to be employed to accomplish those aims.

Exemption from stamp
duty and income tax.

### 27.

(1) No stamp duty shall be charged in respect of any
instrument by or on behalf or in favour of the Authority in cases
where, but for this exception, the Authority would be liable to pay
the duty chargeable in respect of such instrument.
(2) The Authority shall not be liable for the payment of
any income tax.

Regulations.

### 28.

The Authority may, with the approval of the Minister,
make Regulations (a) in respect of the imposition of fees or charges for
services rendered by the Authority;

(b) in respect of the procedure with regard to licensing of
tourist accommodations; and
(c) generally for giving effect to the provisions of this
Act.
