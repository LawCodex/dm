AN ACT TO SANCTION CERTAIN PAYMENTS FROM
THE CONSOLIDATED FUND OF DOMINICA IN
EXCESS OF THE SUM PROVIDED BY LAW FOR
THE SERVICES OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF
DOMINICA DURING THE YEAR ENDING THE
THIRTIETH DAY OF JUNE 2007.
(Gazetted 6th December, 2007.)
BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth of
Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
(2006/2007) (NO. 3) ACT 2007.

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT 13

Legalisation of
unforeseen expenditure.
Schedule.

### 2.

(1) The sum of thirty-four million, two hundred and
eighty-six thousand, six hundred and sixteen dollars and fifty five
cents ($34,286,616.55) as described in the Schedule was withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during the
financial year ending on the 30th day of June, 2007.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen governmental expenditure in anticipation of a
grant being made by the House of Assembly authorising the issue
of the specified sum of money from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of July, 2006 and appropriated to the
services set out in the Schedule.
