29th July, 2010
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE SERVICES OF THE
COMMONWEALTH OF DOMINICA FOR THE
YEAR ENDING ON THE 30TH DAY OF JUNE,
2011\.
(Gazetted 5th August, 2010)

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the Commonwealth
of Dominica as follows:

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the-

Short Title.

2010/2011 APPROPRIATION ACT 2010.

### 2.

The issue from the Consolidated Fund of the sum of four
hundred and forty million, three hundred and seven thousand,

Appropriation.

Schedule.

Payment from the
Consolidated Fund.
Schedule.

Commencement.

APPROPRIATION

ACT 17

nine hundred and twenty-two dollars ($440,307,922) is hereby
authorised for meeting the expenditure for services for the year
ending June 30th, 2011 under the Head of Expenditure specified
in the Schedule, and the sum specified in the Schedule opposite
to the Head is hereby appropriated out of the said sum for the
purposes specified in relation to the Head of the Estimates for the
said year.

### 3.

The Accountant General, is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon warrant of the Minister for Finance, to pay
out of the Consolidated Fund in respect of the several services
specified in the Schedule, the several monies appropriated in the
manner as the said warrant shall direct without further order or
formality.

### 4.

This Act, is deemed to have come into force on the 1st day
of July 2010.
