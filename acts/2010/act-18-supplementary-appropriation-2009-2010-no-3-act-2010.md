### 2.

(1) The sum of fifty-eight million, four hundred and
thirty-three thousand, nine hundred and eighty-five dollars and
ninety-eight cents (EC$58,433,985.98) as described in the
Schedule was withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of
Dominica during the financial year ending on the 30th day of
June 2010.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to
defray unforeseen Government expenditure in anticipation of
a grant being made by the House of Assembly authorising
issue of the specified sum of money made from the
Consolidated Fund of Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of July 2009 and appropriated to
the services set out in the Schedule.
