### 2.

(1) The sum of twelve million, five hundred and seventy
thousand, two hundred and thirty-three dollars and fifty-one cents
(EC$12,570,233.51) as described in the Schedule was withdrawn
from the Consolidated Fund of Dominica during the financial year
ending 30th day of June 2011.
(2) The sum specified in subsection (1) was used to defray
unforeseen Government expenditure in anticipation of a grant
being made by the House of Assembly authorising issue of the
specified sum of money from the consolidated Fund of Dominica.
(3) The sum specified in subsection (1) was lawfully
withdrawn from the 1st day of July 2010 and appropriated to the
services set out in the Schedule.
