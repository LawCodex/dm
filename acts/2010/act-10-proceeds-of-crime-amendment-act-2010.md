### 1.

PROCEEDS OF CRIME (AMENDMENT) ACT 2010.

Short Title

Interpretation

Amendment of section 2

ACT 10

### 2.

In this Act the Proceeds of Crime Act Chap 12:29 is
referred to as “the Act.”

### 3.

Section 2 of the Act is amended by
(a) inserting a definition of “financial institution”between
the terms ‘the court’ and forfeiture order’ as follows:
“financial institution” means any person whose
regular occupation or business is the carrying on
of any activity listed in the Schedule II;”
(b) deleting the word “the” appearing immediately before
the word “Schedule” in the definition of the term
“scheduled offences” and inserting the figure “I”
immediately after the word “Schedule.”

Deletion of Scheduleand
insertion of new
Schedule
