Application of Act.

### 3.

(1) This Act and every other customs enactment apply in
relation to –
(a) the control of coasting trade;
(b) the importation and exportation of goods;
(c) the arrival of persons from outside the territorial
waters of Dominica; and
(d) the departure of persons to a destination outside
the territorial waters of Dominica.

(2) The requirements imposed by Parts V, VI and VII do
not apply–
(a) to a vessel or an aircraft owned by or in the
service of the Government, when being used for
the purpose of customs, police or coastguard; and
(b) where the Comptroller so directs and for such
periods and subject to such conditions and
restrictions as he sees fit to impose, to any vessel
or aircraft owned by or in the service of the
government of any other country.
(3) Subject to subsection (4) (a) sections 29 to 36 do not apply to any member of
any disciplined force or any conveyance under
the control of any disciplined force during such
time as that person or conveyance is required to
respond to an emergency; and
(b) sections 21 to 22 and 24 to 28 do not apply to any
member of any disciplined force or any conveyance
under the control of any disciplined force during
such time as that person or conveyance is involved
in an emergency described in subsection (7)(a) or
(c).
(4) Where a vessel or an aircraft under the control of any
disciplined force which is involved in, or is required to respond to,
an emergency departs from or returns to Dominica, the disciplined
force shall, within a period of 24 hours or such longer period as the
Comptroller may allow (a) notify the Comptroller of the departure of the
vessel or aircraft from Dominica or the arrival of
the vessel or aircraft in Dominica; and
(b) provide to the Comptroller such details relating to
goods and persons on the vessel or aircraft as the
Comptroller specifies.

(5) The power of the Comptroller under subsection (4) to
determine a period or to specify details required may be exercised
generally or in respect of any particular case.
(6) The Minister may make regulations prescribing the
circumstances in which and the conditions under which the powers
conferred under Part XIII may be exercised in relation to (a) a member of a disciplined force;
(b) any vessel under the control of the Police Service; or
(c) access to any security area for the purpose of the
defence and security of the State.
(7) For the purposes of this section, “emergency” means Chap 15:03
Chap 15:02

(a) a national emergency within the meaning of the

### 181.

(1) In this section, references to “prohibited signal” or
“prohibited message” shall be construed as references to any
signal or message connected with the smuggling or intended
smuggling of goods into or out of Dominica.
(2) Where a person, by any means, transmits a prohibited
signal or a prohibited message from any place in Dominica or from
any vessel or aircraft, to a person in any other vessel or aircraft—
(a) the person commits an offence and is liable on
summary conviction to a fine of $10,000 or
imprisonment for one year; and
(b) any equipment or apparatus used by such person
for the transmission of the prohibited signal or the
prohibited message is liable to forfeiture.
(3) Subsection (2) applies whether or not the person for
whom the prohibited signal or message is intended is in a position
to receive it or is actually engaged at that time in smuggling.
(4) For the purposes of this section, where a vessel to
which a prohibited signal is sent or transmitted–
(a) changes its course;
(b) if at anchor, weighs anchor; or

(c) sends a signal in apparent response,
the vessel is deemed to be a smuggling vessel, unless the contrary
is proved.
(5) Where a proper officer or a police officer has
reasonable grounds to believe that a prohibited signal or a
prohibited message is being transmitted or is about to be transmitted
from any vessel, aircraft, vehicle, building or other place in
Dominica, the officer may—
(a) board or enter that vessel, aircraft, vehicle, building
or place; and
(b) take such steps as are reasonably necessary to
prevent the transmission of such signal or
message.
