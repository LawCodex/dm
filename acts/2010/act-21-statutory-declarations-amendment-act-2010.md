## Chapter 5:53

### 2.

In this Act, the Statutory Declarations Act is referred to
as “the Act”.

Amendment of
Section 2.

### 3.

The Act is amended in section 2 by deleting the words
“two thousand five hundred dollars and to imprisonment for six
months” and substituting the words “five thousand dollars and to
imprisonment for one year”.
