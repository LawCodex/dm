8\. Any landlord upon whom, pursuant to section 6, a demand for

9\. For the purpose of section 3, a notice shall be deemed to be

shall be deemed to be the landlord of any premises if such a person is
registered as the owner or agent for purposes of the Tenancies and Rent
Control Act.

12\. Unless the Tribunal otherwise determines, where premises are
damaged by hurricane to such an extent so as to render the premises
untenantable, a tenant of same shall not be liable for payment of rent in

respect of such premises while it remains in the untenantable condition
and any rent paid by the tenant, shall be recoverable by him from the
landlord, upon an application for an order to that effect being made to
the Tribunal.
