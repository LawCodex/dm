1\. This Act may be cited as the TEXTILES QUOTA ACT.
Interpretation.
15 of 1937.

2\. (1) In this Act "foreign country" means any country or territory not being a part of the
Commonwealth;
"prescribed" means prescribed by Rules made hereunder;
"quota" means the quantity of textiles which may be imported during
any period in accordance with an Order made by the Minister
under section 3;
"quota period" means any period in respect of which any quota has been
fixed by the Minister hereunder;
"regulated textiles" means any textiles in respect of which any quota has
been fixed by the Minister hereunder;
"textiles" means any textile goods, and any class of textile goods
manufactured in a foreign country; and for the purposes of this
Act any textile goods in respect of which a quota has been fixed
shall be deemed to be manufactured in a foreign country unless
accompanied by a certificate of Commonwealth origin in the
manner and form prescribed.

(2) This Act shall be construed as one with the Customs
(Control and Management) Act and the Customs Import and Export
Tariff Ordinance or any similar Customs Duties Act for the time being
in force, and all rights, duties, powers and privileges conferred thereunder upon any government or customs officer in relation to importation,
examination, powers and provisions whatsoever contained herein shall
apply mutatis mutandis to the importation, examination, entry and
forfeiture of regulated textiles.

3\. (1) The Minister may by Order fix the total quantity of textiles
manufactured in any foreign country which may be imported during any
quota period.
