SUBSTANCES PROHIBITION ACT
AN ACT to prohibit the use ofoestrogens, arsenical or antimonial
substances in poultry intended for local consumption or for
export.

USE OF OESTROGENS, ARSENICAL OR ANTIMONIAL
SUBSTANCES PROHIBITION ACT.
2\. In this Act "arsenical" means any substance or compound containing arsenic or its
derivatives;
"antimonial" means any substance or compound containing antimony
or its derivatives;
"carcase" means the carcase or any part thereof of any domestic fowl,
turkey, goose, duck, pheasant, guinea-fowl or partridge;
"export" includes disposal for export;
"oestrogens" means the hormones hexoestrol or stilboestrol or any other
similar hormone in its natural or synthetic form;
"poultry" means birds of the following species, that is to say, domestic
fowls, turkeys, geese, ducks, guinea-fowls, pigeons, pheasants
and partridges.

3\. From and after the coming into operation of this Act, no person
shall use oestrogens or any arsenical or antimonial substance in the
treatment of poultry intended for local consumption or for export.

4\. (1) No person shall export from the State any live poultry or any
carcase which has been treated with oestrogens, or with any arsenical or
antimonial substance.

(2) No person shall import into the State for re-export or accept
delivery forre-export of any poultry or carcase unless it is accompanied
by a certificate signed by a duly authorised officer of the Government
of the country of origin stating that the poultry or carcases were not
treated with oestrogens or with any arsenical or antimonial substances.
