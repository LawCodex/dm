REGISTRA nON OF BIRTHS AND DEATHS
AN ACT to authorise the making of new registers of births and
deaths to replace those destroyed by fire at the High Court in
Roseau, Dominica on the sixteenth day of June, nineteen
hundred and seventy-nine and for matters connected therewith and incidental thereto.

1\. (1) This Act may be cited as theREGISTRATION OF BIRTHS AND DEATHS
(NEW REGISTERS) ACf.
(2) This Act shall be read together with, deemed part of and
construed as part of the Registration of Births and Deaths Act (hereinafter referred to as "the principal law").
2\. In this Act "destroyed" means lost, destroyed, or rendered indecipherable by, or
in consequence of the fire;
"the fire" means the fire which destroyed the High Court Building,
Roseau, Dominica, on the said date, and includes all damage
consequent thereon or incidental thereto;
"new registers" means the register books made under the authority of
this Act;
"old registers" means the register books for Dominica under the
principal law in existence immediately prior to the said date;
"Registrar General" has the same meaning assigned to it under the
principal law;
"said date" means the 16th June 1979.

7\. If upon a request in writing made by the Registrar General for
any purpose mentioned in section 6 any person without reasonable
cause refuses or neglects to comply with the request, the Registrar
General shall apply to the Court for an order directing such person to

8\. (1) In any case where a person is unable to obtain copies of his
birth or baptismal certificates or other documentary evidence of his date
of birth he may make application in writing to the Registrar General for
registration of his birth.
(2) The application shall contain the name and date of birth of
the applicant and other particulars relevant to the birt!t and shall be
supported by a statutory declaration made by two persons who witnessed or were present or have personal knowledge of the birth setting
forth the true facts of the birth.
(3) Upon production to the Registrar General of the application
and the statutory declaration he shall register the birth.
(4) For the purposes of this Act the statutory declaration may be
prepared and administered by the Registrar General without payment
of a fee, or by a notary public.
