AN ACT to authorise the making of a new Register of Marriages to
replace that destroyed by fire at the High Court building in
Roseau, Dominica on the sixteenth day of June, nineteen
hundred and seventy-nine and for matters connected therewith and incidental thereto.

1\. (1) This Act may be cited as theMARRIAGE (NEW REGISTER) ACf.

Ch.35:01.

(2) This Act shall be read together with, deemed part of and
construed as part of the Marriage Act, (hereinafter referred to as "the
principal law").

"destroyed" means lost, destroyed or rendered undecipherable by or in
consequence of the fire;
"the fire" means the fire which destroyed the High Court Building,
Roseau, Dominica, on the said date, and includes all damage
thereon or incidental thereto;
"new register" means the marriage register book for Dominica under
the principal law in existence immediately prior to the said
date;
"Registrar General" has the same meaning assigned to it under the
principal law;

3\. (l) The Registrar General is hereby directed to make a new
register for Dominica and for that purpose is hereby given all such
necessary authority as is not expressly conferred by this Act.
(2) This Act applies only in cases where marriages which were
registered in the old register have been destroyed.

4\. (1) The Registrar General may appoint fit and proper persons
to compile a new register for Dominica, and may remove such persons
from such office and in every case of vacancy therein fill up the same.
Every such person shall receive remuneration at the rate of fifty cents
for each marriage duly registered by him and certified by the Registrar
General.

7\. If upon a request in writing made by the Registrar General for
any purpose mentioned in section 6 any person without reasonable
cause refuses or neglects to comply with the request, the Registrar
General shall apply to the Court for an order directing such person to
comply with the request.
8\. (1) In any case where a person is unable to produce documentary evidence of his marriage the Registrar General shall, upon production to him by the person requiring the registration of a statutory
declaration setting forth the names and ages of the parties to the said
marriage, register the said marriage; and any two persons who witnessed or were present at the marriage ceremony shall be required to
make a statutory declaration to this effect.
(2) For the purposes of this Act the statutory declaration may be
prepared and administered by the Registrar General without payment
of a fee, or by a notary public.
