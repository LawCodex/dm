15\. (1) In the course of construction and for the more effective
working of the undertaking, the Company may enter upon and remove
from any public or private land, any tree or any branch, bough or other
part of a tree growing on such land within one hundred feet of any
electric line and which may tend to interfere with, endanger or otherwise
prejudicially affect the working of the undertaking; but the Company
shall not, except with the consent of the owner or occupier of such land,
enter upon any private land under this section until after the expiration
of seven days' notice in writing given to the owner or occupier thereof
or posted up conspicuously thereon. If the owner or occupier, within
seven days from the selVice or posting up of the notice, gives written
notice of his objection thereto, the matter shall be referred by the
Company to the Minister. The Company shall not enter upon the private
land in question if the Minister, within thirty days of being notified by
the Company of any such objection as aforesaid, so directs; but where
any condition exists which is dangerous is interrupting or is threatening
to interrupt the supply of electricity in Dominica or any part thereof, the
Company may immediately enter upon any private land without the
consent of the owner or occupier and take whatever action is necessary
to establish safe conditions or to ensure the continuity of the supply of
electricity; and, whenever the Company takes such action it shall within
three days infonn the owner or occupier of the land in question (either
by selVice of a written notice on him or by posting up conspicuously a
notice on such land) of the action taken.
(2) Except with the written consent of the Company, which
consent shall not be unreasonably withheld and shall be free of charge,

Company may apply to the Minister for the removal or adjustment of the
building or structure in question and, after making any such inquiry as
he thinks necessary, the Minister may make such order as he thinks fit.
Every such order may, by leave of a Judge of the High Court, be

SUPPLY OF ELECTRICITY
\*(A) LIGHTING RATE
Applies to all electricity supplied for electric lighting to premises to
which the industrial rate is applicable and upon the consumer concerned
so requesting in writing, to all electricity supplied to any other premises
at 60.0 cents per unit subject to a minimum monthly charge of $20.00.

Applies to all electricity supplied to premises used exclusively for
private residential purposes in cases where the consumer concerned had
not requested in writing to be charged at the Lighting Rate as follows:
A charge of 47.5 cents per unit for up to 50 units in any month and
56.0 cents per unit for each unit in excess of 50 units in any month
subject to a minimum monthly charge of $2.50.

Applies to all electricity supplied otherwise than for electric lighting
to any premises on which there are installed for the purpose of industry,
electric machinery or equipment having an aggregate maximum power
rating of not less than 5kV A.
The industrial rate shall be as follows:
(i) a service charge of $4.00 per month per kV A of installed
capacity; and
(ll) for electricity supplied between the hours of 6:00 a.m. and
10:00 p.m. at the rate of 52.6 cents per unit; and
(ill) for electricity supplied between the hours of 10:00 p.m. and
6:00 a.m. at the rate of 47.8 cents per unit:
Provided that a consumer to qualify for the rate under (ill) shall give
the Company not less than three (3) months notice of his intention.

Applies to all electricity supplied to premises used as hotels and
guest houses with a minimum number of ten rooms as follows:
A service charge of $4.00 per month per kV A of installed capacity
plus 52.6 cents per unit subject to a minimum monthly charge of the
service charge.

Applies to all electricity supplied to any premises not being used
exclusively for private residential purposes and not being premises to
which the Industrial or Hotel Rates are applicable in cases where the

consumer concerned had not requested in writing to be charged at the
Lighting Rate as follows:
A service charge of $4.00 per month per kVA of installed capacity
plus 60.0 cents per unit subject to a minimum monthly charge of the
service charge.

In addition to the charges set out above the Company shall add or
deduct per unit consumed a fuel surcharge which shall be calculated
from the following formula:
The total number of imperial gallons of diesel fuel consumed in all
the Company's generating stations in Dominica during the calendar
month immediately preceding the calendar month in which the meters
are read;
Multiplied by The current price less the base price Divided byThe total units sold by the Company in Dominica during the calendar

In this context "base price" means $1.4066 per imperial gallon; and
"current price" means the average price payable per imperial gallon for
diesel fuel delivered to the Company at its power stations during
the calendar month immediately preceding the calendar month
in which meters are read.
(H) CONSUMER DEPOSIT
Consumers are required to make a deposit equivalent to an
estimated two months' electricity consumption. This amount is placed

in a Deposit Account in the consumer's name and held as security for
moneys due to the Company. It gains interest at the rate of 4 per cent
per annum and is refundable on termination of the service and upon
settlement of all amounts owing.
(I) RECONNECTION

FEE

A fee of $22.00 is payable for reconnection after disconnection for
non-payment of electricity bills. No fee is payable by a consumer whose
supply was disconnected as a result of hurricane damage and who has
settled all previous electricity accounts up to date.
(1) TRANSFER OF SERVICE
A consumer requesting a transfer of service from one location to
another is required to pay a fee of $5.00 provided that the new location
is within 100 feet (30 metres) of the nearest suitable pole. The extra
material required for supply at a distance in excess of 100 feet must be
paid for separately.
(K) SERVICE CALL
A fee of $5.00 is payable where the fault proves to be in the
consumer's installation.
(L) METER TESTING
For testing a meter at the consumer's request, a fee of $10.00 is
payable. This charge will be refunded if the meter proves to be incorrect
but will be retained if the meter proves to be correct.
