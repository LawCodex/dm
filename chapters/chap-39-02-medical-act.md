Time of examination.

Appointment of
board of examiners.

Materia Medica and shall include the law on the subject of the sale of
drugs and poisons contained in Part VII of the Act, and of the applicant's
knowledge of the poisons contained in Schedule C, Parts I and II, of the
Act, the symptoms produced by each poison and the appropriate
antidote in each case.
