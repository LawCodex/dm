1\. (I) Expenditures incurred by any person for the construction,
acquisition or improvement of assets described in subparagraph (2) which are
not disqualified by reason of subparagraph (3), (4) or (5) shall qualify for
income tax credit in the year of assessment in the basis period for which the
expenditures are incurred.

Subject to subparagraph (3), (4) or (5), the investment lax
credit is allowable with respect to expenditures incurred by a person for the
construction, acquisition or improvement of assets listed in the Second Schedule

(3)
The investment tax credit is not allowable for expenditures incurred by a person in a basis period for a year of assessment in which
the person is granted an income tax holiday unless the expenditures are
unrelated to the activity with respect to which the income tax holiday is granted.

The investment tax credit is not allowable for expenditures incurred by a person for the acquisition of an asset with respect to which
a family member or a related company has been allowed a capital allowance
under the Second Schedule of the Income Tax Act, for the assessment year 1992
or any preceding assessment years.

AMOUNT OF THE INVESTMENT
TAX CREDIT
1\. (1) Subject to paragraph 2, the amount of the investment tax credit
allowable in an assessment year shall equal ten per cent of the cost of
construction, acquiring or improving assets described in Part II paragraph 1
subparagraph (2), as modified by paragraph 1 subparagraphs (3), (4) and (5) of

## Part II, which are put in use in the basis period for the year of assessment

(2) Notwithstanding subparagraph (1) if an expenditure is incurred
with respect to an asset in a basis period which is after the basis period in which
the asset is put in use, the expenditure is allowable for the assessment year of
the later basis period.
2\. The amount of the investment tax credit allowable under paragraph 1
shall not exceed the greater of fifty thousand dollars or fifty per cent of the
income tax liability of any person or company for an assessment year.
3\. Any investment tax credits which are allowable under paragraph 2
shall be carried forward to the next years of assessment and treated as credits
qualifying under paragraph 1 subject to the limits of paragraph 2 in the later
assessment years until the credits have been fully allowed.
PARTlY
DISPOSAL OF ASSETS
1\. Where an investment tax credit has been allowed to any person with
respect to expenditures for the construction, acquisition or improvement of

assets under this Schedule, and any such asset is disposed of in the basis period
for any year of assessment an investment disposal charge shall be made under
this Part.
2\. Where the disposal value of an asset equals or exceeds the amount of
the expenditure with respect to which an investment tax credit was allowed the
investment disposal charge shall equal the amount of the investment tax credit
previously allowed in respect of that asset.
3\. Where the disposal value of an asset is less than the amount of the
expenditure with respect to which an investment tax credit was allowed the
investment disposal charge shall be ten per cent of the disposal value of the
asset.
4\. The investment disposal charge made on any person shall be treated as
an income tax and shall be added to the income tax assessment for the year of
assessment in the basis period for which the asset is disposed of.
S. Where a person has been granted an investment tax credit in respect of
any asset and that person ceases to use the asset for a purpose qualifying under

## Part II paragraph I, the cessation of use shall be deemed to be a disposal of that

asset and the investment disposal charge, based on the disposal value of the
asset at the time of cessation of the qualifying use, shall be imposed in the year
of assessment in the basis period in which the cessation of qualifying use
occurs.
