32\. (1) It shall be a condition in every agreement for any loan made
by the Bank for the cultivation of crops and the expenses of reaping and
making merchantable such crops, that all the crops and produce reaped
or gathered from the land and the by-products thereof shall, until the
advance has been repaid, be dealt with and disposed of as the Bank may
direct, and until so disposed of, shall be held by the person obtaining the
loan in trust for the Bank. Any person to whom a loan has been made,
who disposes of any such crops or produce or by-products except in the
manner directed by the Bank or by this Act, is liable on summary
conviction to a fine of seven hundred and fifty dollars or to imprisonment for six months.
(2) It shall be a condition in every such agreement that the Bank
may enter into an agreement with any person for the disposal of any
crops or produce or any by-products thereof belonging to any person to
whom loans have been made under this Act. The last-mentioned
agreement shall provide for the sale without undue delay of the crop or

produce and the immediate payment to the Bank of the proceeds of the
sale or such part thereof as the Bank thinks fit to the credit of the lastmentioned person.
(3) Any person so entrusted with the disposal of any crop or
produce or by-products thereof of any person obtaining a loan under this
Act who fails to pay the proceeds of sale to the Bank as provided in
subsection (2) is liable on summary conviction to a fine of one thousand
five hundred dollars or to imprisonment for six months.

thousand and eighty dollars ($1,080) and not more than six

in forty-five months, including nine months grace period, in the

agro-processing and on-farm infrastructure; and (Hi) made at an

period; and (Hi) made at an interest rate of one percent less than the
one existing for individual sub-borrowers in accordance with
paragraph l(iii) of this Schedule.

twenty-seven thousand dollars ($27,000) each on a half-yearly
basis; (ii) be repaid in a period not longer than ten years, including
three years grace period; and (iii) be charged an interest rate not
exceeding four and one-half per cent (41/2%) per annum.

hundred and thirty-five thousand dollars ($135,000); (ii) repayable in seven years, including two years grace period; and (iii)

crop liens or both.
