2\. No person shall be appointed to the fire and ambulance services
as a fire officer who (a) is less than eighteen years of age or more than twenty-eight

years of age;
(b) does not satisfactorily pass a medical examination as to his

bodily fitness and an educational examination as to his mental
attainments;
(c) does not produce satisfactory proof of good character.
Probationary
period of fire
officers.

3\. (1) During the first twenty-four months after his appointment,
every fire officer shall be deemed to be on probation, and if, during that
period it is found that he is unlikely to become an efficient fire officer,
his appointment shall be terminated.
(2) If at the end of the aforesaid period of probation the
appointment of a fire officer is not terminated, his appointment shall be
confirmed.
(3) Any fire officer considered to be medically unfit or unsuitable
for retention as a fire officer shall be discharged.
(4) There shall be maintained at fire and ambulance services
headquarters a record of every fire officer's service which shall contain
particulars of examinations, promotions, rewards, commendations,
increments, allowances, leave, discipline, and medical history.
