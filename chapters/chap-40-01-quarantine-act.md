It rests with the authority of the country of destination to decide in what
manner and at what place disinfection shall be carried out and what methods

including rags not carried as merchandise in bulk, may be destroyed by fIre.

9\. (1) No bedding or clothing belonging to any person who is
suffering from any disease mentioned in either of the Schedules A and
B, or coming from any ship which has arrived from an infected place,
shall be landed without the permission of the health officer. Any person
who lands any such bedding or clothing without the permission of the
health officer is guilty of an offence.
(2) The health officer may order that any such bedding or
clothing which he permits to be landed shall be disinfected.
10\. Any person who resists, obstructs, molests or assaults, or who
assists any person to resist, obstlUet, molest or assault any other person
when that other person is acting in the exercise of his powers under or
in virtue of these Regulations, is guilty of an offence.
11\. Any person who is guilty of an offence under these Regulations
is liable on summary conviction to a fine of ten thousand dollars and in
default of payment thereof to imprisonment for six months.
