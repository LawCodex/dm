AN ACT to declare the terms and conditions applicable to local
loans authorised to be raised by the Government of Dominica.

1\. This Act may be cited as the GENERAL LOCAL LOAN ACT.
2\. In this Act "Minister" means the Minister responsible for Finance;
"prescribed" means prescribed by the Act or by any Rules or Regulations made thereunder;
"West Indies" means any or all of the tenitories of Saint Christopher and
Nevis, Anguilla, Antigua, Montserrat, Dominica, Saint Lucia,
Saint Vincent, Grenada, Trinidad and Tobago, Guyana, Barbados, British Virgin Islands, Jamaica, Belize and the Bahamas.
3\. This Act shall be applicable only to loans raised in the West
Indies.

22\. (1) All sums for the time being due or authorised to be raised on Priority of loans.
or in respect of any debentures issued in respect of the same loan under
this Act shall be paid without any preference the one over the other by
reason of the priority of date of any such debentures.
(2) Where more than one loan has been raised under this Act,
the sums for the time being due or authorised to be raised on orin respect
of any debentures issued in respect of each loan shall take priority
according to the date of such loan.
(3) Where any sum of money is authorised to be borrowed in
the manner provided by this Act, the sum may, unless it is otherwise
prescribed, be raised as one loan or several loans as may be considered
most convenient by the Minister so that the aggregate amount authorised to be borrowed is not exceeded.
(4) The date of each loan shall, with a view to the priority of the
loan and to the period within which the loan is to be discharged and for
the other purposes of this Act so far as relates to that period, be fixed by
the Minister, and may be so fixed irrespectively of the dates of the
particular debentures issued in respect of the loan, so that the period
within which the loan is required to be discharged is not exceeded.
