Public Telephones

AN ACT to provide for the establishment of a telephone system by
Cable and Wireless (West Indies) Limited.
Commencement.
[34/1968].

1\. This Act may be cited as the PUBLIC TELEPHONES ACT.
2\. (l) In this Act "Company" means Cable and Wireless (West Indies) Limited being a
Company incorporated under the laws of Great Britain with
limited liability, and having its registered office at Mercury
House, Theobalds Road, London, W.C.I, and the successors,
lessees or assigns of the said Company, and includes any person
duly authorised by the Company to do any act on its behalf;
"land" means land not being a street or road and not being land by the
side and forming part of a street or road, and includes land laid
out for and proposed by the owner to be converted into a street
or road;
"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Communications;
"post" includes a pole, standard, stay, strut or other contrivance for
carrying, suspending or supporting a telephone;
"telephone" includes a wire or wires, a conductor or other means used
for the purpose of telephonic or electric signalling communication with or without any casing, coating, covering, tube, pole or
pipe enclosing, surrounding or supporting the same, and any
insulators, instruments or apparatus connected therewith;
"telephone service" includes installing, maintaining and operating a
telephone system, and the utilisation of the same for the purpose
of transmitting the sound of the human voice or electric signals
over, through or by means of an electric wire or other electric
device;

"tree" means any tree, wood, bamboo, underwood or other produce of

(2) Expressions referring to the construction or maintenance of

8\. Subject to the restrictions and provisions mentioned below, the
Company may place and maintain telephones over any land or building
in Dominica, and may enter any land in Dominica and place and
maintain posts thereon, and repair or renew any posts now thereon, or
placed thereon under the provisions of this Act and may lop, trim or cut
down any tree that in its opinion is likely to damage or obstruct the
telephone service.

Restrictions on
exercise of
power over
private land.

9\. In the exercise of the powers conferred by section 8 the Company shall be subject to the following restrictions:
(a) it shall not place any post or telephone on any private
land that shall cause interference with or obstruction to
the occupier of any lands in any business or cultivation
carried on upon such land.
(b) it shall not, without the consent of the owner or occupier,
place or maintain any telephone on or over any building
at a lesser height than ten feet from the ground.
(c) in placing the telephone over any building it shall not
place it at a lesser height above the roof than six feet if
the owner or occupier shall so object.
(d) it shall not place any post on any private land, or place
any telephone over any private land or building or cut
down any trees thereon, until it has given at least
twenty-one days notice in writing to the owner or
occupier of the land or building of its intention to do so
specifying the work to be done, but no such notice shall
be given until the Company having made every reasonable endeavour to meet any objection by the owner or
occupier fails to reach agreement as to the said work
with the owner or occupier.
(e) on receipt of any notice of the intention of the Company
to place any poston any private land, or to cut down any

trees thereon, the owner or occupier or his agent may,
within fourteen days thereafter, give notice in writing
of his objection through the Company to the Minister
against the execution of the same or any part thereof,
whereupon the Company shall not proceed with the
said work or such part thereof as is objected to until
directed by the Minister to do so.
