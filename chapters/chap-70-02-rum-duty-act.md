## PART I

PRELIMINARY
1\. This Act may be cited as the RUM DUTY ACT.
"cart" means cart, carriage, truck and hand or wheelbarrow;
"cattle" means any animal used for the conveyance of spirits;
"City" means the city of Roseau;
"Comptroller" means the Comptroller of Customs and Excise;
"constable" means any police or other constable;
"depot" means the public warehouse or warehouses for storing spirits;
"depot officer" means the officer in charge of the depot;
"distiller" means the person in whose name any still licence is obtained;
"distillery" means the liquor 10ft,still-house, rum-cellar, rum-store and
every place, whether open or enclosed, connected with the
manufacture of spirits or where the distillation is carried on;
"gallon" means an Imperial gallon;
"manager" means any person who resides upon any estate for the
purpose of superintending its cultivation and the manufacture
of its produce;
"Master" includes every person having command of any vessel;
"Act" includes Regulations made under this Act;
"package" means any article in which spirits can be carried, removed or
conveyed;

"plantation" means any plantation or estate whatever, either under
sugar-cane cultivation or otherwise;
"prescribed" means prescribed by this Act or any Regulations made
under this Act;
"rum" means any description of spirits the manufacture of the Island,
and includes Bay Rum;
"spirits" means rum and all other spirituous liquors of every description
whether the rum or spirituous liquors are mixed with any other
material or not;
"still" means any still strictly so called and any apparatus attached or
belonging to the same or used for distilling spirits;
"stock book" means the book to be kept and produced wherein is set
forth the particulars required to be recorded where a still licence
is granted;
"unpermitted spirits" means any spirits the produce of this Island on
which duty has not been paid or which is removed contrary to
the provisions of this Act;
"vessel" includes every description of ship, vessel, canoe, boat or flat
used in navigation;
"wash" means any liquor or other material from which rum is distilled.

due shall be incurred in respect of every week or part thereof during
which the payment is in default.
