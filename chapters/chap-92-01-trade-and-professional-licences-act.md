merchant, store-keeper or shop-keeper;
"trade" includes the sale, barter or exchange of goods, or disposal of
goods by an agent on commission.

12\. The Comptroller or any police officer or Justice of the Peace
may demand from any person carrying on the business of a huckster the
production of a huckster's licence and may inspect the same; and if that
person refuses, fails or neglects without lawful excuse to produce, or
cause to be produced, the licence for inspection he is guilty of an offence
against this Act.

Metal plates or
badges for
hucksters.
[9 of 1967].

13\. Every person selling, carrying or exposing goods for sale under
the authority of a huckster's licence shall be supplied with a metal plate
or badge carrying a number and the year of the licence, furnished by the
Comptroller, and shall produce the metal plate or badge for inspection
whenever required to do so by the Comptroller, a Justice of the Peace or
a police officer; and if that person fails to produce the metal plate or
badge when requested to do so he is guilty of an offence against this Act.

badge.

14\. In the event of the plate or badge being lost, the huckster shall
infomJ. the Comptroller thereof, and the Comptroller, upon being
satisfied that the plate or badge has been lost, shall issue a new plate or
badge to him upon payment of the sum of ten dollars.

15\. A licensed huckster may employ an assistant to carry on the
business of a huckster on his behalf; but no more than one person shall
carry on the business of a huckster at the same time under the same
licence.
