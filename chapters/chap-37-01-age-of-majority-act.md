AN ACT to amend the law relating to the age of majority by 18 of 1983.
reducing that age from twenty-one to eighteen years and for
purposes connected therewith.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the AGE OF MAJORITY ACf.

### 2.

In this Act "infant" means a person under the age of eighteen Interpretation.
years, and the expression "infancy", "minor" or "minority" shall be
construed accordingly.

### 3.

(1) As from the commencement of this Act a person shall attain
full age on attaining the age of eighteen years, and a person shall be
deemed to have attained full age on that date if he has then already
attained the age of eighteen years but not twenty-one years.
(2) The provisions of subsection (1) applies for the purposes of
any rule or law, and for the construction of the expressions "full age",
"infant", "infancy", "minor", "minority" and similar expressions in (a) any statutory

provision, whether passed or made before, on or after the date on which this Act comes into
force;

(b) any deed, will or other instrument

of whatever nature
(not being a statutory provision) made on or after that
date.

(3) In any written law in force on the coming into operation of
this Act, for any reference to the age of twenty-one years there shall be
substituted a reference to the age of eighteen years; but this subsection
shall not apply in such cases as may be prescribed by Order of the
President subject to negative resolution of the House of Assembly.
