AN ACT to provide for the establishment of a Food and Nutrition
Council in the Commonwealth of Dominica and matters connected therewith.

1\. This Act may be cited as the FOOD AND NUTRITION COUNCIL ACT.
2\. In this Act "Council" means the Food and Nutrition Council established by section
3 (1);
"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Health.

Duty of Council

9\. (1) The Council shall cause to be kept proper books of account
with respect to all sums of money received and expended by it and the
matter in respect of which the receipt and expenditure take place and the
assets and liabilities of the Council.
(2) The accounts of the Council shall be examined and audited
each year by the Director of Audit and the Council shall furnish to the
Minister a copy of the statement of accounts and the report of the
auditor.

Power to make
Regulations.

10\. The Council with the approval of the Minister may make
Regulations with respect to the organisation and administration of the
affairs of the Council, its property and income.
