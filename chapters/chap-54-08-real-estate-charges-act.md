3\. (1) When any person after 31st March 1888 dies seised of or
entitled to any estate or interest in any land or other hereditaments which
at the time of his death is charged with the payment of any sum of money
by way of mortgage, and that person has not, by his will or deed or other
document, signified any other or contrary intention, the heir or devisee
to whom the lands or hereditaments descends or are devised shall not be
entitled to have the mortgage debt discharged or satisfied out of the
personal estate or any other real estate of that person, but the land or
hereditaments so charged shall, as between the different persons claiming through or under the deceased person, be primarily liable to the
payment of all mortgage debts with which the same is charged, every
part thereof according to its value bearing a proportionate part of the
mortgage debts charged on the whole.
(2) Nothing contained in this section shall affect or diminish
any right of the mortgagee of the lands or hereditaments to obtain full
payment or satisfaction of his mortgage debt either out of the personal
estate of the person so dying as aforesaid or otherwise; and nothing
contained in this section shall affect the right of any person claiming
under or by virtue of any will, deed or document already made or to be
made before 1st April 1888.
