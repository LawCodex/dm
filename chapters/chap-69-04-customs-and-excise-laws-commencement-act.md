AN ACT to provide for the immediate collection of additional
customs and excise duties or other additional taxation which
provision is made in any Bill on such Bill being read the first
time in the House of Assembly.

1\. This Act may be cited as the CUSTOMS AND EXCISE LAWS
(COMMENCEMENT) ACT.
2\. In this Act "customs duty" means duty payable in respect of goods, articles or
animals imported into or exported from the State;
"excise duty" means duty payable on spirits made within the State.
