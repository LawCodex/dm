AN ACT to facilitate the investment of trust and other funds in the
United Kingdom in Government securities.

1\. This Act may be cited as the INVESTMENT IN DOMINICA
GOVERNMENT SECURITIES ACT.
2\. In this Act "Crown Agents" means the person or persons forthe
time being acting as Crown Agents for the Overseas Governments and
Administrations or anyone of them.

4\. (1) Whenever by the final judgment, decree, rule or order of any
court of competent jurisdiction in the United Kingdom, any sum of
money is adjudged or declared to be payable by the Government of
Dominica in respect of any Dominica Government Securities that sum
shall, without further appropriation than this Act, be charged on and paid
out of the Consolidated Fund and adequate funds (as and when required)
shall be made available in the United Kingdom by the Government of
Dominica to meet any such final judgment, decree, rule or order.

(2) For the purposes of these sections "final judgment, decree,
rule or order" means in case of appeal the final judgment, decree, rule
or order of the ultimate court hearing the appeal.
