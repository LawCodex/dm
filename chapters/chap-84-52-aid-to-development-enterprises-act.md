AN ACT to encourage the establishment of development enterprises in the State and to make provision for granting relief
from customs duty on goods and raw materials to be used in
such enterprises and for purposes incidental thereto.

2\. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires "development enterprise" means any enterprise deemed by the Minister
to be conducive to economic development in the State;
"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Finance and Development.
Minister may
declare an

enterprise.

3\. (1) Where the Minister is satisfied that any enterprise is not
being conducted in the State on a commercial scale or at all, or that there
are insufficient productive facilities in the State to enable such an
enterprise to be conducted on a commercial scale, or that there is a
favourable prospect for such an enterprise to be further developed, and
where he is satisfied that it is expedient in the public interest to do so,
he may declare such an enterprise to be a development enterprise.
(2) Before declaring an enterprise a development enterprise the
Minister shall have regard to the number of enterprises of the same type
already established or about to be established and to the output or
anticipated output of such enterprises, and the opportunities for employment that the enterprise will create.
