## PART I

PRELIMINARY
1\. This Act may be cited as the CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS WELFARE ACT.

"child" means a person who is under the age of fourteen years;
"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Community Development and Social Affairs;
"Organisation"

means the organisation created under section 3;

"school" means a school established under this Act;
"Trustees" means trustees appointed under section 9;
"young person" means a person between the ages of 14 and 18 years.
