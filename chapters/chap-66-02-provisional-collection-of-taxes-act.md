### 8.

Construction.

AN ACT to make provision for the collection of taxes and the
variation of existing taxes prior to the enactment of appropriate legislation.

1\. This Act may be cited as the PROVISIONAL COLLECTION OF TAXES ACf.
2\. (1) In this Act "budgetary proposals" means any proposals made to the House of
Assembly by or on behalf of the Minister responsible for
Finance for the purpose of raising revenue to meet public
expenditure;
"existing tax" means all taxes of any kind imposed by or under any
enactment whether made or passed before or after the commencement of this Act;
"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Finance;
"Order" means an Order made under section 5;
"tax" includes all assessments, charges, duties, fees, rates, impositions
and other levies however called the proceeds of which are
payable into the Consolidated Fund as part of the revenues of
the State.
(2) In this Act, expressions referring to the imposition of a tax
shall be read as referring to the imposition of a new tax.

3\. (1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, where(a) the imposition of a tax; or
(b) an increase in the rate of an existing tax,

is contained in any budgetary proposals, then from the date specified by
the Minister in the budgetary proposals (hereinafter in this Act referred
to as the specified date) -

(i) the imposition of the tax shall be as effective as if an
enactment had been made or passed imposing the
tax and the tax shall be payable as from the specified
date;
(ii) the increase in the rate of the existing tax shall be
effective from the specified date and the existing
tax shall be payable at the increased rates as from
that date.
(2) Any tax or increase in the rate of an existing tax which
becomes payable pursuant to subsection (1) shall cease to be so payable
if the appropriate enactment for the imposition of the tax or for
increasing the rate of the existing tax is not made or passed within four
months of the date on which the budgetary proposals are made to the
House of Assembly.

rate of an existing tax shall be payable with effect from the date
specified in the Bill, or if no date is specified, from the date on which
the Bill is introduced into the House of Assembly.

(2) Any Order made under this section may contain such

6\. When the tax imposed or varied in any budgetary proposals or
Order is modified by an Act or resolution of the House of Assembly, as
the case may be, any money which has been paid by any person which
would not have been payable under the new conditions affecting the tax
or the increase in an existing tax, shall be repaid or made good and any
payment or deduction made in pursuance of budgetary proposals or an
Order shall, so far as it would not have been authorised under the new
conditions affecting the tax or the increase in an existing tax, be deemed
to be an unauthorised payment or deduction.

provided, no tax imposed under this Act, or any increase in the amount
of any tax payable thereunder pursuant to any section of this Act shall
be refunded to any person when any tax or an increase in any tax ceases
to be payable under any provision of this Act.
