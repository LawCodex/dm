1\. These Regulations may be cited as the CANEFIELD AIRPORT REGULATIONS.
2\. In these Regulations "airport" means Canefield Airport;
"airport manager" means the manager appointed by Government to
govern, supervise, control and protect the airport and includes
the assistant airport manager or any person deputising for or
acting under the authority of them;
"animal" means any cattle, horse, mule, ass, sheep, goat or swine and
includes fowls, turkeys, peacocks, ducks, geese and guinea
birds;
"Minister" means the Minister responsible for Civil Aviation;
"movement area" means that part of an aerodrome to be used for the take
off, landing, and surface movement of aircraft.
