# Dominica — LawCodex

Legislation of the Commonwealth of Dominica as plain Markdown. Part of
[LawCodex](https://github.com/LawCodex).

**Status: partial.** The Government of Dominica publishes the Revised Laws of Dominica 1990
and annual Acts as PDFs. Most of the 1990 chapters and most Acts from 1991–1999 are image-only
scans. Those were **not OCR'd** and are listed below. Everything with a text layer is included.

## What is covered

**Total: 469 texts**, out of 358 chapters and 555 annual Acts listed by the source.

### Sources

- **Laws of Dominica**, Government of the Commonwealth of Dominica web portal:
  https://dominica.gov.dm/laws-of-dominica. The portal's search lists the Revised Laws of
  Dominica 1990 (`/laws/chapters/chapNN-NN.pdf`) and Acts and Statutory Rules and Orders
  1991– (`/laws/<year>/...`). The fetcher indexes the searches "chapter", "act" and
  "constitution" and keeps chapters and Acts. Statutory Rules and Orders (S.R.O.s) are skipped.
  robots.txt sets **Crawl-delay: 30**, which was obeyed: about 950 requests, roughly 8 hours.
- **Constitution:** the dominica.gov.dm copy (`chap1-01-sch1.pdf`) is an image-only scan. The
  text comes from the copy of the **same official Revised Edition print** that WIPO Lex hosts
  with an OCR text layer (https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/9009). The
  Constitution (Amendment) Act 2014 (Act 4 of 2014) is under `acts/2014/` but is **not**
  consolidated into the constitution file.

## Caveats

- The chapters are the **1990** Revised Edition. Later amendments are separate Acts under
  `acts/<year>/` and are not consolidated.
- Many PDFs, both chapters and Acts, have an OCR text layer made by the publisher. That text is
  reproduced as is, OCR errors included (e.g. "12of1990", "ACf").
- Section headings (`### N.`) are inferred from numbering and may be missing where the OCR
  split numbers from text.

## Format

One Markdown file per law with YAML front matter (`title`, `identifier` ("Chap. NN:NN" or
"Act No. N of YYYY"), `type`, `jurisdiction`, `language`, `year`, `edition`, `status`,
`source`, `retrieved`). Text was extracted with `pdftotext` (poppler) and kept as
extracted. Running headers and page numbers were removed. PART / CHAPTER / SCHEDULE lines
become `##` headings and sections become `### N.` headings.

## Licence / copyright

Official legislative texts of the Commonwealth of Dominica, published by the Government for
public access. The portal's disclaimer notes that electronic versions are not the official
versions. This is not an official publication.

## Regenerate

```sh
pip install -r scripts/requirements.txt   # plus poppler-utils
python3 scripts/dm_fetch.py          # index + PDFs into ../_raw/dm (30 s Crawl-delay: ~8 h)
python3 scripts/dm_convert.py        # Markdown for chapters and Acts
python3 scripts/dm_constitution.py   # Constitution (WIPO Lex copy of the Revised Edition)
```
